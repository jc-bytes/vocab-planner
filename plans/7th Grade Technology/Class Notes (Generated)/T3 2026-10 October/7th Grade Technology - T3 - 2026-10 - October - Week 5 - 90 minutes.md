## Class Objective

Evaluate 3 online sources using author/date/evidence/purpose, choose 1 credible source, record image or text credit information.

## Pre-Activities

- Sort source examples into more credible and less credible. Review Week 5 practice vocabulary (part 2). credible: Trustworthy and believable citation: Information that shows where a source came from plagiarism: Using someone else's work as if it were your own

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Grade #4: Evaluate 3 online sources using author/date/evidence/purpose, choose 1 credible source, record image or text credit information, and write 2 sentences explaining why the source is usable.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the source check in Google Classroom. Share one warning sign of a weak source.
