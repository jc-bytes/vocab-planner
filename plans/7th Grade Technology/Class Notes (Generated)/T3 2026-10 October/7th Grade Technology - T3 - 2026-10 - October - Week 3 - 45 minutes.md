## Class Objective

Sort and filter a small spreadsheet data set.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 1). sort: To arrange data in order filter: To show only data that matches a rule COUNTIF: A spreadsheet function that counts items matching a condition

## While Activities

- Sort and filter a small spreadsheet data set. Highlight two patterns and write one conclusion from the filtered data.

## Post-Activities

- Save the filtered version. Explain one situation where filtering is useful.
