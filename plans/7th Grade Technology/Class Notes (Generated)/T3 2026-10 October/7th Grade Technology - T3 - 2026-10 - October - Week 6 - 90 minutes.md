## Class Objective

Complete a blog/media responsibility reflection about organization, source credit, effort, receiving feedback, and revision habits.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 6 practice vocabulary (part 2). layout: How text and images are arranged call to action: Words that ask the reader to do something credit: A note that says who made or provided something

## While Activities

- Complete Appreciation Grade #2: Complete a blog/media responsibility reflection about organization, source credit, effort, receiving feedback, and revision habits. Then create a short Google Sites blog-style post with heading, short paragraph, image, source credit, and call to action.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the draft and reflection. Write one revision to improve clarity or credibility.
