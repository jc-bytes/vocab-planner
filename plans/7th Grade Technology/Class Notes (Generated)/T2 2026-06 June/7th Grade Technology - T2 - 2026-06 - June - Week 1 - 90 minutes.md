## Class Objective

Create a vocabulary table for these 10 words: audience, purpose, poster, Canva, contrast, alignment, readability, copyright-free, Creative Commons, credit.

## Pre-Activities

- Write 5 search terms for an environmental topic, such as recycling, water conservation, clean energy, plastic pollution, or reforestation. Explain why copyright-free or Creative Commons images should be used.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Summative #1: Create a vocabulary table for these 10 words: audience, purpose, poster, Canva, contrast, alignment, readability, copyright-free, Creative Commons, credit. 
- Plan an environmental poster, record image credit, and build a first draft in Canva.

## Post-Activities

- Save the draft. Write one change needed before the poster is graded.
