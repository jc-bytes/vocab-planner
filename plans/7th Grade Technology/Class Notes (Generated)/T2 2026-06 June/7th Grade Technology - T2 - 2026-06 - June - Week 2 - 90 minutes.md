## Class Objective

Create and submit an environmental poster in Canva with a clear title, one main message, one appropriate image, readable text, intentional colors, and image credit/source evidence.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 2) typography: The style and arrangement of text consistency: Keeping choices the same across a design feedback: Helpful comments about how to improve revision: A changed version made after review

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Summative #2: Create and submit an environmental poster in Canva with a clear title, one main message, one appropriate image, readable text, intentional colors, and image credit/source evidence.
- Then recolor or adapt a provided logo to match your poster style and create one matching slide with the same topic, color palette, logo, and message.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the poster in Google Classroom. Save the slide and logo evidence.
