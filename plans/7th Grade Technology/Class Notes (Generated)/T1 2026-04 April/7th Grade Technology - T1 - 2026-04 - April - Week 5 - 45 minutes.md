## Class Objective

Test speed and time values and record how they affect robot distance.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 5 practice vocabulary (part 1). speed: How fast something moves time: How long something happens distance: How far apart two things are Predict which robot will travel farther: high speed for 1 second or low speed for 3 seconds.

## While Activities

- Practice testing speed and time values, record sample distances, and discuss how the calibration chart should be completed in the long class.

## Post-Activities

- Compare your practice results with another pair. Mark the setting that worked best for a short controlled movement.
