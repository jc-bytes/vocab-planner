## Class Objective

Create a movement calibration chart and improve a square-path route using tested speed and turn values.

## Pre-Activities

- Study a square path and predict the forward and turn commands needed. Review Week 5 practice vocabulary (part 2). direction: The way something moves or points calibration: Adjusting something so it works more accurately accuracy: How close a result is to what was expected

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Grade #4: Create a movement calibration chart with 3 speed values, 3 time values, observed distance, and one conclusion sentence about how speed and time affect movement. Then program the mBot to complete a square path, adjust turn time or speed, and retest until the route becomes more accurate.

## Post-Activities

- Explain which value you changed and why. Submit the calibration chart and a screenshot of the block sequence.
