## Class Objective

Combine movement, LED, and buzzer blocks to communicate robot states during a route.

## Pre-Activities

- Predict when the robot should show light or play sound during a short route. Review Week 7 practice vocabulary. LED: A small light that a program can control buzzer: A part that makes a sound state: The current condition of something event: Something that starts an action in a program signal: A sign or message sent to show information pattern: Something that repeats or has a clear order

## While Activities

- Combine movement, LED, and buzzer blocks in one program. Program a start signal, moving state, stop signal, and short alert sound. Test the program and debug the order of blocks.

## Post-Activities

- Present the final state sequence to another pair. Explain how lights and sound help a user understand the robot.
