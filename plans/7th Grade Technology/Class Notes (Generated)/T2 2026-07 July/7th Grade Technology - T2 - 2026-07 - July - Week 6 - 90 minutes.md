## Pre-Activities

## While Activities

- Complete Appreciation Grade #1: Complete a peer feedback checklist about respectful comments, specific suggestions, participation, and response to feedback. Then create a countdown or rhythm-timing program that uses a loop and a variable, test the timing, and adjust one value to improve the result.
