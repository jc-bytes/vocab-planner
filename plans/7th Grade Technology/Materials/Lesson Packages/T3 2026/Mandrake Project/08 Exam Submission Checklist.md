# Mandrake Obstacle-Detection System exam submission checklist

**Due and demonstrated:** December 4, 2026

Use the Sensor Systems module, this Mandrake packet, class instruction, and teacher-assigned hardware/simulator only.

- [ ] Purpose states the user, removal problem, distance input, and warning result.
- [ ] Diagram labels all eight assigned roles/parts and input-process-signal-output.
- [ ] Code/file or paper trace contains all six fixed logic steps and uses `distance > 18 cm`, with no alert at 18 cm.
- [ ] Test record contains 17, 18, and 19 cm plus three repeated 19 cm trials, with expected and actual results.
- [ ] One observed problem/limitation, one controlled change, and one same-condition retest are recorded.
- [ ] Explanation states goal, system chain, boundary rule, test evidence, result, and improvement.
- [ ] Each student submits at least three dated log entries and the six-question reflection.
- [ ] Group file: `GroupName_7A-or-7B_MandrakeSystem`; individual files begin `Lastname_Firstname_7A-or-7B_`.

The equal fallback is the labeled simulator or paper trace in `07 Make-Up and Simulation Path.md`. A forgotten personal device or unprepared login may reduce only the 9-point readiness criterion. A documented school, platform, teacher-provided account, connection, or hardware failure outside the student's control does not reduce readiness points when the fallback is completed.
