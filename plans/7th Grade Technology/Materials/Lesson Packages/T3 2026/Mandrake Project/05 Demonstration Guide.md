# Mandrake demonstration guide

## Prepare

- Open the final project file or labeled simulation on the computer.
- Place the test record and short speaking notes beside the computer.
- Confirm the demonstration can show one reading on each side of the threshold.

## Explain in 1 to 1:30 minutes

1. State the purpose and user need.
2. Name the micro:bit, ultrasonic input, threshold process, Bluetooth signal, and sound output.
3. Show one sensor reading and explain the threshold decision.
4. Demonstrate the warning or labeled simulated output.
5. State one challenge, the test result, and one realistic improvement.

## Teacher question

Be ready to explain why the chosen threshold is supported by the test record.

## Finished when

The working project or simulation, log, test record, explanation, and reflection are submitted. No phone photo or screenshot is needed.
