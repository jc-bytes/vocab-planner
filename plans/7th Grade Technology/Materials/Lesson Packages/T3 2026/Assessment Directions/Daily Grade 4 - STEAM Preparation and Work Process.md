# Daily Grade 4: STEAM Preparation and Work Process

**Date:** October 16, 2026  
**Who receives it:** every student, regardless of whether Mathematics, Arts, Science, or Technology leads the assigned project  
**Evidence:** individual [Technology STEAM work log](<../Assessment Assets/Daily Grade 4 - STEAM Individual Technology Log.md>) plus announced teacher checks  
**Not accepted:** phones, photos, screenshots, or another student's log

Continue the assigned cross-subject project. Technology does not add another project. Complete three individual entries naming your task, checkable result, and next step.

## Scoring — 40 points

- Four preparation checks—project/role, milestone, task sheet, safe workspace: 9
- Four individual role-work checks—start, assigned step, progress communication, usable handoff: 9
- Four responsible-work checks—safety, materials, focus, respect for roles: 9
- Three individual Technology log entries: 9
- Readiness/responsibility: 4

## Checklist

- [ ] I completed the four preparation checks.
- [ ] I completed the four individual role-work checks.
- [ ] I completed the four responsible-work checks.
- [ ] My three log entries name my task, result or evidence, and next step.
- [ ] I will submit the digital record or return the printed log.

## Make-up

Use the shared kit's individual make-up role or supplied record for the same assigned project. Complete an equivalent contribution and return the same log.

Submit `Lastname_Firstname_7A-or-7B_D4_STEAM_Process` or the printed sheet.

## Readiness/responsibility is 4 of 40 points

If digital work is required, bring your assigned charged computer and have your school login ready. Begin on time, use files and materials responsibly, and submit or return the required work by the announced deadline.

A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated reminders may lower only these 4 points. The academic work is scored separately and is not reduced for those readiness issues.

A documented school system, school account, network, or hardware failure does not lower the grade when you complete the equal fallback.
