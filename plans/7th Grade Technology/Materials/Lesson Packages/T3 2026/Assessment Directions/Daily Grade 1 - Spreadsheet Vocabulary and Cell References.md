# Daily Grade 1: Spreadsheet Vocabulary and Cell References

**Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Evidence:** one individual spreadsheet or completed printed template  
**Allowed:** Spreadsheet Analysis module, class notes, and the [fixed vocabulary template](<../Assessment Assets/Daily Grade 1 - Vocabulary Template.md>)  
**Not needed or accepted:** web research, phones, photos, or screenshots

## Task

Copy the fixed table into a spreadsheet. Keep the exact three headers and ten terms. For each term, write one student-friendly definition and one correct spreadsheet example. Then answer the six fixed cell-reference questions in the template. Save as `Lastname_Firstname_7A-or-7B_Daily1_SpreadsheetVocabulary` and submit the working file or printed template.

## What is scored (40 points)

- Ten required terms: 9
- Ten accurate definitions: 9
- Ten matching examples: 9
- Six fixed cell-reference answers: 9
- Filename, usable file/print fallback, checklist, and responsible work process: 4

## Checklist

- [ ] All 10 terms have a definition and matching example.
- [ ] All six cell-reference questions are answered.
- [ ] The filename is correct.
- [ ] I will submit the working file or return the printed template.

## Make-up and device failure

Complete and return the same fixed printed template.
