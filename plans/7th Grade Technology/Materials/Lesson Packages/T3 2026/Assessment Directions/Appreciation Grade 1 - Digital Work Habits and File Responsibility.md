# Appreciation Grade 1: Digital Work Habits and File Responsibility

**Date:** November 6, 2026  
**Evidence:** completed individual [fixed task sheet](<../Assessment Assets/Appreciation Grade 1 - Digital Work Habits Task Sheet.md>)  
**Allowed:** the fixed task sheet and class file-naming notes  
**Not needed or accepted:** open-web research, phones, photos, or screenshots

## Task

Use the six supplied files and rules. Place every file in its correct folder, write each corrected filename, answer the six responsibility cases, and complete the four-part verification record. Submit as `Lastname_Firstname_7A-or-7B_A1_FileResponsibility` or turn in the printed sheet.

## What is scored (40 points)

- Correct folder for all six fixed files: 9
- Correct rule-based filename for all six files: 9
- Six fixed responsibility decisions with reasons: 9
- Four verification checks—location, open/read test, final version, submission/fallback: 9
- Readiness and responsibility: 4

## Checklist

- [ ] I placed all six files in the correct folders.
- [ ] I wrote all six corrected filenames.
- [ ] I answered all six responsibility cases and gave a reason for each answer.
- [ ] I completed the four verification checks.
- [ ] I will submit the digital record or return the printed sheet.

## Make-up and fallback

Complete and return the same fixed paper sheet.

## Readiness/responsibility is 4 of 40 points

If digital work is required, bring your assigned charged computer and have your school login ready. Begin on time, use files and materials responsibly, and submit or return the required work by the announced deadline.

A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated reminders may lower only these 4 points. The academic work is scored separately and is not reduced for those readiness issues.

A documented school system, school account, network, or hardware failure does not lower the grade when you complete the equal fallback.
