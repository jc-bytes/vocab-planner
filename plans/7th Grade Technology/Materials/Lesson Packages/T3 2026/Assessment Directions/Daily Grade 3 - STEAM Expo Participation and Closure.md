# Daily Grade 3: STEAM Expo Participation and Closure

**Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Who receives it:** every student, regardless of the subject leading the assigned project  
**Evidence:** individual [Technology STEAM expo/closure sheet](<../Assessment Assets/Daily Grade 3 - STEAM Individual Expo and Closure Sheet.md>) plus announced teacher checks  
**Not accepted:** phones, photos, screenshots, or another student's reflection

Complete your assigned expo/closure role. Explain the project goal, subject connection, one process step, one result, and one improvement. Complete your individual contribution/result/improvement reflection.

## Scoring — 40 points

- Four assigned-role actions: 9
- Five project-explanation parts: 9
- Four respect/closure checks: 9
- Three-part individual Technology reflection: 9
- Readiness/responsibility: 4

## Checklist

- [ ] I completed all four assigned-role actions.
- [ ] My explanation includes the goal, subject connection, process step, result, and improvement.
- [ ] I completed the four respect and closure checks.
- [ ] My reflection names my contribution, evidence of the result, and next improvement.
- [ ] I will submit the digital record or return the printed sheet.

## Make-up

Use the supplied assigned-project record from the shared kit. Prepare the same five-part explanation, complete an equivalent closure task, and return the same reflection sheet.

Submit `Lastname_Firstname_7A-or-7B_D3_STEAM_Closure` or the printed sheet.

## Readiness/responsibility is 4 of 40 points

If digital work is required, bring your assigned charged computer and have your school login ready. Begin on time, use files and materials responsibly, and submit or return the required work by the announced deadline.

A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated reminders may lower only these 4 points. The academic work is scored separately and is not reduced for those readiness issues.

A documented school system, school account, network, or hardware failure does not lower the grade when you complete the equal fallback.
