# Appreciation Grade 2: Mandrake Project Process and Peer Feedback

**Date:** November 27, 2026  
**Evidence:** completed individual [process and peer-feedback sheet](<../Assessment Assets/Appreciation Grade 2 - Mandrake Process and Peer Feedback Sheet.md>)  
**Allowed:** the fixed Mandrake project packet, current project/simulation, and test record  
**Not needed or accepted:** open-web research, phones, photos, or screenshots

## Task

Confirm the four fixed milestone checks, complete three dated process entries, inspect a classmate's project with the four fixed peer-review prompts, then record one change and a same-condition retest. Submit as `Lastname_Firstname_7A-or-7B_A2_MandrakeProcess` or turn in the printed sheet.

## What is scored (40 points)

- Four milestone checks completed before review: 9
- Three dated process entries with action, result, and next step: 9
- Four evidence-based peer-review checks: 9
- Before issue, selected change, reason, and same-condition retest: 9
- Readiness and responsibility: 4

## Checklist

- [ ] I confirmed all four milestone checks.
- [ ] I completed three dated process entries.
- [ ] I completed the four fixed peer-review checks.
- [ ] I recorded one revision and a same-condition retest.
- [ ] I will submit the digital record or return the printed sheet.

## Make-up and fallback

Use the same sheet with the teacher-provided sample system record as the peer project. Complete the same revision and simulated retest, then return the sheet.

## Readiness/responsibility is 4 of 40 points

If digital work is required, bring your assigned charged computer and have your school login ready. Begin on time, use files and materials responsibly, and submit or return the required work by the announced deadline.

A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated reminders may lower only these 4 points. The academic work is scored separately and is not reduced for those readiness issues.

A documented school system, school account, network, or hardware failure does not lower the grade when you complete the equal fallback.
