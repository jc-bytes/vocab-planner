# Daily Grade 4: Source Credibility and Image Credit

**Date:** October 30, 2026  
**Evidence:** one completed individual response sheet  
**Allowed:** Source Credibility `#credibility-learn`, `#credibility-practice`, `#credit-learn`, `#credit-practice`; [fixed source cards and response sheet](<../Assessment Assets/Daily Grade 4 - Fixed Source Cards and Response Sheet.md>)  
**Not permitted or needed:** open-web searching, replacement sources, phones, photos, or screenshots

## Task

Complete all 12 source-check cells, choose the best source, give two card-specific reasons, write the four-part fixed media credit, and identify the opinion and commercial cards. Save as `Lastname_Firstname_7A-or-7B_Daily4_SourceCheck` or submit the printed sheet.

## What is scored (40 points)

- Twelve author/date/evidence/purpose checks: 9
- Best-source decision plus two accurate card-specific reasons: 9
- Four-part media credit—creator, title, original source, license: 9
- Four responsible-use checks—only fixed cards used, opinion identified, commercial purpose identified, no unsupported claim: 9
- Filename, usable response/fallback, checklist, and responsible work process: 4

## Make-up and device failure

Complete the same printed cards and questions. No web access is needed. A documented school network, platform, teacher-provided account, or hardware failure outside your control does not lower the score when you follow the assigned fallback. A forgotten personal device or unprepared login may lower only the responsibility points.
