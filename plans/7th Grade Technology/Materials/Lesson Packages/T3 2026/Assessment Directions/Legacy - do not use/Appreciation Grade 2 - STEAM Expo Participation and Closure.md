# Appreciation Grade 2: STEAM Expo Participation and Closure

**Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Who receives it:** every Grade 7 Technology student, regardless of the subject leading the assigned project  
**Evidence:** individual [Technology STEAM expo and closure sheet](<../Assessment Assets/Appreciation Grade 2 - STEAM Individual Expo and Closure Sheet.md>) plus the teacher's announced observation checklist  
**Allowed:** the assigned project, shared STEAM kit, project record, and fixed sheet  
**Not accepted:** phones, photos, screenshots, or another student's reflection

## Individual task

Complete your assigned expo/closure role. Explain the project's goal, subject connection, one process step, one result, and one improvement. Complete your individual contribution/result/improvement reflection.

## Scoring — 40 points

- Four assigned-role actions: station/material ready, role performed, transition supported, handoff complete: 9
- Five project-explanation parts: goal, subject connection, process, result, improvement: 9
- Four respect/closure checks: listening, respectful speech, safety, cleanup: 9
- Individual reflection: contribution, checkable result, next improvement: 9
- Readiness/responsibility: 4

## Readiness and responsibility

Readiness/responsibility is 4 of 40 points. Bring your assigned charged computer and have your school login ready when digital work is required; begin on time; use files and materials responsibly; and submit or return the required evidence by the deadline. A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated responsibility reminders may lower only these 4 readiness points; the academic criteria score the work itself. A documented school system, teacher-provided account, network, or hardware failure does not lower the grade when you complete the equal fallback.

## Make-up and fallback

Use the same assigned-project record from the shared kit. Prepare the same five-part explanation, complete an equivalent closure task, and submit the same individual reflection. External schedule, school-system, or hardware failure carries no penalty when the equal fallback is completed.

Submit `Lastname_Firstname_7A-or-7B_STEAM_A2_Closure` or the printed sheet.
