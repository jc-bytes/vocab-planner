# Appreciation Grade 1: STEAM Preparation and Work Process

**Date:** October 16, 2026  
**Who receives it:** every Grade 7 Technology student, regardless of whether the assigned STEAM project is led by Mathematics, Arts, Science, or Technology  
**Evidence:** individual [Technology STEAM work log](<../Assessment Assets/Appreciation Grade 1 - STEAM Individual Technology Log.md>) plus the teacher's announced observation checklist  
**Allowed:** the assigned cross-subject project, shared STEAM kit, project materials, and the fixed individual log  
**Not accepted:** phones, photos, screenshots, or another student's log

## Individual task

Continue the project assigned by the responsible STEAM teacher. Technology does not assign another project. In your own log, complete three entries that identify your task, the checkable result, and the next step. The Technology teacher also checks preparation, assigned-role work, and responsible work.

## Scoring — 40 points

- Four preparation checks: project/role, day's milestone, task sheet, safe workspace: 9
- Four individual role-work checks: start, assigned step, progress communication, usable handoff: 9
- Four responsible-work checks: safety, materials, focus, respect for roles: 9
- Three individual Technology log entries with task, result/evidence, next step: 9
- Readiness/responsibility: 4

## Readiness and responsibility

Readiness/responsibility is 4 of 40 points. Bring your assigned charged computer and have your school login ready when digital work is required; begin on time; use files and materials responsibly; and submit or return the required evidence by the deadline. A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated responsibility reminders may lower only these 4 readiness points; the academic criteria score the work itself. A documented school system, teacher-provided account, network, or hardware failure does not lower the grade when you complete the equal fallback.

## Make-up and fallback

Use the shared kit's individual make-up role for the same assigned project or its supplied project record. Complete an equivalent plan/check/test/explanation contribution and the same three-part log. A documented school-system, account, or hardware failure carries no penalty when the equal fallback is completed.

Submit `Lastname_Firstname_7A-or-7B_STEAM_A1_Log` or the printed sheet.
