# Daily Grade 5: Mandrake Detection System Design Plan

**Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Evidence:** one individual digital or printed plan  
**Allowed:** Sensor Systems `#system-learn`, `#system-practice`, `#threshold-learn`, `#threshold-practice`, `#testing-learn`, `#testing-practice`; [fixed design template](<../Assessment Assets/Daily Grade 5 - Fixed Mandrake Design Template.md>)

## Task

Complete the fixed plan for `distance > 18 cm`. State the user need and testable goal; complete input-process-signal-output; use all assigned components; draw/type the six-step flow; and predict results for 17, 18, and 19 cm.

## Submit

Submit the completed fixed design template as `Lastname_Firstname_7A-or-7B_Daily5_MandrakePlan`. If the digital tool is unavailable, submit the same completed printed template with your full name and group.

## What is scored (40 points)

- User need and three-part measurable goal: 9
- Five system checks—distance input, `> 18` process, boundary result, Bluetooth signal, sound output: 9
- Six required flow steps and all assigned components: 9
- Three boundary tests with Bluetooth result, sound result, and safe/setup condition: 9
- Filename, usable plan/fallback, checklist, and responsible work process: 4
