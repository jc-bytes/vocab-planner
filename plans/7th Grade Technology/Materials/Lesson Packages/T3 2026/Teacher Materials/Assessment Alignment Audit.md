# Grade 7 T3 assessment alignment audit

**Active structure:** exactly five Daily grades, two Appreciation grades, and one 90-point Mandrake exam.

| Date | Assessment | Fixed evidence | Equal fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sep 18 | Daily 1 — Spreadsheet Vocabulary and Cell References | Ten terms, definitions/examples, six cell-reference answers | Same printed template |
| Sep 25 | Daily 2 — Spreadsheet Formulas and Column Chart | Fixed data, SUM/AVERAGE/MAX, column chart, two interpretations | Formula/results and labeled paper chart |
| Oct 2 | Daily 5 — Scratch Custom Block and Repeat Loop | Fixed `.sb3`/share plus three tests | Same block sequence and paper traces |
| Oct 16 | Daily 4 — STEAM Preparation and Work Process | Individual Technology log plus twelve announced teacher checks | Shared-kit individual make-up role/record and same log |
| Oct 23 | Daily 3 — STEAM Expo Participation and Closure | Individual Technology sheet plus announced role/closure checks | Supplied assigned-project record, same explanation/reflection |
| Nov 6 | Appreciation 1 — Digital Work Habits and File Responsibility | Six folder placements, six filenames, six decisions, verification | Same fixed paper sheet |
| Nov 27 | Appreciation 2 — Mandrake Project Process and Peer Feedback | Milestones, process log, peer checks, revision/retest | Fixed sample record and same sheet |
| Dec 4 | Exam — Mandrake Obstacle-Detection System | Fixed project/simulation, log, tests, demo, explanation, reflection | Labeled simulation or paper trace with same evidence |

Every student receives both Technology STEAM Daily grades regardless of whether Mathematics, Arts, Science, or Technology leads the assigned project. Technology assesses the student's own role, process, explanation, reflection, and responsibility; it does not add another project.

Source credibility and the Mandrake design milestone remain taught and collected as formative or exam-process evidence.

Readiness/responsibility is 4 of 40 points or 9 of 90 points. A documented school network, platform, teacher-provided account, or hardware failure does not lower the score when the equal fallback is completed. Phones, photos, and screenshots are not evidence.

`grade7-assessment-spec.json` is the source for the eight active DOCX and Classroom XLSX rubrics. Superseded rubrics, directions, and fixed assets are stored under `Legacy - do not use`.
