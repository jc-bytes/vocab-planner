## Class Objective

Run final maze tests, document program evidence, and prepare a short explanation of the solution.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 12 practice vocabulary. presentation: Sharing work or ideas with others demonstration: Showing how something works solution: A way to solve a problem challenge: A task that takes effort to complete result: What happens at the end of a task or test reflection: Thinking about what happened and what you learned Prepare the robot, maze, and program for final testing. Review how to explain sensors, motors, code, and improvements.

## While Activities

- Continue Exam Project: Run the full maze or best available maze section, document the final program, collect trial evidence, and prepare a 1-2 minute explanation of the solution and challenges.

## Post-Activities

- Practice the explanation with another group. Revise one unclear part of the presentation.
