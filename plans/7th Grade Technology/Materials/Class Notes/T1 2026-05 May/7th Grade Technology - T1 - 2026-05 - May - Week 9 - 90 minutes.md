## Class Objective

Build and test a practice maze section, record movement or sensor issues, and identify one improvement.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 10 practice vocabulary. goal: What you are trying to achieve sensor: A part that detects information from the environment sequence: Steps in the correct order teamwork: Working together with others responsibility: Taking care of your part of the work evidence: Information that shows what happened or supports an idea Review the project rubric for programming, problem solving, robot performance, understanding, punctuality, and responsibility. List the materials and code blocks needed.

## While Activities

- Build a practice maze section with available classroom materials. Test one short route with the mBot. Record one movement issue, one sensor issue, and one improvement idea. Complete any final make-up task for regular trimester grades if needed.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the route sketch and planning notes. Write one goal for the exam project build week.
