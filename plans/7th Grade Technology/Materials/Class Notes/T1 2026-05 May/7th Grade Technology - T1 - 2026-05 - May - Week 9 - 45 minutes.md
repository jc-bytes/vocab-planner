## Class Objective

Sketch a maze route with turns, obstacles, movement arrows, and possible sensor checks.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 9 practice vocabulary. maze: A path challenge with turns and obstacles route: The planned path to follow checkpoint: A place used to check progress on a route obstacle: Something that blocks the way prototype: An early version used for testing algorithm: A clear set of steps to solve a problem Examine a sample maze and identify easy, medium, and difficult sections.

## While Activities

- Sketch a maze route with start, finish, at least 2 turns, and 2 obstacles. Label the route with movement arrows and possible sensor checks. Complete any missing daily-grade or appreciation evidence from weeks 1-8 if needed.

## Post-Activities

- Share one route decision with a partner. Revise one part of the plan after feedback.
