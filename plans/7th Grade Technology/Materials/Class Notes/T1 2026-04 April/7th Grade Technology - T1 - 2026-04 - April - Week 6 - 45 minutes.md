## Class Objective

Draw a grid route with checkpoints and translate movement arrows into mBlock commands.

## Pre-Activities

- Arrange arrow cards to reach a finish point without crossing obstacles. Review Week 6 practice vocabulary (part 1). path: The way from one place to another route: The planned path to follow checkpoint: A place used to check progress on a route

## While Activities

- Draw a route map with start point, finish point, 2 checkpoints, and at least 6 movement arrows. Translate the arrows into a short mBlock command list.

## Post-Activities

- Compare the map with a partner. Correct one command that does not match the route.
