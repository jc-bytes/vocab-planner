## Class Objective

Program a route, remove an unnecessary command, and explain how the route became more efficient.

## Pre-Activities

- Compare two programs for the same route and choose the more efficient one. Review Week 6 practice vocabulary (part 2). obstacle: Something that blocks the way plan: A set of steps for what to do improve: To make something better

## While Activities

- Program a taped floor route from start to finish. Test the mBot, remove one unnecessary command, and retest. Complete Appreciation Grade #2: Complete a self-reflection about effort, organization, receiving feedback, and perseverance during testing and debugging.

## Post-Activities

- Demonstrate the improved route to another group. Write one change that made the route more efficient.
