## Class Objective

Practice revising the environmental poster in Canva.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 1) brand: The style and identity of a product or group logo: A symbol or design that represents a group or idea palette: A planned set of colors Compare two poster drafts and identify which one communicates more clearly. Use contrast, alignment, readability, image, and message in the discussion.

## While Activities

- Practice revising the environmental poster in Canva. Check the title, main message, image, readability, color choices, and image credit/source evidence before the graded submission.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining your strongest design choice. List one revision to finish in the long class.
