## Class Objective

Build, test, debug, and record an mBot movement sequence for a taped route.

## Pre-Activities

- Arrange arrow cards to plan a route from a start point to a finish point. Predict which movement blocks will be needed.

## While Activities

- Build a movement sequence in mBlock for a taped floor route. Test the robot, debug one movement, and retest. Complete Appreciation Grade #1 for robotics safety and responsibility during setup, testing, and cleanup. Record the final sequence using arrows or block names.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the final sequence. Write one debugging step that improved the route.
