# 7° Technology - June 2026

## Monthly Focus

Students begin the second trimester by studying clear digital messages, responsible image use, poster design, and basic branding. The work is practical and visual, using Canva and Google Classroom to create and submit design evidence.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 class hours

## Main Resources

- Pen
- pencil
- technology notebook
- computer
- student's email and password

## Competences

- Analyze digital messages by identifying purpose, audience, clarity, and visual effectiveness.
- Use copyright-free or Creative Commons images responsibly with source or credit evidence.
- Create clear Canva visual products using readable text, image choice, layout, color, and basic branding.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the features of effective environmental posters and explain how they communicate a message.
- Choose useful search terms, select appropriate copyright-free images, and record source evidence.
- Revise a Canva poster or related visual design using feedback, readability, alignment, contrast, and consistent style.

## Learning Outcomes

- Complete a poster and media design vocabulary table with definitions, illustrations, and example sentences.
- Submit an environmental poster with title, message, appropriate image, readable text, and image credit or source evidence.
- Create or adapt a matching visual design using consistent colors, logo, typography, and message.

## Evaluation

- Daily Grade #1: poster/media design vocabulary table.
- Daily Grade #2: environmental poster.
- Appreciation Grade #1: feedback and collaboration checklist.
