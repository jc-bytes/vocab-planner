# 3rd Grade Technology - September 2026

## Monthly Focus

September begins the third trimester with Canva exploration, templates, tool labels, and a short display connection to the Trimester 2 Scratch Sound STEM Mini-Project. Students learn that design choices should match a message and help explain a project idea.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 3

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #1 in Week 2: STEM project display/template check.
- All other September T3 activities are formative Canva exploration, planning, feedback, and revision.
