# 3rd Grade Technology - October 2026

## Monthly Focus

October completes the Canva mini-product, then introduces guided AI awareness and simple sprite movement events/actions. AI work is teacher-guided and does not require independent student accounts.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #2 in Week 4: Canva mini-project.
- Summative Activity #3 in Week 6: AI awareness check.
- All other October activities are formative design, AI discussion, and Scratch movement practice.
