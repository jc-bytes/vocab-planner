# 3rd Grade Technology - December 2026

## Monthly Focus

December is for evidence improvement, make-up work, quiet review, and trimester closure. No new formal grade is added.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes

- Month plan weeks: 2

## Evaluation

- No new formal grade.
