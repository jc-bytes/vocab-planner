# 3rd Grade Technology - November 2026

## Monthly Focus

November develops events/actions through maze movement, drawing lines, adding features, and debugging. Students submit one movement/path check and one short debugging challenge evidence item.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #4 in Week 8: sprite movement and maze direction check.
- Summative Activity #5 in Week 11: debugging and movement challenge evidence.
- All other November activities are formative practice, testing, feedback, and revision.
