# 3rd Grade Technology - August 2026

## Monthly Focus

August focuses on Scratch sequences, sound commands, design choices, and a small Scratch Sound STEM Mini-Project. The work stays short and evidence-based: students design, test, and explain one sound/action idea without turning it into a large final project.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #4 in Week 9: Scratch sequence/sound command check.
- Summative Activity #5 in Week 11: Scratch Sound STEM Mini-Project evidence.
- All other August activities are formative practice, revision, and review.
