# 3rd Grade Technology - June 2026

## Monthly Focus

June introduces digital devices, inputs, outputs, simple processes, and connected computers. Students use pictures, sorting cards, simple drawings, and short explanations to understand how devices work and share information.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #1 in Week 2: input/output device sorting check.
- Summative Activity #2 in Week 4: connected-computers/network diagram.
- All other June activities are formative practice, drawing, sorting, and feedback.
