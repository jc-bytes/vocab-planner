# 3rd Grade Technology - July 2026

## Monthly Focus

July develops Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Scratch basics. Students practice digital organization first, then explore sprites, blocks, and simple commands with paper fallbacks.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #3 in Week 7: Google tools mini-workflow.
- All other July activities are formative practice, guided tool use, and feedback.

## Monthly Plan

| Week | Class | Topic | Pre-Activities | While Activities | Post-Activities |
