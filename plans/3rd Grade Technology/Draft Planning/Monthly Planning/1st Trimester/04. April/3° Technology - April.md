# 3rd Grade Technology - April 2026

## Monthly Focus

April moves students from vocabulary into Makerzoid kit organization, safe build planning, intelligent car assembly evidence, and a guided app/block tutorial. The month values safe process evidence more than perfect robot performance.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 5

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #3 in Week 6: kit inventory and safe build plan.
- Summative Activity #4 in Week 8: intelligent car basic structure evidence.
- All other April activities are formative practice, kit exploration, guided build work, app practice, and feedback.
