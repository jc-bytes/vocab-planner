# 3rd Grade Technology - March 2026

## Monthly Focus

March introduces robotics routines, safe teamwork, basic robot vocabulary, common robot parts, and simple command sequences. Students use pictures, drawings, labels, and short notebook evidence before working deeply with the Makerzoid kit.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #1 in Week 2: robot vocabulary/image matching.
- Summative Activity #2 in Week 4: robot parts and function check.
- All other March activities are formative practice, notebook work, guided discussion, and feedback.
