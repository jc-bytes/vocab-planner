# 3rd Grade Technology - May 2026

## Monthly Focus

May closes the robotics trimester with sensor detection, vocabulary/programming review, simple algorithm practice, optional Pivoting Fan enrichment, and evidence make-up. No new build project becomes a formal final grade.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #5 in Week 11: robotics vocabulary and programming review.
- Week 13 is protected for make-up, review, and evidence closure.
- All other May activities are formative observation, guided testing, algorithm practice, enrichment, and feedback.
