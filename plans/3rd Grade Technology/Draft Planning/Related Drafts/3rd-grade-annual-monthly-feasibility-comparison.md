## Difficulty Judgment

Overall, the year is feasible if the drafts simplify the annual language.

Keep:
- Robot pictures, part labels, vocabulary matching, kit organization, safe handling, command cards, simple path maps.
- Input/output sorting, simple network diagrams, Google Docs/Sheets/Drive tasks, Scratch blocks, sound sequences.
- Canva templates, AI-or-not-AI sorting, guided AI idea use, sprite movement, debugging with paper fallbacks.

Simplify or avoid as formal requirements:
- Advanced robot components beyond simple part names and functions.
- Bluetooth/Wi-Fi troubleshooting as a formal grade.
- Loops/conditions as independent Grade 3 programming theory.
- Full robot builds as final projects.
- Complex animation, branching databases, desktop publishing, and maze projects as large products.

## Recommendation

Create three 13-week coverage maps, then create March-December monthly Markdown drafts before editing any official DOCX files.

The coverage maps should:
- Lock exactly 5 formal grades per trimester.
- Reuse existing local summative resources where they fit.
- Keep every digital or robotics activity supported by a paper/picture-card fallback.
- Protect each trimester's final week for evidence check, make-up, review, and quiet closure.
