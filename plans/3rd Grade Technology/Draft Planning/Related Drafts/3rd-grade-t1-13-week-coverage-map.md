## Grade 3 Difficulty Guardrails

- Use pictures, cards, drawings, labels, and short sentences before hands-on build.
- Keep vocabulary small and repeated.
- Use command cards and everyday routines before app programming.
- Treat Bluetooth/app connection issues as teacher-managed, not gradeable.
- Do not make a full robot build into a final project.
