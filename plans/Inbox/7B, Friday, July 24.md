
We began by reviewing how to use Scratch, including:

- How to navigate the application
- The different sections and coding categories
- Where to find the necessary blocks and tools

Next, we discussed the summative activity and reviewed the two programs students could create. We also reviewed this week’s vocabulary words and explained how students would work in pairs.

Before beginning the activity in the computer lab, we:

- Reviewed the rubric and activity requirements
- Assigned seats for the class
- Reviewed the computer lab rules
- Discussed constructive criticism and how to provide feedback politely and helpfully

Students then worked in pairs. Each partner selected a different program. For example, if one student chose the Countdown program, the other selected the Rhythm Timer program.

After completing their programs, students prepared to review each other’s code by asking questions and providing constructive feedback. Although we did not have enough time to complete the peer-review portion, everyone was able to finish and save their code.

Students submitted their programs through Google Classroom. We will continue the peer-review activity next Friday.
