## Quiz And Presentation Balance

| Trimester | Quiz / Quiz-Style Check | Why It Fits | Presentation / Demo | Why It Fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Week 4: SVG and Vector Concepts Check | Students have already practiced vector vocabulary, Inkscape tools, paths, nodes, grouping, and SVG basics. | Week 12: Google Sites Portfolio Presentation | Students present a real portfolio product with original graphics, required video, design choices, and improvement explanation. |
| 2 | Week 4: Arduino Digital I/O and Safety Check | Students have already practiced safety, board parts, LED output, button input, HIGH/LOW, and input-process-output. | Week 12: Arduino Prototype Demonstration | Students demonstrate a physical computing prototype and explain input, output, code decision, and testing/improvement. |
| 3 | Week 7: Python Selection/Loop Debugging Check | Students have already practiced input/output, variables, arithmetic, selection, random branches, loops, and debugging. | Week 12: Scratch + Micro:bit Game Presentation | Students demonstrate a sensor-controlled game and explain controls, game logic, challenge, and solution. |

Notes:
- The added quiz-style checks do not change the required grade count unless you decide to grade them.
- The third-trimester quiz-style check is embedded in Daily Grade #4, so it counts as one of the 5 daily grades.
- The exam project presentations/demonstrations count as the trimester presentation because each requires student explanation of a product.
