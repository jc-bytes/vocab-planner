# 8° Technology - May 2026

## Monthly Focus

Students complete the first trimester by turning their vector graphics, environmental video, campaign research, and web-design practice into a Google Sites environmental campaign portfolio. Weeks 10 and 11 are dedicated to project building and revision, Week 12 is presentation week, and Week 13 is exam/admin buffer.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 class hours

## Main Resources

- Pen
- pencil
- technology notebook
- student's email and password
- Inkscape
- vector graphics files
- Google Sites access
- Google Classroom
- text editor and browser
- environmental video file
- project rubric
- checklist or worksheet
- project planning sheet
- projector

## Competences

- Build and organize a multi-page Google Sites portfolio with clear navigation, campaign purpose, and required evidence.
- Integrate original vector graphics, environmental video, campaign media, research, and source credits responsibly.
- Test, present, and reflect on navigation, readability, accessibility, media choices, design decisions, and learning.

## Learning Objectives

- Prepare and check final evidence from vector graphics, research, HTML/CSS practice, and campaign planning.
- Build Google Sites pages with homepage, campaign page, media/evidence page, reflection, navigation, and credits.
- Present the finished portfolio and explain how graphics, layout, content, media, and sources support the audience.

## Learning Outcomes

- Submit a portfolio planning map, evidence checklist, and Google Sites setup evidence.
- Build and revise the exam portfolio during Weeks 10-11 using testing, feedback, accessibility, and credit checks.
- Present the final portfolio and submit reflection/archive evidence during Weeks 12-13 as scheduled.

## Evaluation

- Daily Grade #5 in Week 9: portfolio planning map and evidence checklist.
- Exam Project begins in Week 10 and continues in Week 11.
- Exam Project Presentation happens in Week 12.
- Week 13 is reserved for exams, make-up work, reflection, and administration.
