# 8° Technology - June 2026

## Monthly Focus

Students begin Arduino physical computing with the Freenove Projects Kit. They learn lab safety, board parts, breadboard basics, digital output, digital input, and serial communication through small controlled builds.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 class hours

## Main Resources

- Pen
- pencil
- technology notebook
- computer
- student's email and password

## Competences

- Identify Freenove Arduino board parts and basic electronic components used in simple circuits.
- Build safe circuits using LEDs, resistors, buttons, breadboard connections, and classroom safety routines.
- Upload, test, and improve simple Arduino sketches by observing circuit behavior and troubleshooting problems.

## Learning Objectives

- Follow safety rules when preparing, handling, connecting, testing, and organizing electronics materials.
- Explain the role of pins, power, ground, resistors, LEDs, buttons, breadboards, and the Serial Monitor.
- Modify simple code values, test circuit behavior, and use troubleshooting steps when a circuit or program does not work.

## Learning Outcomes

- Complete an Arduino basics vocabulary table with key circuit, component, and programming terms.
- Build and demonstrate a button-controlled LED circuit with a labeled photo or diagram.
- Complete a safe material use and teamwork checklist while documenting a small LED/button interaction.

## Evaluation

- Daily Grade #1 in Week 1: Arduino basics vocabulary table.
- Daily Grade #2 in Week 3: button-controlled LED build.
- Appreciation Grade #1 in Week 4: safe material use and teamwork checklist.
