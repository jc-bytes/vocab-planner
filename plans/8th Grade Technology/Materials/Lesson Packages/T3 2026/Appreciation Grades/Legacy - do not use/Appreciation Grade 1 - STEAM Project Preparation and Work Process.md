# Appreciation Grade 1: STEAM Project Preparation and Work Process

Grade 8 Technology · 40 points · 8A/8B Thursday, October 15

Continue the one group project assigned by the responsible STEAM teacher. Technology does not assign a second product. Every student receives an individual Technology grade regardless of whether the project topic belongs to Technology, Mathematics, Arts, or Science.

Use this submission checklist: assigned subject/project and role; one dated contribution with action, result, and next step; one test row with condition, expected result, actual result, and improvement; organized files/materials; and a 30–60 second explanation of your contribution. Submit or show every item. The teacher uses the shared group log plus individual observation. No phone, photo, or screenshot evidence.

Readiness/responsibility is 4 of 40 points. Bring your assigned charged computer and have your school login ready when digital work is required; begin on time; use files and materials responsibly; and submit or return the required evidence by the deadline. A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated responsibility reminders may lower only these 4 readiness points; the academic criteria score the work itself. A documented school system, teacher-provided account, network, or hardware failure does not lower the grade when you complete the equal shared-kit individual fallback.
