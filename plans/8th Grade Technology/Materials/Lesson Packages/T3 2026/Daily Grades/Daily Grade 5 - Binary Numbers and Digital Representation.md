## Fixed questions

1. Match the six terms: representation, bit, binary, decimal, place value, conversion.
2. Convert decimal `2`, `6`, and `10` to four-bit binary.
3. Convert binary `0100`, `1011`, and `1111` to decimal.
4. Explain why a bit pattern needs an agreed representation rule.
5. Explain why leading zeros do not change the value of `0011`.

Show the 8, 4, 2, 1 place values for all six conversions.
