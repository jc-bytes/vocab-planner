## Class Objective

Write a simple campaign page using basic HTML structure, tags, text, list, and link.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 7 practice vocabulary (part 1). HTML: Markup used to structure a web page head: The part of HTML with page information body: The part of HTML shown on the page heading: A title for a page or section paragraph: A group of sentences about one idea Label the parts of a simple HTML document: html, head, body, heading, paragraph, list, and link. Predict what a short HTML snippet will show.

## While Activities

- Write a simple campaign page with a heading, two paragraphs, one list, and one hyperlink. Use correct opening and closing tags.

## Post-Activities

- View your page in a browser. Record one tag issue to review before the next web task.
