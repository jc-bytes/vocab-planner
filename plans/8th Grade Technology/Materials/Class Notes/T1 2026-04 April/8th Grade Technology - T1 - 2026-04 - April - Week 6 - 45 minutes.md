## Class Objective

Test an approved AI demo and record data, prediction, and one limitation or ethical question.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 6 practice vocabulary (part 1). AI: Software that can do tasks that seem intelligent machine learning: A type of AI that improves using data examples data: Facts or values that can be used or studied training: Using examples to teach a model Sort examples into AI, non-AI software, and unsure. Define data, training, testing, and prediction in your notebook.

## While Activities

- Test Google Teachable Machine or an approved AI demo. Record what data was used, what the model predicted, and one mistake or limitation.

## Post-Activities

- Write one ethical question about AI use in school or society.
