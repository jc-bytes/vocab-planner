## Class Objective

Complete source and credit notes for campaign resources and explain why responsible remixing matters.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 6 practice vocabulary (part 2). testing: Trying something to see if it works prediction: A guess or result made by a model model: A simple version or representation of something limitation: Something that makes results less complete or certain Match source code, open source, remix, credit, and license with definitions. Open an approved Scratch or web example that can be inspected.

## While Activities

- Explore how viewing code helps people learn. Create a short source/credit check for two images or resources you might use in your campaign site, including title, creator/source, link, and permission note.

## Post-Activities

- Submit your source/credit notes. Explain why credit matters when using online resources.
