## Class Objective

Label computing system parts and explain how hardware, software, input, output, and processor work together.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 5 practice vocabulary (part 1). hardware: The physical parts of a computer or device software: Programs that tell hardware what to do input: Information that goes into a system or program output: What a system or program produces processor: The part that carries out instructions Match hardware, software, input, output, processor, memory, and storage with examples. Identify these parts in a laptop, phone, or school computer: keyboard or touchscreen as input, screen or speaker as output, processor/memory/storage inside the device, and operating system/software on the screen.

## While Activities

- Practice labeling a computing systems diagram and draft one sentence explaining how the parts work together to run a website before the graded diagram check.

## Post-Activities

- Compare your practice diagram with a partner. Note one label to review before submitting corrections.
