## Class Objective

Create a Google Sites portfolio map and video outline with required pages, evidence, and source needs.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 8 practice vocabulary (part 2). credit: A note that says where information or media came from navigation: How people move between pages page: One screen or section in a website storyboard: A plan that shows scenes in order Review examples of strong page titles, headings, navigation labels, calls to action, and short campaign video scenes. Choose the pages your Google Sites portfolio will need.

## While Activities

- Complete Appreciation Grade #2: Complete a self-reflection about effort, organization, digital responsibility, receiving feedback, and improving work during the web and research unit. Create a Google Sites portfolio map with Home, Campaign, Vector Graphics, Research/Credits, and Reflection pages. Create a 5-shot storyboard or outline for the required environmental video with topic, message, visuals, narration/text, and source needs.

## Post-Activities

- Submit your site map and video outline. Write one question you still have before the final project begins.
