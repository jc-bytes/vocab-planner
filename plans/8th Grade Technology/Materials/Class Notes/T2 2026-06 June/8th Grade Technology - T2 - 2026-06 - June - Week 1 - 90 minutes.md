## Class Objective

Create a vocabulary table for these 10 words: Arduino, Freenove, microcontroller, circuit, breadboard, pin, power, ground, resistor, LED.

## Pre-Activities

- Review safe handling rules with your team members.
- Review LED polarity, resistor purpose, and safe connection order.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Summative #1: Create a vocabulary table for these 10 words: Arduino, Freenove, microcontroller, circuit, breadboard, pin, power, ground, resistor, LED.
- Build the LED Blink circuit and change delay values. Submit Blink circuit code.
