## Class Objective

Practice building and tracing a button-controlled LED circuit and draft the input-process-output explanation.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 1). state: The current condition of something serial: A way for a device and computer to send messages send: To move information to another place receive: To get information from another place
- Sort examples into input devices and output devices. Review HIGH/LOW behavior from the previous class.

## While Activities

- Practice building and tracing a button-controlled LED circuit and draft the input-process-output explanation.
