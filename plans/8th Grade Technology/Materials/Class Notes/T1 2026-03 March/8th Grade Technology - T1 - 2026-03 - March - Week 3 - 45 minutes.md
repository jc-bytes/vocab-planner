## Class Objective

Plan a campaign icon with audience, message, colors, shapes, and vector techniques.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 1). campaign: A planned message that asks people to care or act audience: The people who will see or use something message: Information sent or shown icon: A small picture that represents an idea Choose an environmental campaign topic such as water conservation, recycling, clean energy, or protecting local nature. List the audience and the action you want viewers to take.

## While Activities

- Create a planning sketch for one campaign icon or logo. Include the message, colors, shapes, and at least 5 vector techniques you plan to use.

## Post-Activities

- Share your sketch with a partner. Record one suggestion you received.
