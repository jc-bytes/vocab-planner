## Class Objective

Complete vector vocabulary and create an environmental badge using shapes, colors, and object order.

## Pre-Activities

- Preview the 10 Trimester 1 vocabulary words. Label the fill, stroke, and z-order in a sample vector image.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Grade #1: Create a vocabulary table for these 10 words: vector graphic, bitmap, fill, stroke, z-order, shape, layer, object, path, node. Then create a simple environmental badge using shapes, colors, and z-order.

## Post-Activities

- Export or save your badge draft. Record two changes you made and why they improved the design.
