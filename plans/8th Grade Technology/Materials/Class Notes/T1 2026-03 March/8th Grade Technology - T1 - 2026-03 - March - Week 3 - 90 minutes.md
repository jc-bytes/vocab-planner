## Class Objective

Create an original campaign icon or logo using required vector techniques and a clear message.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 2). logo: A symbol or design that represents a group or idea originality: Making work that is your own idea sketch: A rough drawing or plan Review the rubric criteria for clarity, originality, vector technique, and campaign connection. Open your planning sketch.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Grade #2: Create one original campaign icon or logo in Inkscape with at least 8 objects, clear fill and stroke choices, at least 2 grouped parts, at least 1 edited path, and a one-sentence campaign message.

## Post-Activities

- Save your design and upload the required file or screenshot. Write one improvement goal for next class.
