## Class Objective

Create or revise a campaign homepage with title, purpose statement, call to action, and vector graphic.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 10 practice vocabulary (part 1). homepage: The main page of a website title: The name of a page or project purpose: The reason something is made caption: Short text that explains an image Read the exam project goal and rubric. Identify the audience, campaign message, required pages, and required evidence.

## While Activities

- Begin Exam Project: Create or revise the homepage with a clear campaign title, short purpose statement, call to action, and one original vector graphic.

## Post-Activities

- Save and preview the homepage. Record one question for the next work session.
