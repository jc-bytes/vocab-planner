## Class Objective

Check required portfolio evidence and identify missing pieces before building the final site.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 9 practice vocabulary (part 1). portfolio: A collection of work that shows learning evidence: Information that shows what happened or supports an idea campaign: A planned message that asks people to care or act research: Looking for information to answer a question Review the required evidence: vector graphic/icon, required environmental video, campaign message, research/source credits, page plan, navigation plan, and reflection. Check which items are already complete.

## While Activities

- Practice planning the portfolio map and evidence checklist. Identify the pages, graphics, video location or storyboard status, source credits, and call to action needed before the graded planning map.

## Post-Activities

- Mark missing project pieces with a star. List one item to finish in the long class.
