## Class Objective

Create a portfolio planning map and build the Google Sites shell with required pages.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 9 practice vocabulary (part 2). source credit: A note showing where information or media came from page plan: A plan for pages and content call to action: Words that ask the reader to do something Open Google Sites and review the project rubric. Choose a site title and page names that match your campaign purpose.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Grade #5: Create a portfolio planning map with at least 5 pages, required content for each page, 3 original vector graphics to include, required environmental video location or storyboard status, 2 source credits, and one campaign call to action. Then create the site shell with Home, Campaign, Vector Graphics, Research/Credits, and Reflection pages.

## Post-Activities

- Submit your planning map. Preview your site and write one navigation or video-access issue you need to fix.
