## Class Objective

Build and demonstrate a servo and buzzer system controlled by a button, potentiometer, or sensor.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 7 practice vocabulary (part 2). servo: A motor that moves to an angle angle: A measurement of a turn signal wire: A wire that carries a control signal sweep: A smooth movement from one side to another
- Label servo, angle, signal wire, power, ground, and sweep on the diagram in Google Classroom. Predict what a servo angle value does.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Summative #4: Build and demonstrate a servo and buzzer system controlled by a button, potentiometer, or sensor; submit the circuit labeled diagram, the input used, the output behavior, and one explanation sentence.

## Post-Activities

- Save your diagram and code notes. Identify one improvement for smoother control.
