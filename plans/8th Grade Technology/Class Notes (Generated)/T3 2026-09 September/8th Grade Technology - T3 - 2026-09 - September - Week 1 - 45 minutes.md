## Class Objective

Decompose one app idea into smaller tasks and explain its purpose for a real user need.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer: What problem could an app help solve at school or in the community, such as homework reminders, recycling reports, lunch choices, lost-and-found, club sign-ups, or study help? Preview decomposition by breaking one app idea into smaller tasks: choose purpose, identify users, plan screens, decide inputs, show outputs, and test feedback.

## While Activities

- Break one app idea into at least 5 smaller tasks without completing the graded vocabulary table in this class.

## Post-Activities

- Share your app idea with a partner. Record one question a user might ask.
