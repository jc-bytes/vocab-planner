## Class Objective

Create improved search queries for a campaign topic and choose useful sources for the portfolio.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 8 practice vocabulary (part 1). search engine: A tool for finding information online keyword: An important search word query: Words typed into a search tool source: Where information or media comes from Sort search terms into broad, specific, and too vague. Identify keywords from your environmental campaign topic.

## While Activities

- Create 5 improved search queries using AND, OR, NOT, quote search, or the minus sign. Record which query gives the most useful results and why.

## Post-Activities

- Choose two useful sources for your portfolio. Save their links for later.
