## Class Objective

Test an ultrasonic ranging sketch.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 9 practice vocabulary (part 1). ultrasonic sensor: A sensor that uses sound to measure distance distance: How far apart two things are echo: A sound or signal that bounces back trigger: Something that starts an action Match ultrasonic sensor, distance, echo, trigger, centimeter, and threshold with definitions. Predict how distance can control an output, such as LED color, buzzer sound, servo angle, or warning message.

## While Activities

- Test an ultrasonic ranging sketch. Record at least 5 distance readings and identify one reliable threshold for near/far behavior.

## Post-Activities

- Write one project idea that could use distance sensing.
