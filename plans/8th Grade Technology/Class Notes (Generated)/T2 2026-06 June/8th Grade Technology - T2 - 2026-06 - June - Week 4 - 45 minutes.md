## Class Objective

Build and demonstrate a button-controlled LED circuit that changes state when pressed.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 4 practice vocabulary (part 1). power: Electricity used to run a circuit ground: The return path for electricity in a circuit resistor: A part that limits electric current direction: The way something faces or moves
- Review the rehearsal checklist and identify one likely mistake to avoid.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Summative #2: Build and demonstrate a button-controlled LED circuit that changes state when pressed; submit a labeled photo or diagram, one input-process-output sentence, and three button/LED observations or check results.

## Post-Activities

- Submit your evidence. Record one debugging step that helped your circuit work.
