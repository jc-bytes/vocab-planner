## Class Objective

Label a Freenove/Arduino board and breadboard diagram for practice, including safety rules.

## Pre-Activities

- Inspect the kit without connecting power. Identify parts you already recognize, such as Arduino/Freenove board, breadboard, LED, resistor, jumper wire, button, USB cable, or sensor, and discuss safe handling.
- Write down the parts you recognize and share one with the class.

## While Activities

- Label a Freenove/Arduino board and breadboard diagram for practice, including safety rules.

## Post-Activities

- Organize components back into the kit. Write one material you must handle carefully and why.
