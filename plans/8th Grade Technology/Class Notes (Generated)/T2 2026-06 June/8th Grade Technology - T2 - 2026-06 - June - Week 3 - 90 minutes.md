## Class Objective

Complete a formative rehearsal before Daily Summative #2: rebuild or trace the button-controlled LED circuit, use the checklist for power, ground, resistor, LED direction.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 2). monitor: A screen or tool used to watch information baud rate: The speed used for serial messages message: Information sent or shown debug: To find and fix a problem
- Match serial, send, receive, monitor, baud rate, and message with definitions.
- Predict why engineers use data messages while testing.

## While Activities

- Complete a formative rehearsal before Daily Summative #2: rebuild or trace the button-controlled LED circuit, use the checklist for power, ground, resistor, LED direction, button wiring, pin, upload, and cable, test three button presses, and draft the labeled photo/diagram plus input-process-output sentence. Then upload a serial sketch and record three Serial Monitor observations.

## Post-Activities

- Save your rehearsal notes. List one thing to fix before the Daily Summative.
