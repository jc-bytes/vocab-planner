## Class Objective

Complete a safe material use and teamwork checklist about responsibility, communication, careful handling, organization, and perseverance during the build challenge.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 4 practice vocabulary (part 2). checklist: A list used to make sure steps are done builder: The person who builds the physical parts coder: The person who works on the code recorder: The person who writes notes or evidence
- Review lab roles: builder, checker, coder, recorder. Choose roles for the team task.

## While Activities

- Complete Appreciation Summative #1: Complete a safe material use and teamwork checklist about responsibility, communication, careful handling, organization, and perseverance during the build challenge.
- Build a small LED/button interaction and document it with a diagram.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the checklist and diagram. Clean the workstation and confirm all parts are returned.
