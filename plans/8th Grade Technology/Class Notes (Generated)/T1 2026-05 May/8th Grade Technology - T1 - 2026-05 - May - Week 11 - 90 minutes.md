## Class Objective

Complete research, credits, and reflection pages and prepare the final share link.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 11 practice vocabulary (part 2). link: A way to open a web page or file revision: A changed version made after review publish: To make a site or work available to others Review the rubric categories: campaign purpose, vector graphics, website structure, visual design, source credit, and reflection.

## While Activities

- Continue Exam Project: Complete the Research/Credits and Reflection pages. Write a short reflection explaining what you made, what vector techniques you used, what challenge you solved, and how your site supports the campaign.

## Post-Activities

- Publish or prepare the final share link as instructed. Practice a 1:30-2 minute explanation.
