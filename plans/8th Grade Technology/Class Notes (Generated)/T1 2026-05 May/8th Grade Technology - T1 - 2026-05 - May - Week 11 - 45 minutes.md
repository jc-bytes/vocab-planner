## Class Objective

Test site navigation, readability, media, links, and credits, then fix at least three issues.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 11 practice vocabulary (part 1). feedback: Helpful comments about how to improve readability: How easy text is to read alt text: Text that describes an image for accessibility Use a partner checklist to test navigation, readability, image visibility, links, and source credits.

## While Activities

- Continue Exam Project: Fix at least 3 issues found during testing or peer feedback. Add missing alt text, captions, credits, or navigation links.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the peer checklist. Write which fix helped your audience most.
