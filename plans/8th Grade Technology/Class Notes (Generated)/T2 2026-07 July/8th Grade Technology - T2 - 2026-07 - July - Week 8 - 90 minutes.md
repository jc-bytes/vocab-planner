## Class Objective

Complete the self-reflection worksheet about perseverance, organization, careful testing, response to feedback, and improvement during Arduino work.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 8 practice vocabulary (part 2). challenge: A task that takes effort to complete perseverance: Continuing to try when work is difficult testing: Trying something to see if it works improvement: A change that makes something better Review input-process-output and choose one challenge: light alert, adjustable warning sound, servo gate, or color signal.

## While Activities

- Complete Appreciation Summative #2: Complete the self-reflection worksheet about perseverance, organization, careful testing, response to feedback, and improvement during Arduino work. Build and test one of the integrated challenges using at least one input and one output.

## Post-Activities

- Submit code and demonstrate your integrated challenge. Clean and return all materials.
