## Class Objective

Test a photoresistor circuit and record at least 4 light readings.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 6 practice vocabulary (part 1). photoresistor: A sensor that changes value with light light sensor: A sensor that detects light threshold: A limit where an action changes dim: Not very bright
- Match light sensor, photoresistor, threshold, dim, bright, and condition with definitions. Predict sensor values in bright and dark places.

## While Activities

- Test a photoresistor circuit and record at least 4 light readings. Choose a threshold for dim and bright conditions.

## Post-Activities

- Explain how a light sensor could help an environmental project.
