## Class Objective

Use alignment, distribution, and grouping tools to organize repeated objects in a vector design.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 1). alignment: Lining objects up neatly distribution: Spacing objects evenly grouping: Joining objects so they move together Sort examples into aligned, distributed, grouped, and ungrouped objects. Review the previous badge file.

## While Activities

- Use alignment and distribution tools to organize repeated objects in a pattern or border. Group related objects and move them as one unit.

## Post-Activities

- Submit a screenshot of your organized design. Explain one situation where grouping saves time.
