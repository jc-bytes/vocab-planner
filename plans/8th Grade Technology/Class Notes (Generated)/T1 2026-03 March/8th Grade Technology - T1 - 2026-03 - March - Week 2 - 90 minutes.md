## Class Objective

Create and edit paths and nodes to build a simple environmental symbol.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 2). duplicate: To make a copy resize: To change size rotate: To turn an object Complete a quick word match for path, node, curve, freehand, and pen tool. Trace a simple shape on paper and mark possible nodes.

## While Activities

- Create straight and curved paths with the pen tool. Convert at least two shapes to paths and edit their nodes. Build a leaf, water drop, or recycling symbol using paths and edited nodes.

## Post-Activities

- Save your path practice. Write one sentence comparing a shape and a path.
