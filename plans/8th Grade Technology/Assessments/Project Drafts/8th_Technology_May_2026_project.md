# 8th Grade Technology - May 2026 Project Reference

## Project Name
Google Sites Environmental Campaign Vector Portfolio

## Project Summary
Students create a Google Sites portfolio that combines original vector graphics, a required environmental video, environmental campaign content, responsible source credit, and a short presentation. This reference supports the rewritten May monthly plan and should be treated as the first trimester exam project.

## Required Product
- A Google Sites portfolio with at least 5 pages: Home, Campaign, Vector Graphics, Research/Credits, and Reflection.
- At least 3 original vector graphics created or improved in Inkscape.
- An environmental campaign message, audience, and call to action.
- A required environmental campaign video created in class.
- Source credits for outside images, research, or media.
- A short reflection explaining tools used, design choices, challenge, and improvement.

## Required Demonstration
- Present the published or previewed Google Site.
- Show the original vector graphics.
- Show the required environmental video or the page where it is embedded.
- Explain one design choice and one improvement made after feedback.
- Explain how the site supports the environmental campaign.

## Suggested Rubric Needs
- Campaign purpose and audience.
- Original vector graphics and technical skill.
- Site structure and navigation.
- Visual design and readability.
- Source credit and digital responsibility.
- Presentation and reflection.
