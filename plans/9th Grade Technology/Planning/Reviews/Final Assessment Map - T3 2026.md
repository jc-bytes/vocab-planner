# Grade 9 Technology — Final Assessment Map — T3 2026

November 17–21 is Grade 9 review/project week. November 24–27 is academic exams/closure, and Grade 9 may finish early. No required Technology evidence follows November 21.

| Label | Official assessment | Date | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Module Work Habits and Evidence Organization | 9A/9B Sep 21 | Ordered attempts, correction, reconstructable report, organized evidence |
| D1 | Image and Sound Data Calculations | 9A Sep 22; 9B Sep 23 | Fixed calculations, units, working, and interpretations |
| A2 | Cybersecurity Work Process and Reflection | 9A/9B Sep 28 | Analysis process, source evidence, revision, fixed reflection |
| D2 | Cybersecurity Threats and Protections | 9A Sep 29; 9B Sep 30 | Fixed scenario responses and justifications |
| D4 | STEAM Project Preparation and Work Process | 9A Oct 13; 9B Oct 14 | Individual role, dated contribution, test row, routine, explanation; one shared group product |
| D3 | STEAM Expo Participation and Closure | 9A Oct 20; 9B Oct 21 | Individual role/explanation, question, cleanup, reflection; same shared product |
| D5 | Six-Risk Cybersecurity Analysis | 9A/9B Nov 2 | R1–R6 ratings, protections, reasons, priority paragraph |
| Exam | STEM Prototype: Build, Test, Improve, and Demonstrate | 9A Nov 17; 9B Nov 18 | Product, versioned files, log, three tests, improvement, demo, reflection |

Every student receives individual Technology Daily Grades 3 and 4 regardless of project subject. Appreciation Grades 1 and 2 use regular module and cybersecurity-process evidence. The group keeps one shared product and group log; Technology assigns no second project. Readiness/file responsibility is no more than 10%; documented school failures receive an equal fallback without penalty. No required Grade 9 evidence follows November 21, and December contains no required Grade 9 work.
