## Student Understanding Check
- Vocabulary is introduced through matching, sorting, labeling, tables, games, or scenario tasks rather than only lists.
- Difficult terms are reused later: input/output/state/debug in robotics; list/loop/data/chart/outlier in data; pixel/sample/risk/prototype in STEM.
- The clearest student products are diagrams, charts, tables, screenshots, logs, checklists, and short explanations.
- The main risk is not comprehension of instructions; it is login/setup friction with Arduino IDE, Tinkercad, audio tools, and Google Classroom.

## Calendar Risk Check
- Daily and appreciation grades are completed by week 9 where possible.
- Week 9 is a high-pressure planning week in all trimesters; students absent in week 9 will need make-up access to planning templates.
- Weeks 10-11 are protected for project work.
- Week 12 is presentation/demonstration week.
- Week 13 is preserved for exams, administration, make-up evidence, and reflection.
- The biggest calendar risk is hardware or account setup loss during project weeks; prepare materials before week 10.
