## Vocabulary
**Physical Computing:** Systems that use hardware and software to interact with the physical world.
**Input**: Information received by a system.
**Process**: Actions performed on input data to transform it.
**Output**: The action or result produced by a system.
**System**: A group of components working together to perform a task.
**Logic**: Rules that determine how a system processes input and produces output.
