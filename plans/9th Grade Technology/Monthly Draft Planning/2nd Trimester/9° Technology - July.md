# 9th Grade Technology - July 2026

## Monthly Focus
Students move from Python sequence practice into data science. They collect, clean, organize, visualize, and interpret small data sets while using Tinkercad for simple web-based 3D design connected to a data-story or prototype idea.

## Weekly Time
- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 135 minutes

## Competences

- • Plan, collect, clean, organize, and interpret data for a clear question and responsible purpose.
- • Use charts, patterns, correlations, outliers, and simple models to communicate data findings.
- • Design a small data product or prototype using evidence, privacy rules, and feedback.

## Learning Objectives

- • Draft a measurable data question with survey/measurement items, data types, collection method, and privacy rule.
- • Collect, organize, clean, and chart data while identifying patterns, outliers, and limits.
- • Create a data conclusion and prototype plan that communicates the finding clearly.

## Learning Outcomes

- • Submit a data collection plan with question, method, expected data types, and privacy rule.
- • Submit cleaned data, chart/pattern evidence, and a short conclusion with limitation.
- • Create a data product prototype or model plan supported by organized evidence.

## Evaluation
- Daily Grade #3 in Week 5: data question and collection plan.
- Appreciation Grade #1 in Week 6: pair programming/data teamwork checklist.
- Daily Grade #4 in Week 7: chart and interpretation check.
