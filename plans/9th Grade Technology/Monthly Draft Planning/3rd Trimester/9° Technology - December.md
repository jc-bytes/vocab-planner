# 9th Grade Technology - December 2026

## Monthly Focus
Students present the STEM exam project in week 12 and reserve week 13 for exams, make-up evidence, reflection, and administrative closure. No new major topics are introduced.

## Weekly Time
- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 135 minutes

## Competences

- • Present a STEM prototype with design evidence, testing results, improvement, and limitations.
- • Explain how the project meets its goal using rubric language and project evidence.
- • Complete final reflection, file organization, and exam/admin requirements.

## Learning Objectives

- • Rehearse and deliver the final STEM project demonstration with problem, design, testing, improvement, and limitation.
- • Verify that prototype evidence, logbook, reflection, screenshots/notes, and presentation files are submitted.
- • Complete final reflection or make-up evidence without adding new assessed content.

## Learning Outcomes

- • Present the STEM project and submit final project evidence in Week 12 if scheduled.
- • Complete Week 13 exam/admin, reflection, make-up, or file cleanup tasks as assigned.
- • Finish with final project files organized for review, archive, or return.

## Evaluation
- Exam Project presentation in Week 12.
- Week 13 reserved for exams/admin and make-up evidence.

| Word | Simple Definition |
| --- | --- |

| testing evidence | Proof from tests that shows what happened. |
| final reflection | A final note about learning and improvement. |
| product | Something created for a project. |
| presentation | Sharing work or ideas with others. |
