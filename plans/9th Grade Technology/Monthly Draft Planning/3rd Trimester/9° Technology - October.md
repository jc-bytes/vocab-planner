# 9th Grade Technology - October 2026

## Monthly Focus
Students study cyber security and responsible digital behavior, then connect those ideas to STEM project planning. The month emphasizes scenarios, risk analysis, teamwork habits, and a practical design plan rather than heavy theory.

## Weekly Time
- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 135 minutes

## Competences

- • Identify cybersecurity risks, social engineering tactics, malware behavior, and protective habits.
- • Analyze risks using probability, impact, data sensitivity, and safer-choice reasoning.
- • Create a STEM design plan based on a real problem, tools/platforms, prototype idea, tests, and evidence.

## Learning Objectives

- • Explain personal/public/sensitive data, social engineering, malware, and script-kiddie risks using scenarios.
- • Create a risk map and recommend protections based on probability and impact.
- • Plan a STEM prototype with roles, materials, platform/tool choice, test criteria, and expected evidence.

## Learning Outcomes

- • Complete a cybersecurity scenario quiz and digital responsibility/collaboration evidence.
- • Submit a risk map with probability, impact, safer choices, and protection recommendations.
- • Submit a STEM prototype planning artifact with problem, users, goal, tools, sketch, materials, and tests.

## Evaluation
- Appreciation Grade #1 in Week 4: collaboration and digital responsibility checklist.
- Daily Grade #3 in Week 5: cyber security scenario quiz.
- Appreciation Grade #2 in Week 6: STEM planning habits and organization self-reflection.
- Daily Grade #4 in Week 7: risk map.

### Practice Vocabulary

#### Week 3: Personal Data

| Word | Simple Definition |
| --- | --- |
| personal data | Information about a person. |
| public data | Information that is okay for many people to see. |
| sensitive data | Information that needs extra protection. |

| safer choice | An action that better protects people or data. |

#### Week 4: Social Engineering and Malware

| Word | Simple Definition |
| --- | --- |
| phishing | A trick message that tries to steal information. |
| baiting | A trick that offers something tempting to get a person to click or act. |
| pretexting | A trick where someone pretends to have a reason to ask for information. |
| urgency | Pressure to act quickly. |
| malware | Harmful software. |
| virus | Malware that spreads by attaching to files or programs. |
| worm | Malware that can spread across networks. |
| trojan | Malware that pretends to be safe software. |
| ransomware | Malware that locks files and demands payment. |
| botnet | A group of infected devices controlled together. |

| scenario | A short situation used for practice. |
