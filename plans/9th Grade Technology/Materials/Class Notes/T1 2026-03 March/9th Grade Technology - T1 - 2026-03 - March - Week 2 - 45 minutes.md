## Class Objective

Compare digital and analog signals and create an input-process-output diagram for a physical computing system.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 1). digital: Using clear separate values, like on or off analog: Using values that can change smoothly signal: A sign or message sent by a system LED: A small light that a program can control
- Compare two examples: a light switch and a volume knob.

## While Activities

- Create an input-process-output diagram for one physical computing system, such as a smart lamp, automatic door, or classroom alarm. Include 2 inputs, 1 process, 2 outputs, and a 2-sentence explanation of how the system reacts.

## Post-Activities

- Compare your diagram with a partner and correct any unclear label.
