## Class Objective

Practice creating a chart and drafting interpretation sentences before the graded chart check.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 7 practice vocabulary (part 1). chart: A visual way to show data bar chart: A chart that compares values using bars line chart: A chart that shows change or trends scatter: A chart that shows points for two values
- Match chart types with uses: bar, line, pie, scatter, table.

## While Activities

- Practice creating a chart and drafting interpretation sentences before the graded chart check. Write one sentence for pattern, comparison, outlier or limitation, and conclusion.

## Post-Activities

- Highlight the strongest practice evidence sentence.
