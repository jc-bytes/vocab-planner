## Class Objective

Create an addressable LED light pattern with multiple colors and speed settings.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 7 practice vocabulary (part 1). addressable LED: A light that can be controlled separately in a strip or group pixel: One tiny dot in a digital image or display animation: A sequence of images or changes that looks like movement pattern: Something that repeats or has a clear order speed: How fast something happens or moves
- Match these words with definitions using the vocabulary website: addressable LED, pixel, animation, pattern, speed.

## While Activities

- Create a simple addressable LED light pattern with at least 3 colors, such as green, yellow, and red, and 2 speed settings. Compare fixed and moving patterns.

## Post-Activities

- Sketch the final pattern and label each color state.
