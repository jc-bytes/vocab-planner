## Class Objective

Choose a reliable sensor threshold from sample readings and predict expected outputs.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 6 practice vocabulary (part 1). sensor: A part that detects information from the environment threshold: A limit where an action changes reading: A value measured by a sensor expected output: What you think the system should produce
- Inspect a light-sensor setup and sample reading table and predict bright and dark values.

## While Activities

- Practice reading sensor values, choosing a threshold, and predicting expected outputs. Use sample readings such as 120, 450, 650, and 900 as evidence for choosing one reliable threshold.

## Post-Activities

- Compare your practice threshold with another student and decide which one is more reliable.
