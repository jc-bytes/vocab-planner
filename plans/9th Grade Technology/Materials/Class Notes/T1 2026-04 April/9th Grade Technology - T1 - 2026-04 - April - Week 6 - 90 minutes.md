## Class Objective

Complete a threshold check and program RGB colors for ready, active, warning, and error states.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 6 practice vocabulary (part 2). RGB: Red, green, and blue colors used to make other colors channel: One part of a color or signal alert: A warning signal
- Use the simulator to test red, green, and blue color combinations.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Grade #3: Complete a quiz-style sensor threshold check with 6 readings, one justified threshold, the expected output for each reading, and a 2-sentence explanation of the rule. Then program RGB states for ready, active, warning, and error and explain why each color matches the system state.

## Post-Activities

- Explain which color is clearest for an alert and why.
