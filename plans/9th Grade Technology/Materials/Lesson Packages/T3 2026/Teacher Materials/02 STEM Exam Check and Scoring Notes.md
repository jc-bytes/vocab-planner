# STEM exam project check and scoring notes

Teacher-only. Use with the existing 90-point exam packet and rubric.

## Before the November 17/18 final demonstration

- Confirm each proposal has a specific problem, users, testable goal, feasible format, and three measurable criteria.
- Confirm the saved-file location or share-link method.
- Prepare the brief, logbook, milestone sheet, testing record, demonstration notes, reflection, and make-up path.
- Decide which physical materials, accounts, and datasets are approved.
- Use November 9 for proposal setup, November 11 for optional 9B support, and November 16 for the common build-test-rehearsal readiness check.
- Complete final scoring and evidence collection on November 17 for 9A and November 18 for 9B. Assign no new whole-class Grade 9 Technology evidence after November 21 or during December.

## Milestone conference questions

1. Show the feature or model section named in today's target.
2. Which criterion are you testing?
3. What changed between expected and actual result?
4. Which log entry proves the decision?
5. What will you do next, and why?

Do not initial a milestone based only on a student's statement. Open the saved file or inspect the product and typed record.

## Rubric evidence guide

| Criterion | Evidence to inspect |
|---|---|
| Problem and design | Approved proposal, users, goal, tools, labeled concept, materials, three criteria |
| Prototype or product | Live check of main feature or model action and final saved file/share link |
| Testing evidence | At least three trials with condition, expected result, actual result, and criterion comparison |
| Improvement and iteration | Identified weakness, focused change, retest, and before/after explanation |
| Logbook and reflection | Dated entries, version list, decisions, feedback, next actions, and specific reflection |
| Presentation and responsibility | Live demonstration, clear explanation, safe use, preparation, and timely evidence |

## Special cases

- A product may still earn strong testing and improvement evidence when the final result is imperfect. Score the documented process against the rubric.
- If a link fails because sharing was never checked, the prototype/product and documentation evidence are not yet available. Give the student the directed recovery step before final scoring when policy allows.
- For group work, score each student's explanation, log contribution, responsibility, and reflection individually. Shared product evidence does not prove equal participation.
- For an approved offline equivalent, inspect the labeled model, simulated trials, evidence-based revision, and live explanation using the six academic criteria. Score the separate 9-point readiness criterion from the announced checkpoints and the external-failure boundary.
