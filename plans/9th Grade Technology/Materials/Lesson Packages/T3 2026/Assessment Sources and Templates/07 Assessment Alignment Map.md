# Grade 9 assessment alignment map

This teacher-facing map defines the closed set of sources and assessed skills. A rubric may not introduce a requirement that is absent here and in the student directions.

| Assessment | Taught and practiced source | Fixed student material | Product checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily Grade 1 | `IMAGE-LEARN-01`, `IMAGE-PRACTICE-01`, `SOUND-LEARN-01`, `SOUND-PRACTICE-01` | `02 Image and Sound Reference.md` and the named check | Fixed image/sound calculations, working, units, interpretations |
| Daily Grade 2 | Threat and protection learn/practice sections | `03 Cybersecurity Source Page.md` and the named quiz | Six threat/protection rows, justifications, safe response |
| Daily Grade 3 | Shared STEAM expo and individual Technology closure evidence | Same shared product/log plus role, five-point explanation, question, cleanup, reflection | Individual Technology grade regardless of project subject; October 20/21 |
| Daily Grade 4 | Shared STEAM preparation and individual Technology process evidence | Shared project/log plus role, dated contribution, test row, routine, explanation | Individual Technology grade regardless of project subject; October 13/14 |
| Daily Grade 5 | Risk learn/practice/record sections | `03 Cybersecurity Source Page.md`, `04 Fixed Six-Risk Record.md` | Six fixed risk rows plus one priority paragraph |
| Appreciation 1 | Module work-habits lesson and fixed checklist | Assigned attempts, correction, reconstructable report, organized evidence | Regular Technology process grade; September 21 |
| Appreciation 2 | Cybersecurity process lesson and fixed reflection | Analysis process, source evidence, revision, reflection | Regular Technology process grade; September 28 |
| Exam | Approved ungraded proposal milestone and option card | `10–16` exam files plus option card and proposal | Product, three controlled trials, evidence-based improvement, logbook, demo, reflection |

The STEM proposal is an ungraded exam milestone. No assessment requires an internet search, personal phone, photograph, screenshot, unapproved formula, unpracticed programming structure, or a different project topic. Technology does not assign a second STEAM product.
