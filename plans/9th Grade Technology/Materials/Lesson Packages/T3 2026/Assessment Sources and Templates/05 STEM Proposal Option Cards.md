# Grade 9 STEM proposal option cards

The teacher assigns one card. Use only that card for the ungraded proposal milestone and exam project unless the teacher writes an approved change on your proposal. You do not need to search the internet.

## Option A: image-size adviser

- Problem: Students choose image settings without knowing how they affect uncompressed file size.
- Users: Grade 9 students.
- Product: A spreadsheet, small program, or interactive slide model that accepts width, height, and color depth and shows pixel count and uncompressed bits.
- Required test inputs: `10 × 8 at 8 bits`, `20 × 8 at 8 bits`, and `10 × 8 at 16 bits`.
- Required knowledge source: `MOD-DIGITAL-REPRESENTATION-01`, `IMAGE-LEARN-01` and `IMAGE-PRACTICE-01`.

## Option B: sound-size adviser

- Problem: Students choose sound settings without knowing how they affect uncompressed file size.
- Users: Grade 9 students.
- Product: A spreadsheet, small program, or interactive slide model that accepts sample rate, sample size, and duration and shows uncompressed mono sound bits.
- Required test inputs: `8,000 Hz × 8 bits × 5 seconds`, `16,000 Hz × 8 bits × 5 seconds`, and `8,000 Hz × 16 bits × 5 seconds`.
- Required knowledge source: `MOD-DIGITAL-REPRESENTATION-01`, `SOUND-LEARN-01` and `SOUND-PRACTICE-01`.

## Option C: phishing decision guide

- Problem: Students need a safe way to decide what to do with a suspicious school-login message.
- Users: Grade 9 students.
- Product: A clickable slide, flowchart, or small program that asks about the sender, link, request for credentials, and verification path, then gives a safe action.
- Required test inputs: the fake closure message, fake principal message, and one legitimate teacher reminder that contains no login link or request for private information.
- Required knowledge source: `MOD-CYBERSECURITY-RISK-01`, `THREAT-LEARN-01` and `PROTECTION-LEARN-01`.

## Option D: school risk-priority guide

- Problem: A school team needs a consistent way to choose which digital risk to address first.
- Users: A school technology team.
- Product: A spreadsheet, flowchart, or interactive slide that records a risk, assigns low/high probability and impact, recommends a protection, and explains the priority.
- Required test inputs: records `R1`, `R4`, and `R6` from `04 Fixed Six-Risk Record.md`.
- Required knowledge source: `MOD-CYBERSECURITY-RISK-01`, `RISK-LEARN-01`, `RISK-PRACTICE-01`, and `RISK-RECORD-01`.

## Requirements shared by all four options

- Use a teacher-approved tool already available at school.
- Do not collect names, passwords, recordings, or other personal data.
- Create three measurable success criteria from the required test inputs.
- Save a working version, a final version, a typed logbook, and a controlled testing record.
- A paper interaction model is the approved fallback if the assigned tool is unavailable.
