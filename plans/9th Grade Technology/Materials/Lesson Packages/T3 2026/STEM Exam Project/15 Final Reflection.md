# STEM exam project final reflection

Student: __________________________ Class: ______ Project: ___________________

Answer with specific actions and evidence from the project.

1. What part of the final product best addresses the approved problem?

2. Which test result taught you the most? Include the condition and actual result.

3. What weakness did you identify, and how did the evidence support that conclusion?

4. What improvement did you make? Compare the result before and after the change.

5. What limitation remains? Why does it matter?

6. What skill improved during this project? Cite a file, log entry, test, decision, or teacher-observed action.

7. If you had one more class, what would you test or change next? Explain why.

Finish check: Each answer names the project, not a general statement that could fit any assignment.
