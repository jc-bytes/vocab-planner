# STEM exam project make-up and offline path

Use this path after an absence, device failure, software failure, account problem, or missed November 17/18 presentation. The teacher assigns only the evidence that remains missing and arranges the contingency individually before the Grade 9 cutoff when possible. No whole-class Technology task is assigned after November 21 or during December.

## If project files still open

1. Complete the missing milestone in the logbook.
2. Provide the saved working file or teacher-approved share link.
3. Complete at least three typed test trials.
4. Explain one evidence-based improvement.
5. Demonstrate live to the teacher or in a scheduled small-group session.
6. Submit the final reflection.

## If the computer or software is unavailable

Create a checkable paper model of the same approved idea. It must include:

- labeled components or screens
- arrows showing inputs, process, and output
- a written explanation of the main feature
- three teacher-assigned test inputs or conditions
- expected and actual simulated results
- one evidence-supported change to the model
- a live explanation using the same demonstration notes

This path is not a different or easier topic. It preserves the approved problem, users, goal, criteria, testing, improvement, and reflection.

## If the live demonstration was missed

Bring the final product or paper equivalent, logbook, test record, demonstration notes, and reflection. Complete a 5–8 minute teacher conference:

1. Show the product or model.
2. Explain the problem, users, and design.
3. Walk through three trials.
4. Explain the weakness and improvement.
5. Answer one teacher question about a limitation or next test.

## Make-up checklist

- [ ] Approved problem and goal are unchanged or the teacher approved the revision.
- [ ] Product or paper equivalent is checkable.
- [ ] Three test trials are present.
- [ ] Improvement follows from evidence.
- [ ] Logbook and reflection are complete.
- [ ] Teacher conference or demonstration is complete.

The teacher scores the same seven exam-rubric criteria. The six academic criteria score the project evidence. The 9-point readiness criterion may reflect a forgotten or uncharged personal device, unprepared login, lateness, or missed responsibility checkpoint. A documented school, platform, teacher-provided account, or hardware failure outside the student's control does not reduce readiness points when this path is completed.
