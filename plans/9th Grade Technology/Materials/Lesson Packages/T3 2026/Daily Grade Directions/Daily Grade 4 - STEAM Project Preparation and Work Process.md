# Daily Grade 4: STEAM Project Preparation and Work Process

Grade 9 Technology · 40 points · 9A Tuesday, October 13 · 9B Wednesday, October 14

Continue the one group project assigned by the responsible STEAM teacher. Technology does not assign a second product. Every student receives an individual Technology Daily Grade 4 regardless of whether the project belongs to Technology, Mathematics, Arts, or Science.

Each student must show an assigned project subject and role, one dated contribution with action, result, and next step, one test row with condition, expected result, actual result, and improvement, responsible file or material handling, and a 30 to 60 second explanation. The group keeps one shared product and log. The teacher grades the student's individual evidence and observation.

Submit or show the shared-log entries and individual explanation. No phone, photograph, or screenshot evidence. Readiness is at most 4 of 40 points. A documented school failure uses the equal shared-kit individual fallback without a deduction.
## Submission and responsibility checklist

- [ ] Complete every task item named above.
- [ ] Submit or return the required file, printed page, shared-log entry, or other named evidence by the announced deadline.
- [ ] Use the equal fallback if a school-controlled technology problem prevents the normal route.

Readiness/responsibility is 4 of 40 points. Bring your assigned charged computer and have your school login ready when digital work is required. Begin on time, use files and materials responsibly, and submit or return the required evidence.

A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, an unprepared login, lateness, or repeated reminders may lower only these 4 points. The academic work is scored separately from readiness and responsibility.

A documented school system, teacher-provided account, network, or hardware failure does not lower the grade when you complete the equal fallback.
