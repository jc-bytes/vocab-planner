## Task

Complete the assigned quiz in:

`../../../../Assessments/Rubrics/IIIT/Daily/9th grade - IIIT - Week 6 - Summative 3 - Cyber Security Scenario Quiz.docx`

For the six printed scenarios, identify one threat from the source page and choose one matching protection. Then choose three different scenario numbers and, for each, name the clue, explain why the threat fits, and explain how the protection reduces the risk. Finally, choose one source-page protection as a school rule and explain why it helps many users.

Do not test attacks, open suspicious files, contact suspicious senders, or enter real credentials. Analyze only the written scenarios.
