## Class Objective

Create a graded robotics design plan and build a practice feature with an evidence-based improvement.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 9 practice vocabulary (part 2).
- design plan: A plan for building and testing a project
- test case: A planned test with expected behavior
- feedback: Helpful comments about how to improve
- Review your design plan and choose the first feature to build.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Grade #5: Create a robotics design plan with a project goal, 2 inputs or controls, 2 outputs, a state chart, a component list, one risk, and 3 test cases. Then build a practice version of one project feature, such as one LED alert, one button control, or one sensor response, and update the design plan with one evidence-based improvement.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the design plan in Google Classroom. Record what worked, what failed, and what you will build first in the exam project.
