## Class Objective

Outline a robotics design plan with goal, inputs, outputs, state chart, components, risks, and tests.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 9 practice vocabulary (part 1). robot goal: What the robot should do component list: A list of parts needed for a project state chart: A table or diagram showing states and actions
- Review examples of robot goals such as obstacle alert, light follower, smart signal, or safety alarm.

## While Activities

- Practice outlining a robotics design plan with a project goal, inputs or controls, outputs, state chart, component list, risks, and test cases before the graded design plan. Use a goal example such as safety alarm, light signal, or obstacle alert.
