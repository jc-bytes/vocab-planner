## Class Objective

Create one chart from a dataset and write 4 interpretation sentences: one pattern, one comparison, one outlier or limitation, and one conclusion.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 7 practice vocabulary (part 2). pattern: Something that repeats or has a clear order outlier: A value that is very different from the others correlation: A relationship where two things seem to change together conclusion: A final idea based on evidence
- Sort sample points into normal pattern and possible outlier.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Grade #4: Create one chart from a dataset and write 4 interpretation sentences: one pattern, one comparison, one outlier or limitation, and one conclusion. Then analyze a dataset for patterns, outliers, and possible correlation.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the chart. Write one careful conclusion that avoids exaggeration.
