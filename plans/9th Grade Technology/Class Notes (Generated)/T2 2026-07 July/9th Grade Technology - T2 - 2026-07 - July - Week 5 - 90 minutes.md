## Class Objective

Create a data collection plan with 1 question, 5 survey or measurement items, expected data type for each item, collection method, and one privacy rule.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 5 practice vocabulary (part 2). data type: The kind of data, such as number or text method: A command that performs an action on data privacy: Keeping personal information protected
- Review your approved data question and decide who or what will be measured, how many responses are needed, and what information must stay private.

## While Activities

- Complete Daily Grade #3: Create a data collection plan with 1 question, 5 survey or measurement items, expected data type for each item, collection method, and one privacy rule. Then collect or enter a small dataset and organize it into a table with clear headings, units, and categories.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the plan and table. List two data quality issues you checked.
