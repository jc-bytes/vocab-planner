## Class Objective

Complete the collaboration checklist about responsibility, respectful communication, shared work, data care, and persistence while cleaning the assigned dataset.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 6 practice vocabulary (part 2). model: A simple version or representation of something shape: A basic form such as a circle, box, or triangle label: A name or short description Open Tinkercad and identify move, rotate, scale, color, and group tools.

## While Activities

- Complete Appreciation Grade #1: Complete the collaboration checklist about responsibility, respectful communication, shared work, data care, and persistence while cleaning the assigned dataset. Record at least 3 cleaned values in a change log. Then create a simple Tinkercad model that represents one category, object, or display idea from your data topic.

## Post-Activities

- Save the model link/screenshot in Google Classroom and write how it connects to the data topic.
