## Class Objective

Practice image and sound representation questions covering pixel, resolution, color depth, sample rate, sample size, microphone, speaker, file size, quality.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 1). sample rate: How often sound is measured each second sample size: How much detail each sound sample stores microphone: A device that captures sound speaker: A device that produces sound Identify which device captures sound and which device produces sound.

## While Activities

- Practice image and sound representation questions covering pixel, resolution, color depth, sample rate, sample size, microphone, speaker, file size, quality, and compression before the graded check. Include one image example and one sound example.
