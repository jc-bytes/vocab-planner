## Class Objective

Sort device examples into input, process, and output and explain one system response.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer a quick prompt: Name one device that reacts to the world around it, such as an automatic door, motion light, thermostat, phone brightness sensor, or school alarm.

## While Activities

- Sort 8 classroom or home devices into input, process, or output parts. For each example, justify the category and explain how the parts work together as one system response.
- Submit your Canva file to Google Classroom.

## Post-Activities

- Share one sorted example and explain the system response in one sentence.
