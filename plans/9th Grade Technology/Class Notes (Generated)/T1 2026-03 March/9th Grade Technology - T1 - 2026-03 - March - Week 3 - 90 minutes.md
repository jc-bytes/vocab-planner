## Class Objective

Build and test a simple LED control circuit, identify one issue, and record fix evidence.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 2). persistence: Continuing to try when something is difficult instruction: A step that tells what to do fix: A change that solves a problem Trace the order of three program instructions before testing the circuit.

## While Activities

- Complete Appreciation Grade #1: Complete the lab safety and responsibility checklist about preparation, careful material use, listening, cleanup, and persistence during testing. Then build a simple LED control circuit, test it three times, identify the likely cause of one issue, and record the fix evidence.

## Post-Activities

- Demonstrate the LED behavior to a partner and describe the fix you made.
