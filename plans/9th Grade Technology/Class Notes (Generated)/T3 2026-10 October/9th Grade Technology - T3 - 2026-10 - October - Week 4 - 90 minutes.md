## Class Objective

Complete the collaboration and digital responsibility checklist about respectful communication, source care, privacy, participation, and safe account behavior.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 4 practice vocabulary (part 2). virus: Malware that spreads by attaching to files or programs worm: Malware that can spread across networks trojan: Malware that pretends to be safe software ransomware: Malware that locks files and demands payment botnet: A group of infected devices controlled together Match malware terms with descriptions: virus, worm, trojan, ransomware, botnet.

## While Activities

- Complete Appreciation Grade #1: Complete the collaboration and digital responsibility checklist about respectful communication, source care, privacy, participation, and safe account behavior. Then research or inspect short case summaries of cyber attacks and create a comparison chart.

## Post-Activities

- Share one protection that would help a school user.
