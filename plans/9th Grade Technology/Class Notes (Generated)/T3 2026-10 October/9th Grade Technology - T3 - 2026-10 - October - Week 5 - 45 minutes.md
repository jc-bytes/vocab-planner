## Class Objective

Practice identifying threats, choosing protections, and justifying answers before the graded cyber security scenario quiz.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 5 practice vocabulary (part 1). brute force: Trying many passwords until one works DDoS: An attack that overloads a service with too much traffic firewall: A tool that helps block unsafe network traffic update: A change made during a loop or process Review brute force, DDoS, phishing, malware, firewall, and update.

## While Activities

- Practice identifying threats, choosing protections, and justifying answers before the graded cyber security scenario quiz. Use examples such as phishing, malware, weak password, suspicious download, and DDoS.

## Post-Activities

- Correct one missed or uncertain practice scenario using notes.
