## Class Objective

Analyze 4 online-service scenarios and identify what data is collected, why it matters, and one safer choice.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 1). personal data: Information about a person public data: Information that is okay for many people to see sensitive data: Information that needs extra protection Sort examples into personal data, public data, and sensitive data.

## While Activities

- Analyze 4 online-service scenarios and identify what data is collected, why it matters, and one safer choice. Include examples such as name, school email, password, location, photo, or public comment.

## Post-Activities

- Write one personal data rule for project research.
