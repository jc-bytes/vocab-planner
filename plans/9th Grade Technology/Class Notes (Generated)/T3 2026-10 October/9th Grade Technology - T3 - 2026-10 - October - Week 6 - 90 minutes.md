## Class Objective

Complete a self-reflection about effort, organization, safe material use, perseverance, and how you responded to feedback during STEM planning.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 6 practice vocabulary (part 2). logbook: A record of project notes and tests material: An item or part used for work test: To try something and check what happens Review the project logbook sections: problem, research, design, build, test, improve, reflect.

## While Activities

- Complete Appreciation Grade #2: Complete a self-reflection about effort, organization, safe material use, perseverance, and how you responded to feedback during STEM planning. Then create a first STEM project plan with goal, roles, materials, digital tools, sketch or Tinkercad concept, and 3 planned tests.

## Post-Activities

- Update the logbook with one feedback note and one habit goal for the project build.
