## Class Objective

Draft a STEM project proposal with problem, users, goal, tools, concept, materials, test criteria, and expected evidence.

## Pre-Activities

- Review Week 8 practice vocabulary (part 1). proposal: A plan submitted for approval platform: The tool or system used to build something sketch: A rough drawing or plan Review the difference between a topic and a solvable project problem.

## While Activities

- Practice drafting a STEM project proposal with problem, users, goal, tools/platforms, sketch or Tinkercad concept, materials, 3 test criteria, and expected evidence before the graded proposal. Use a test criterion such as works safely, communicates the idea, or can be explained in one minute.
