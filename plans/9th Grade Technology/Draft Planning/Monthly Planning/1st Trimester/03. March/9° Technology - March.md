# 9th Grade Technology - March 2026

## Monthly Focus
Students review physical computing systems and rebuild confidence with input, process, output, digital signals, safe lab routines, and simple programmed outputs. The month prepares students for robotics work by connecting circuits, code, vocabulary, testing, and debugging.

## Weekly Time
- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 135 minutes

## Competences

- Analyze physical computing systems using input, process, output, signal, state, and response language.
- Use lab materials safely while collecting testing evidence from simple Arduino/Freenove output systems.
- Modify, test, and debug LED programs by recording expected behavior, actual behavior, and one justified fix.

## Learning Objectives

- Classify inputs, processes, and outputs in real devices and explain how the parts interact as a system.
- Compare digital and analog signals and predict how signal changes affect system outputs.
- Modify LED output code, test multiple delay values, and document a debugging decision with evidence.

## Learning Outcomes

- Complete a physical computing vocabulary table with technical definitions, diagrams, and example sentences.
- Create a system map that explains input, process, output, state, and response for a real device.
- Complete a lab safety/responsibility checklist and demonstrate mini-LED control with a recorded test and fix.

## Evaluation
- Daily Grade #1 in Week 1: physical computing vocabulary table.
- Appreciation Grade #1 in Week 3: lab safety and responsibility checklist.
