# 4th Grade Technology - August 2026

## Monthly Focus

August completes the robotics trimester with an inertia car STEM investigation, programming app practice, a simple STEM movement challenge, and guided sprite motion enrichment. The month keeps builds and coding short, evidence-based, and supported by paper fallbacks.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Competences

- Test an inertia car STEM investigation safely and explain the result with simple motion language.
- Navigate a block-programming environment with teacher support.
- Create or trace a simple movement sequence and debug one issue.
- Reflect on movement, sequence, and responsible tool use.

## Learning Objectives

- Record a simple inertia car test with prediction, result, and explanation.
- Use motion blocks or command cards to create a short movement sequence.
- Test and revise a movement sequence.
- Use sprite motion enrichment to reinforce movement blocks without adding a new formal grade.

## Learning Outcomes

- Complete Summative Activity #4: inertia car STEM investigation evidence.
- Complete Summative Activity #5: block-programming STEM movement challenge.
- Complete formative app tutorial practice, guided motion enrichment, review, and reflection.

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #4 in Week 9: inertia car STEM investigation evidence.
- Summative Activity #5 in Week 11: block-programming STEM movement challenge.
- All other August activities are formative practice, guided testing, enrichment, feedback, and review.
