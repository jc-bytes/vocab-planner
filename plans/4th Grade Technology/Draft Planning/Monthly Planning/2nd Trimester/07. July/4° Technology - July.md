# 4th Grade Technology - July 2026

## Monthly Focus

July moves from robot parts and command maps into block-programming interfaces, kit organization, sensor behavior, and inertia car planning. The month protects hands-on work with paper and picture-card fallbacks.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Competences

- Recognize basic programming block categories and workspace areas.
- Organize kit parts responsibly and plan a safe assembly.
- Describe simple robot sensor behavior using input and response language.
- Plan an inertia car using a sketch, parts list, and motion prediction.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify app or Scratch workspace parts such as stage, sprite/object, blocks, workspace, and run button.
- Sort kit or picture-card pieces by purpose and safety rule.
- Use simple sensor scenarios to plan a robot response.
- Explain inertia as an object wanting to keep moving unless something changes its motion.

## Learning Outcomes

- Complete Summative Activity #3: robot kit inventory and safe assembly plan.
- Complete formative block-interface labels, sensor scenarios, and inertia car planning.

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #3 in Week 6: kit inventory and safe assembly plan.
- All other July activities are formative practice, guided exploration, discussion, planning, and feedback.
