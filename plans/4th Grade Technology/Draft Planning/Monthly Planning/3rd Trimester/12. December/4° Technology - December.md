# 4th Grade Technology - December 2026

## Monthly Focus

December is for evidence improvement, review, make-up, and quiet trimester closure. No new formal grade is added.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 2

## Evaluation

- No new formal grade.
- Students complete, correct, or organize evidence for Summative Activities #1-#5 from the third trimester.
