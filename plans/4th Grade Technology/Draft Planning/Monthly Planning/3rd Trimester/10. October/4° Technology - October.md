# 4th Grade Technology - October 2026

## Monthly Focus

October connects data analysis with AI-supported story logic and clear step-by-step instructions. Students answer questions from a small data set, then practice sequencing and improving ideas in a guided, teacher-controlled way.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Competences

- Analyze a small data set using sorting, counting, and simple charts.
- Use guided AI-style examples responsibly to improve story ideas.
- Organize a story with beginning, middle, and end.
- Write clear step-by-step instructions in simple language.

## Learning Objectives

- Sort or chart a small data set and answer 3 questions from it.
- Identify character, setting, and events in a story idea.
- Improve a story sequence using teacher feedback or AI-style feedback.
- Order instructions and identify missing steps.

## Learning Outcomes

- Complete Summative Activity #2: data logging/analysis check.
- Complete Summative Activity #3: AI story sequence and improvement check.
- Complete formative AI story organizers and step-by-step instruction practice.

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #2 in Week 4: data logging and analysis check.
- Summative Activity #3 in Week 6: AI story sequence and improvement check.
- All other October activities are formative practice, feedback, revision, and notebook checks.
