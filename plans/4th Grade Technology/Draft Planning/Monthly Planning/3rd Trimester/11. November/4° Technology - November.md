# 4th Grade Technology - November 2026

## Monthly Focus

November develops simple logic and repetition in games. Students use everyday if/then choices, repeated actions, Scratch/block-card practice, and a short game modification evidence item. The month avoids turning game work into a large final build.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Competences

- Recognize simple if/then decisions in daily life and programs.
- Identify repeated actions and predict what loop blocks do.
- Modify a sprite/object action using repeated movement.
- Test, debug, and explain a small repetition-based change.

## Learning Objectives

- Complete a simple logic and repetition check with ordered steps, if/then choices, and repeated actions.
- Predict the result of a short loop or block sequence.
- Modify a sprite/object action using a loop or repeated command.
- Submit screenshot, diagram, or paper block evidence for a short game modification.

## Learning Outcomes

- Complete Summative Activity #4: step-by-step logic and repetition check.
- Complete Summative Activity #5: repetition-in-game modification evidence.
- Complete formative loop prediction, animation practice, modification practice, and debugging notes.

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #4 in Week 8: step-by-step logic and repetition check.
- Summative Activity #5 in Week 11: repetition-in-game modification evidence.
- All other November activities are formative practice, guided tool use, feedback, and revision.
