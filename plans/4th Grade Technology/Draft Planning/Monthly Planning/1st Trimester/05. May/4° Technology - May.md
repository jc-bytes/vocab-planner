# 4th Grade Technology - May 2026

## Monthly Focus

May closes the trimester with guided responsible AI awareness, Scratch/block-based repetition, loop shape evidence, make-up time, and quiet review. The month keeps programming visual and concrete. IF/ELSE, variables, constants, and robotics decision examples are removed from required first-trimester evidence.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Competences

- Recognize simple examples of AI and explain that AI tools can make mistakes.
- Identify repeated patterns and describe how repeated steps can create shapes.
- Use Scratch or block-based commands to create a simple loop shape.
- Complete missing evidence, make corrections, and reflect on first-trimester learning.

## Learning Objectives

- Sort AI and non-AI examples using guided class examples.
- Compare a human answer and an AI-style answer with teacher support.
- Build or trace repeated steps using blocks, command cards, or Scratch.
- Create one loop-shape evidence item using repeat, movement, and turn commands.
- Complete make-up work and submit missing trimester evidence.

## Learning Outcomes

- Complete Summative Activity #5: Scratch/block-based loop shape evidence.
- Complete formative AI awareness sorting, pattern practice, repeat-step planning, and reflection.
- Finish missing evidence from Summative Activities #1-#4 during the trimester closure week if needed.

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #5 in Week 12: loop shape evidence.
- Week 13 is protected for make-up, review, and evidence closure; no new formal grade is added.
- All other May activities are formative practice, guided discussion, revision, review, and make-up support.
