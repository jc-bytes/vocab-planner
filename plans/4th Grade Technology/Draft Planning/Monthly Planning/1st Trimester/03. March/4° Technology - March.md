# 4th Grade Technology - March 2026

## Monthly Focus

March introduces technology class routines, basic Internet/network vocabulary, safe sharing, website parts, and Google Drive organization. The month stays concrete and visual so students can label, draw, organize, and submit simple evidence before later digital-product work.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Competences

- Use technology class routines, account information, and notebook organization responsibly.
- Identify basic network and Internet parts using pictures, labels, and simple explanations.
- Recognize safe sharing, website parts, and controlled examples of trustworthy information.
- Organize a Google Drive folder and submit a file to the teacher using clear naming and sharing steps.

## Learning Objectives

- Identify and describe basic vocabulary: network, Internet, device, router, server, website, browser, URL, folder, file, and share.
- Draw and label a simple network diagram with connected devices, a router, and a server or website.
- Recognize safe sharing choices and basic website parts using teacher-selected examples.
- Create, name, move, and share a simple file in Google Drive.

## Learning Outcomes

- Complete Summative Activity #1: Internet/network vocabulary plus a simple labeled network diagram.
- Complete Summative Activity #2: Google Drive organization and teacher submission task.
- Complete formative notebook setup, safe-sharing practice, website-parts sketching, and guided copyright/fact-checking examples.

## Evaluation

- Summative Activity #1 in Week 2: Internet/network vocabulary and diagram.
- Summative Activity #2 in Week 4: Google Drive organization task.
- All other March activities are formative practice, discussion, notebook checks, guided examples, and feedback.
