## Planning Rules Applied

- Grade level: 4th grade.
- Band: primary education.
- Formal assessment rule: exactly 5 formal grades in the trimester.
- No exam project, final project, or project presentation is required by default.
- Annual plan remains canonical, but activities are simplified where needed for Grade 4 feasibility.

## Canonical T3 Spine

1. STEM data and information: questions, data collection, logging, and analysis
2. AI for creative writing and story logic
3. Step-by-step logic, if/then decisions, and repeated actions
4. Programming B: repetition in games
5. Game modification and simple repetition-based design evidence
6. Evidence closure and make-up

## Monthly Rewrite Guide

- September covers Weeks 1-3: STEM data questions, data collection, and logging, Summative #1.
- October covers Weeks 4-7: data analysis, AI story logic, and step-by-step instructions, Summatives #2-#3.
- November covers Weeks 8-11: if/then choices, repetition in games, animation/modification practice, Summatives #4-#5.
- December covers Weeks 12-13: evidence improvement, review, make-up, and quiet closure only.

## Grade 4 Difficulty Guardrails

- Use class-safe, non-private STEM data from inertia, movement, or teacher-provided cards.
- Use small data sets and simple charts; avoid complex graphing.
- Keep AI teacher-guided and focused on checking, improving, and sequencing ideas.
- Treat if/then as everyday choices before code.
- Keep game work as a short modification or guided build, not a final game project.
