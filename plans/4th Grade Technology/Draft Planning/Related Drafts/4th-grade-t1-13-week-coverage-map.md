## Grade 4 Difficulty Guardrails

Use these rules when rewriting the monthly lesson cells:

- Start with pictures, examples, labels, and guided practice.
- Keep vocabulary sets short, usually 4-6 words per class.
- Use student actions such as identify, label, draw, sort, match, create, test, submit, and reflect.
- Avoid open-ended research unless sites/examples are controlled by the teacher.
- Use block-based programming before text-based syntax.
- Keep presentations short and natural; do not turn products into a final project.
- Provide paper fallback for every digital summative.
