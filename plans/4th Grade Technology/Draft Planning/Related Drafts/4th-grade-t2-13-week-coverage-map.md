## Grade 4 Difficulty Guardrails

- Use real parts only when safe and ready; pictures and paper models are valid.
- Avoid object-oriented programming, sorting/searching algorithms, and advanced loops as formal expectations.
- Treat programming as visual blocks, command cards, motion steps, and simple debugging.
- Treat inertia as a simple motion idea: an object keeps moving unless something stops or changes it.
- Keep robot builds short and evidence-based; do not create a final project.
- Use STEM language consistently for the inertia investigation and movement challenge.
