## Annual and Monthly Alignment Issues Found

1. The annual plan included Python intermediate content, but Python is not available for 5th grade.
2. The annual plan included Crumble-based physical computing, but Crumble controllers are not available.
3. The annual plan included video production expectations that implied camera/mobile recording, but camera or mobile device use is not available.
4. The monthly plans did not originally distribute all three MEDUCA trimesters as 13 weeks each.
5. Trimester 1 was short one instructional week.
6. Trimester 2 was missing August 31-September 4.
7. Existing monthly plans did not consistently include exactly 5 summative activities per trimester.
8. Several activities were too broad or too theoretical for 5th grade.
9. Summative activities were not clearly separated from formative activities.
10. Some monthly activities used generic phrasing instead of clear student-facing instructions.

## Changes Made

1. Rewrote all 10 monthly Markdown plans using one consistent structure.
2. Applied the 3-hour weekly schedule: one 45-minute class and one 90-minute class.
3. Used the MEDUCA 2026 calendar exactly, with 13 weeks per trimester.
4. Added August 31-September 4 as Trimester 2 Week 13 inside the August monthly plan.
5. Removed Python from monthly planning.
6. Removed Crumble controller requirements from monthly planning.
7. Removed camera/mobile-device recording requirements from monthly planning.
8. Simplified difficult topics into short products, diagrams, checks, demos, and guided practice.
9. Set exactly 5 summative activities per trimester.
10. Placed all summatives in the 90-minute class only.
11. Kept all summatives within the first 9 weeks of each trimester.
12. Marked the final week of each trimester as exam/no-new-work time.
13. Reframed heavy projects as short products, checks, demos, or product-sharing activities.
14. Made Pre/While/Post activities student-facing and action-oriented.
15. Added 1-2 practice keywords with short definitions to every class.
16. Labeled every summative activity explicitly as `(Summative)`.

Balance note:
- Every trimester includes at least one quiz or quiz-style check.
- Every trimester includes at least one presentation, demonstration, or speaking/product-sharing activity.
- Each speaking/demo/product-sharing activity connects naturally to a product, design, robot/model, routine, or investigation.
- The 5th grade plan has no exam projects, so no exam-project presentation is counted.

## Annual Plan Updates Recommended

1. Remove `Programming with Python (intermediate)` from the 5th grade annual plan or replace it with `logic, flowcharts, sequencing, and simple Scratch/Blockly-style commands`.
2. Replace Crumble-specific physical computing language with `input-output logic, conditions, selection, debugging, and paper/Scratch/Blockly-style simulations`.
3. Replace camera/mobile-device video production with `storyboard, visual media planning, slide/poster sequence, audience, captions, and product sharing without filming`.
4. Add a clear yearly dosification map showing all 39 weeks.
5. Add the rule that each trimester has exactly 5 summative activities.
6. Add the rule that summatives happen in the 90-minute class.
7. Clarify that all summatives should happen by Week 9 when possible.
8. Clarify that Week 13 is exam week/no-new-work time.
9. Replace vague assessment labels with concrete assessment products such as diagrams, sorting checks, scenario quizzes, build plans, storyboards, and short demos.
