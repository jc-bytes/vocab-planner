# 5th Grade Feasibility and Dosification Audit

Source checked:
- Annual plan: `Annual Plans/5° Technology and Robotics - Annual Plan - 2026.md`
- Monthly drafts: `Draft Planning/Monthly Planning`
- Official MEDUCA 2026 school calendar: https://www.meduca.gob.pa/calendario-escolar-2026/

## Executive Finding

The 5th grade sequence is broadly feasible as a full-year technology plan, but it needs a correction pass before it should be treated as final.

The strongest part is Trimester 3. It has a workable 13-week distribution across databases, vector drawing, and Scratch quiz selection.

Trimester 1 and Trimester 2 both need correction. Trimester 1 has the most practical, equipment-heavy content, but the monthly plans currently show only 12 planned weeks against the annual plan's 13 working weeks. Trimester 2 also needs one more official week because the MEDUCA calendar confirms June 8 to September 4 is 13 weeks, not 12. March and April also still use date labels that match the old source calendar, not the 2026 annual plan.

## Date Consistency Check

March and April are not aligned to 2026.

Examples:
- The March plan labels March 11 as Tuesday, but March 11, 2026 is Wednesday.
- The April plan labels April 1 as Tuesday, but April 1, 2026 is Wednesday.
- The April plan carries a Holy Week/holiday placement from the old source calendar, not the 2026 calendar.

May through December are date-consistent with the 2026 Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday schedule.

## Trimester 1 Feasibility

Annual topics:
- Organization and Learning of the Robotic class
- Advanced Robotics Components
- Programming with Python (intermediate)
- Robot Communication and Coordination
- Advanced Robot Sensors and Data Processing
- Robot K96131
- Worm Robot
- Telescopic Clamp
- Propeller Aircraft
- Programming with Blockly/Scratch and Robotics

Current pacing:
- March: robotics orientation, advanced components, Python basics
- April: communication, Python if statements, sensors/data, K96131, Worm Robot, Telescopic Clamp intro
- May: Telescopic Clamp, Propeller Aircraft, Blockly/Scratch robotics, review/assessment

Feasibility rating: tight but possible after correction.

Main risks:
- Missing one week against the annual plan.
- March/April dates are not 2026.
- K96131 basic handling receives only one 1-hour block before students move into the Worm Robot.
- Worm Robot receives about 3 hours total, which is tight for build, troubleshoot, and programming.
- Telescopic Clamp receives about 5 hours, which is reasonable but still depends on kit readiness.
- Propeller Aircraft receives about 3 hours, which is tight for assembly and motion control.
- Python intermediate content appears in only a few sessions; it should be treated as introductory support for logic/flowcharts, not as a full programming unit.

Recommended Trimester 1 correction:
- Make March a 4-week plan.
- Correct March and April dates to 2026.
- Use the added March week for lab routines, robotics kit setup, robot parts, and simple sequencing.
- Keep Python lightweight and directly connected to flowcharts/conditions.
- Protect May for hands-on build, testing, review, and assessment.

## Trimester 2 Feasibility

Annual topics:
- Computing Systems and Networks
- Searching on the Web
- Video Production
- Programming A: Selection in Physical Computing

Current pacing:
- June: computing systems and web search
- July: video production
- August: physical computing

Feasibility rating: workable after adding the missing 13th week.

The current 12-week plan gives each major unit approximately 12 hours:
- 12 hours for computing systems/search
- 12 hours for video production
- 12 hours for physical computing

Main risks:
- MEDUCA confirms the trimester continues through September 4, so the monthly plan should not end Trimester 2 in August.
- August 31-September 4 needs a clear 2nd-trimester closing/buffer week.
- The extra week should not introduce a large new unit. It should protect completion, remediation, assessment, presentation, and evidence collection for physical computing.

Recommended Trimester 2 correction:
- Keep the June-July-August content distribution.
- Add August 31-September 4 as a 2nd-trimester closing week.
- Add assessment language for the three products: search challenge, video project, physical computing challenge.
- Use the added week for physical computing final testing, code challenge completion, project demonstrations, notebook evidence, and trimester reflection.

## Trimester 3 Feasibility

Annual topics:
- Flat-File Databases
- Vector Drawing
- Programming B: Selection in Quizzes

Current pacing:
- September: databases
- October: vector drawing
- November: Scratch selection quiz
- December: final quiz completion, demonstration, reflection

Feasibility rating: strong.

This trimester is coherent. The Scratch quiz receives enough time because November and December together provide about 18 hours. Database work is compressed into 9 hours, but it is still realistic if activities are guided and the database project is simple.

Recommended Trimester 3 correction:
- Keep the overall distribution.
- Add or generate the missing rubrics/study guides/summatives later.
- Make sure September's 3-week database plan includes one clear product or evidence item.
- Keep December as polish, presentation, and reflection rather than new content.

## Highest-Priority Fixes

1. Correct March and April dates to 2026.
2. Add or restore the missing Trimester 1 week so Trimester 1 has 13 weeks.
3. Add the missing Trimester 2 week for August 31-September 4.
4. Add a Monthly Coverage Map appendix back into the annual plan. It is not currently present in the annual Markdown or DOCX.
5. Build missing assessment support materials after pacing is corrected:
   - rubrics
   - study guides
   - non-rubric summatives
   - teacher keys

## Recommendation

Do not rebuild all monthly plans yet. First fix the calendar and dosification:
- March should become a 4-week plan.
- April should keep 5 weeks but use 2026 dates.
- May can stay 4 weeks.
- June-August should become a 13-week Trimester 2 plan by adding August 31-September 4 as the closing/assessment week.
- September-December can stay as the 13-week Trimester 3 plan.

After that, the plan is feasible enough to become the basis for rubrics, study guides, and summative assessments.
