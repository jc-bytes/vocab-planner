# 5th Grade Technology - August 2026

## Monthly Focus
August covers physical computing ideas through input-output cards, Scratch or Blockly-style logic, classroom simulations, and an Interactive Routine STEM Project. The added August 31-September 4 week is used as the required 13th week for Trimester 2 closing, make-up work, and evidence collection.

## Weekly Time
- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 5

## Competences
- Explain how inputs, conditions, choices, and outputs work in simple everyday and computer-style examples.
- Test and debug short if-then routines using cards, Scratch, Blockly, or paper fallback options.
- Design a low-risk simulated STEM routine with one input, one condition, one output, and one test result.
- Organize Trimester 2 evidence and complete make-up work without adding new graded content after Week 9.

## Learning Objectives
- Identify inputs, outputs, conditions, choices, true/false results, and simple logic errors.
- Complete and correct if-then examples with visible condition and output labels.
- Create a short Interactive Routine STEM Project and prepare Trimester 2 review or make-up evidence.

## Learning Outcomes
- Complete Summative Activity #5: Interactive Routine STEM Project in Week 11.
- Complete formative if-then practice, debugging preparation, review game, and evidence workshop tasks.
- Use Week 13 only for exam-week quiet review, organization, or school-directed tasks.

## Evaluation
- Summative Activity #5 in Week 11: Interactive Routine STEM Project.
- Week 9 debugging work is formative preparation. Weeks 10-12 are formative review, make-up, product sharing, and trimester reflection. Week 13 is exam week/no new work.
