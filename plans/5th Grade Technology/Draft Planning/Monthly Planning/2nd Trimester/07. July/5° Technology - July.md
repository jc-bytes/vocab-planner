# 5th Grade Technology - July 2026

## Monthly Focus
July simplifies the annual video-production topic into storyboard and visual media planning without filming. Lessons use visual communication, scene order, sound notes, audience choices, drawings, slides, posters, and short product sharing.

## Weekly Time
- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Competences
- Plan a clear visual message for a specific audience using title, visuals, captions, order, and purpose.
- Use storyboard panels to organize a short visual story without filming or mobile-device work.
- Share a short visual media product and use feedback to improve readability and design choices.

## Learning Objectives
- Identify clear and confusing design choices in posters, slides, or sample visual media.
- Create a six-panel storyboard with title, audience, purpose, scene order, captions, and sound/narration notes.
- Present a short visual product with clear evidence of audience, purpose, and one design choice.

## Learning Outcomes
- Complete Summative Activity #3: six-panel storyboard design plan.
- Complete Summative Activity #4: short visual media product-sharing presentation.
- Complete formative media comparison, visual sketching, peer feedback, revision, and presentation practice.

## Evaluation
- Summative Activity #3 in Week 6: six-panel storyboard design plan.
- Summative Activity #4 in Week 8: short visual media product-sharing presentation.
- All other July activities are formative media comparisons, sketching, peer feedback, and revision.
