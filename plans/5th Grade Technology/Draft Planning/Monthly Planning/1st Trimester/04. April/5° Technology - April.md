# 5th Grade Technology - April 2026

## Monthly Focus
April connects robot parts to communication, sensors, safe kit handling, and the first K96131 build tasks. Lessons stay practical: students sort examples, label diagrams, plan build steps, and prepare short demos instead of studying long theory.

## Weekly Time
- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 5

## Competences
- Explain simple robot communication, signals, sensors, and safe responses using concrete examples.
- Identify and organize K96131 kit parts or picture cards before using guided build steps.
- Plan, test, and explain Worm Robot and Telescopic Clamp movement with sketches, arrows, and safety notes.

## Learning Objectives
- Match robot signals and sensor responses to simple scenarios.
- Create a K96131 inventory and describe what selected parts do.
- Prepare Worm Robot and Telescopic Clamp plans with parts, movement arrows, safe steps, and troubleshooting ideas.

## Learning Outcomes
- Complete Summative Activity #3: communication and sensor scenario quiz.
- Complete Summative Activity #4: Worm Robot build plan with parts, movement sketch, safe steps, and troubleshooting ideas.
- Complete Summative Activity #5: Telescopic Clamp mechanism check with labels, arrows, and safety notes.

## Evaluation
- Summative Activity #3 in Week 5: communication and sensor scenario quiz.
- Summative Activity #4 in Week 7: Worm Robot build plan.
- All other April activities are formative practice, sorting, guided build work, notebook evidence, and short demos.
