# 5th Grade Technology - May 2026

## Monthly Focus
May protects hands-on robotics time for the Telescopic Clamp, Propeller Aircraft, Scratch or Blockly control, review, make-up work, and simple demonstrations. The last two weeks are intentionally lighter so students can finish evidence, review skills, and prepare for trimester closing needs.

## Weekly Time
- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Competences
- Assemble or model simple robotics mechanisms using safe guided steps and paper fallback options when needed.
- Test Telescopic Clamp and Propeller Aircraft movement through observation, diagrams, and short explanations.
- Use Scratch, Blockly, or command cards to model simple robot routines with sequences, repeats, and responses.

## Learning Objectives
- Test clamp extension, retraction, grip, and support using a simple checklist.
- Explain propeller movement with propeller, thrust, motion, balance, and safety language.
- Create and revise a short Scratch/Blockly-style routine or paper command routine with a clear response.

## Learning Outcomes
- Complete formative clamp assembly, clamp testing, propeller movement, and propeller demo evidence.
- Create a Scratch/Blockly or paper routine with at least 5 commands and one response idea.
- Use exam-week classes only for quiet review, organization, make-up evidence, or school-directed tasks.

## Evaluation
- No new summative activities are added in May because Trimester 1 already has 5 summatives by Week 9.
- Weeks 10-12 include build practice, demo preparation, make-up work, review checks, and short reflections. Week 13 is exam week/no new work.
