# 5th Grade Technology - December 2026

## Monthly Focus
December is reserved for buffer time, exams, school activities, review, make-up work, product sharing if needed, and year-end reflection. No new summative activities are introduced.

## Weekly Time
- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 2

## Competences
- Review key technology ideas from databases, vector drawing, and selection logic.
- Organize final evidence and complete missing work using checklists and quiet review routines.
- Reflect on safe, responsible, and creative technology learning without starting new graded content.

## Learning Objectives
- Review database, vector drawing, and selection vocabulary with examples.
- Complete make-up work or improve weak evidence when directed.
- Organize notebooks or digital folders for trimester closing and exam-week routines.

## Learning Outcomes
- Complete formative review, make-up work, reflection, and evidence organization in Week 12.
- Use Week 13 only for exam-week quiet review, organization, or school-directed tasks.
- Do not add new summative activities because Trimester 3 already has five summatives by Week 9.

## Evaluation
- No new summative activities are added in December because Trimester 3 already has 5 summatives by Week 9.
- December formative work includes review, make-up work, reflection, and evidence organization in Week 12. Week 13 is exam week/no new work.
