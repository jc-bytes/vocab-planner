# 5th Grade Technology - November 2026

## Monthly Focus
November shares the vector product and introduces Scratch selection in quizzes. Programming stays light through conditions, outcomes, if-then choices, simple quiz logic, guided examples, and small checks.

## Weekly Time
- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 hours
- Month plan weeks: 4

## Competences
- Present a short vector product with clear evidence of tools, design choices, and improvement.
- Identify conditions, outcomes, true/false choices, and logic errors in simple Scratch or Blockly-style selection.
- Use selection language to plan, test, debug, and explain short quiz-style logic tasks.

## Learning Objectives
- Share a vector product or polished icon using product evidence and a one-minute explanation.
- Complete if-then and if-then-else examples by identifying conditions, correct outcomes, and incorrect outcomes.
- Correct simple logic errors and revise quiz questions or feedback messages.

## Learning Outcomes
- Complete Summative Activity #4: vector product-sharing presentation.
- Complete Summative Activity #5: Scratch selection quiz-style check.
- Use Weeks 10-12 for formative selection practice, review, make-up planning, and guided evidence revision.

## Evaluation
- Summative Activity #4 in Week 8: vector product-sharing presentation.
- Summative Activity #5 in Week 9: Scratch selection quiz-style check.
- Weeks 10-12 are formative buffer, make-up, review, and reflection. Week 13 is exam week/no new work.
