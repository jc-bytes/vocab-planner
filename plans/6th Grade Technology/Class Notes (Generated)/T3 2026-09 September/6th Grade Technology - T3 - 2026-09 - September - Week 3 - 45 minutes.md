## Class Objective

Use formulas on the mBot data table.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: Which mBot result can we calculate? Review formula, cell reference, sum, average, input, output, and value.

## While Activities

- Use formulas on the mBot data table. Calculate one total, count, or average. Predict the answer before calculating. Record one input and output example.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining what the formula calculated. Share one result.
