## Class Objective

Calculate totals and averages from mBot data.

## Pre-Activities

- Open the prepared mBot formula practice file or class data table. Review how to copy a formula without changing the headers.

## While Activities

- Calculate totals and averages from mBot data. Change one input value and observe what changes. Record one input/output change and one question a chart could answer next week.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the formula practice task. Complete a reflection: What changed when the input changed?
