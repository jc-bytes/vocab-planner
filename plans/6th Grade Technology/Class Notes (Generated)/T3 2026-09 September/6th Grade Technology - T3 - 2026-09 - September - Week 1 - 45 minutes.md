## Class Objective

Inspect numbered mBots using the readiness checklist.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What evidence did we leave ready from Trimester 2? Review September Week 1 practice vocabulary (part 1). data: information from a test record: one row header: column label table: rows and columns

## While Activities

- Inspect numbered mBots using the readiness checklist. Match each robot with its challenge sheet, saved code/screenshot, route/test data, and sensor/output notes. Identify what data could be collected in a second challenge.

## Post-Activities

- Mark the robot ready, needs charging, or needs teacher help. Write one data field you could record.
