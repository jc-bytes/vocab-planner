## Class Objective

Practice your one-minute explanation with a partner.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What should your audience understand about your program? Review December Week 3 practice vocabulary. input: information or action going in output: what a device shows or does condition: rule that is checked variable: stores a changing value improve: make better demonstrate: show how something works explain: make clear with words Prepare answers to: What does it do? What input does it use? What output does it show? What did I improve?

## While Activities

- Practice your one-minute explanation with a partner. Use vocabulary words correctly in your explanation. Improve one part of your explanation after practice.
