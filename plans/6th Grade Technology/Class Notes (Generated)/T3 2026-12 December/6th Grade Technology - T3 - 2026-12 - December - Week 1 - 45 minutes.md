## Class Objective

Practice building an if/then flowchart with a sample light or temperature condition.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: How do devices know what is happening around them? Review December Week 1 practice vocabulary (part 1). sensor: part that detects something condition: rule that is checked if/then: rule where one action follows a condition light: brightness Complete three everyday if/then examples.

## While Activities

- Practice building an if/then flowchart with a sample light or temperature condition. Review the sensor condition flowchart expectations before the next class.

## Post-Activities

- Share the condition from your flowchart. Write one sentence explaining what the output does.
