## Class Objective

Plan the project input, output, and success rule.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What should your project do for a user? Review December Week 2 practice vocabulary. input: information or action going in output: what a device shows or does variable: stores a changing value test: try and check improve: make better success rule: rule that says the task worked project: work made to show or solve something Choose one simple project idea: alarm, mood display, counter, reaction game, night light, or step-counter-style idea.

## While Activities

- Plan the project input, output, and success rule. List the blocks or features you already know how to use. Check that the project can be finished in class time.

## Post-Activities

- Submit the project plan. Explain your success rule to a partner.
