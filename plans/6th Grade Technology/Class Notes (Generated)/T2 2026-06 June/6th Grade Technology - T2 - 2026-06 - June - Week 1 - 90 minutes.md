## Class Objective

Create a vocabulary table with definition, illustration, and example sentence for these words: robot, mBot, program, instruction, safety, motor, wheel, sensor, LED, buzzer.

## Pre-Activities

- Play Blooket to review safety actions

## While Activities

- Complete Summative Activity #1: Create a vocabulary table with definition, illustration, and example sentence for these words: robot, mBot, program, instruction, safety, motor, wheel, sensor, LED, buzzer. Submit your vocabulary table in class or on Google Classroom.
- Open mBot Knowledge Cards Part 1 and find the card connected to parts and setup.
- Identify the main mBot parts: wheels, motors, board/controller, sensors, LEDs, buzzer, power. Label the main parts on the mbot diagram.
- Diagram link: https://canva.link/o1ak5tjgk8h5chk

## Post-Activities

- Submit the labeled mBot diagram. Share one mBot part and its function.
