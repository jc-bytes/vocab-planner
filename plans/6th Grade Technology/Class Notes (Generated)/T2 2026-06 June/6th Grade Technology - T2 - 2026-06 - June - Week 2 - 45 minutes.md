## Class Objective

Complete an unplugged movement activity with step-by-step commands.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: Why do instructions need to be in order?
- Review the weeks practice vocabulary (part 1). sequence: steps in order forward: toward the front backward: toward the back turn: change direction

## While Activities

- Put the movement commands in the correct order.
- Complete an unplugged movement activity with step-by-step commands.
- Predict what a short mBot command sequence will do. Record the predicted movement in your notebook.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining why sequence matters. Share your command order with a partner.
