## Class Objective

Add an LED or buzzer signal to a movement program.

## Pre-Activities

- Complete a vocabulary review activity using June Week 4 practice vocabulary (part 2). state: what something is doing now start: beginning finish: end Review one mBot Knowledge Card connected to LED, buzzer, or output.

## While Activities

- Add an LED or buzzer signal to a movement program. Test whether the signal happens at the correct time. Ask another group to identify what your signal means. Improve the signal if it is not clear.

## Post-Activities

- Show your movement plus signal program. Complete a short reflection about how outputs help robots communicate.
