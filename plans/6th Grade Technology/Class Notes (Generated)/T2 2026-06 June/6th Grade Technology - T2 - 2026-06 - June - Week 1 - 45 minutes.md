## Class Objective

Identify three class rules for using the mBot responsibly: handle the robot carefully, check that no parts are loose, and test it only in the assigned safe area.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What can robots do? Choose the safest action for three robotics classroom situations.
- 1. A robot stops working during testing.
- 2. A wire or cable becomes loose.
- 3. A robot drives too close to the edge of the table.

## While Activities

- Identify three class rules for using the mBot responsibly: handle the robot carefully, check that no parts are loose, and test it only in the assigned safe area.
- Let’s make groups! Discuss how the rules protect people and materials.

## Post-Activities

- Share one robotics safety rule. Explain why the rule protects people or materials.
