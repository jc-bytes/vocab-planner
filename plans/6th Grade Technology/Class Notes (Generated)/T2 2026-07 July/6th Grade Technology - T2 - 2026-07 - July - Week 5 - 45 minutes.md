## Class Objective

Practice drawing a short mBot route map with movement steps and labeled turns.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What commands did you use in June?
- Review July Week 1 practice vocabulary (part 1). speed: how fast something moves time: how long something lasts turn: change direction sequence: steps in order Predict the route made by a short command list.

## While Activities

- Practice drawing a short mBot route map with movement steps and labeled turns.
- Compare your practice route with a partner. Review the route map and command plan expectations before the next class.

## Post-Activities

- Compare your route map with a partner. Circle one step that must be very accurate.
