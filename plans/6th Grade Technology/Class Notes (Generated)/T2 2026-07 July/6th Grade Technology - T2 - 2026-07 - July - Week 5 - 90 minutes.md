## Class Objective

Draw an mBot route map with at least 4 movement steps, write the command sequence, and label each turn or stop point.

## Pre-Activities

- Review July Week 1 practice vocabulary with Blooket or a crossword puzzle (part 2). route: path to a place accurate: correct and close to target marker: object or line showing a place Choose one mBot Knowledge Card connected to movement.

## While Activities

- Complete Summative Activity #3: Draw an mBot route map with at least 4 movement steps, write the command sequence, and label each turn or stop point. Submit the route map and command plan in class or on Google Classroom.
- Program mBot through a route with at least three moves. Test each section of the route. Debug one movement that is not accurate. Improve the route after testing.

## Post-Activities

- Demonstrate one working sequence. Write what you debugged.
