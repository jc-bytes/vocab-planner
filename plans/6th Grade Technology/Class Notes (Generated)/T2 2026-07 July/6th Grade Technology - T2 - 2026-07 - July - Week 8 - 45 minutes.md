## Class Objective

Practice building an if/then flowchart with a sample sensor condition, true response, and false response.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What happens if it rains? Review July Week 4 practice vocabulary (part 1). if: starts a condition condition: rule that is checked response: what happens after Complete three everyday if/then sentences.

## While Activities

- Practice building an if/then flowchart with a sample sensor condition, true response, and false response. Review the condition flowchart expectations before the next class.

## Post-Activities

- Share the condition and response from your flowchart. Write one sentence explaining why conditions help robots make decisions.
