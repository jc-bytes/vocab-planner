## Class Objective

Practice labeling at least three shapes on a draft sketch.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: Why should we plan before building? Review October Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 1). model: simple version or design design: plan for how something works prototype: first version for testing sketch: quick drawing Sketch a small model, such as a name badge, desk item, or project part.

## While Activities

- Practice labeling at least three shapes on a draft sketch. Discuss the purpose of the model and one possible improvement. Review the 3D model design plan expectations.

## Post-Activities

- Share the purpose of your model with a partner. Circle the part of your sketch you plan to improve.
