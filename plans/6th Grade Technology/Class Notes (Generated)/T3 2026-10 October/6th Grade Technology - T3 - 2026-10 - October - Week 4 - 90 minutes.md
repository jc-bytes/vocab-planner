## Class Objective

Label 5 micro:bit parts and sort 8 examples into input or output.

## Pre-Activities

- Complete a word soup or crossword puzzle using October Week 4 practice vocabulary (part 2). button: thing you click or press display: screen or lights icon: small picture or symbol Open your first micro:bit project.

## While Activities

- Complete Summative Activity #5: Label 5 micro:bit parts and sort 8 examples into input or output. Submit the completed input/output check in class or on Google Classroom. Create a simple program that shows text or an icon on the LED display. Add one button input if time allows. Test the program in the simulator or device.

## Post-Activities

- Show your working display or button program. Write which input or output your program used.
