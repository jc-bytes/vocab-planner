## Class Objective

Identify the title, labels, and categories in a sample chart.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: When is a chart easier than a table? Review October Week 1 practice vocabulary (part 1). chart: visual way to show data title: name of a page or project label: word that names something category: group Open the mBot STEM data table from September.

## While Activities

- Identify the title, labels, and categories in a sample chart. Choose one mBot data question that could become a chart. Explain what the chart should show.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining what charts help people understand. Share your chart idea with a partner.
