## Class Objective

Create one chart from the mBot STEM data set, add a title and labels, answer 3 questions about the chart, and write one conclusion sentence.

## Pre-Activities

- Complete a vocabulary review activity using October Week 1 practice vocabulary (part 2). axis: chart line for values or groups compare: look for similarities or differences conclusion: idea from evidence Open the mBot STEM data set.

## While Activities

- Complete Summative Activity #3: Create one chart from the mBot STEM data set, add a title and labels, answer 3 questions about the chart, and write one conclusion sentence. Submit the chart task in class or on Google Classroom.

## Post-Activities

- Share one conclusion from your chart. Write one situation where a chart is better than a table.
