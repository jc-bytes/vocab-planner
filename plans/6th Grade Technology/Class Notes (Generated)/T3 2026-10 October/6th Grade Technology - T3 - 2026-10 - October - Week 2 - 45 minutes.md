## Class Objective

Create a basic shape.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What objects are made from simple shapes? Review October Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 1). 3D: height, width, and depth shape: form like cube or sphere move: change place resize: make bigger or smaller Identify basic shapes in a real object.

## While Activities

- Open the 3D modelling tool. Create a basic shape. Practice moving, resizing, and rotating the shape. Record one tool you used.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining what 3D means. Save or screenshot your first shape practice.
