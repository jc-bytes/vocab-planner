## Class Objective

Identify basic web page parts and organize a title, section, text, and image for a specific audience and purpose.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What should visitors see first on a web page? Review April Week 2 practice vocabulary. title: name of a page or project header: top label or section section: part of a page image: picture text: written words layout: how things are arranged preview: look before final readable: easy to read Label these parts on a sample web page.

## While Activities

- Choose a title and first section for your web page. Organize your planned text and images. Check that your page idea matches your audience and purpose.

## Post-Activities

- Write one improvement you want to make before building. Show your plan to a partner.
