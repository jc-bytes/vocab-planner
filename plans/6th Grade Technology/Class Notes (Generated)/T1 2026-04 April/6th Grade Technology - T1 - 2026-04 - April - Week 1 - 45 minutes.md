## Class Objective

Distinguish safe and unsafe image use and explain why source and credit matter.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: Can we use every image we find online? Review April Week 1 practice vocabulary (part 1). copyright: rule that protects created work credit: say who made or owns something source: where information or an image came from creator: person who made something Sort examples into OK to use or not OK to use.

## While Activities

- Choose the best image source for a class project. Explain why credit matters when using images. Record two places where you can look for copyright-friendly images.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining why copyright-free images are useful. Share one safe image-search habit.
