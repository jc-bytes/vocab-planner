## Class Objective

Build a simple Google Sites page, preview it in different views, and improve two details for readability.

## Pre-Activities

- Complete a quick review puzzle using: title, header, section, image. Review your web page plan before opening your project.

## While Activities

- Create or open your Google Sites page. Add a title, header, text, and at least one image. Preview your page in different views. Improve two details that make the page easier to read.

## Post-Activities

- Complete the website checklist. Submit or show your improved web page.
