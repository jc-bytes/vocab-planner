## Class Objective

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: Where do we see tiny computers? Review November Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 1). micro:bit: tiny coding computer input: information or action going in output: what a device shows or does LED: small light Label the basic parts of a micro:bit picture.

## While Activities

- Practice sorting examples into input and output. Review the micro:bit parts and input/output check expectations before the next class.

## Post-Activities

- Share one input and one output example. Write one thing the LED display can show.
