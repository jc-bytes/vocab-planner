## Class Objective

Create a simple plan for a counter program.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What numbers change in games or counters? Review November Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 1). variable: stores a changing value value: number or data increase: make larger Predict what happens when a value increases.

## While Activities

- Create a simple plan for a counter program. Choose which button will increase the value. Choose how the value will appear on the LED display.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining what a variable remembers. Share your counter plan with a partner.
