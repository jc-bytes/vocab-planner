## Class Objective

Contribute to a shared digital file and use respectful online comments or messages to support group work.

## Pre-Activities

- Review March Week 3 practice vocabulary with Blooket, Gimkit, or a matching activity (part 2). chat: short online messages comment: note added to work respectful: kind and careful with others help: make work easier for someone Complete two sentences using the words correctly.

## While Activities

- Create a short shared slide or document with your group. Add one useful contribution to the shared file. Use one respectful comment or message to help your group. Check that every group member contributed.

## Post-Activities

- Reflect on one thing your group did well. Submit or show the shared digital work.
