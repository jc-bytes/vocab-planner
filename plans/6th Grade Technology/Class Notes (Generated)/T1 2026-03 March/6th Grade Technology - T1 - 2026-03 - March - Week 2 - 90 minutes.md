## Class Objective

Model packet delivery, rebuild a message in order, and explain one problem caused by missing or out-of-order packets.

## Pre-Activities

- Complete a word soup or crossword puzzle using March Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 2). missing: not there when needed route: path to a place delivery: getting something to the right place Review the definition of each word with a partner.

## While Activities

- Create a short packet message in a group. Send packet pieces through the classroom simulation. Rebuild a packet message in the correct order. Record what happened when the message arrived.

## Post-Activities

- Explain one problem that can happen when packets are missing or out of order. Submit the completed packet activity sheet.
