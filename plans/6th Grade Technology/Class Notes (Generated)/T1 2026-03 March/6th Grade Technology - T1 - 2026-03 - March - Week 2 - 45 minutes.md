## Class Objective

Describe the header and payload of a data packet and explain why long messages are sent in smaller parts.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: How could you send a long message in smaller pieces? Review March Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 1). send: move a message to someone receive: get a message part: one piece of something bigger order: correct sequence Draw a simple packet with a header and payload.

## While Activities

- Split a short sentence into packet pieces. Label the header and payload in one packet example. Predict what happens when a packet is missing.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining why data is sent in packets. Complete one exit question about header or payload.
