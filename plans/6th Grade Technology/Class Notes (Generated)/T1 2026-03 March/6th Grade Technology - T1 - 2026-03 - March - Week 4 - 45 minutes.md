## Class Objective

Distinguish public and private communication and identify audience and permission needs in simple online scenarios.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What should stay private online? Review March Week 4 practice vocabulary (part 1). public: seen by many people private: kept for only certain people audience: people who see or hear work permission: saying it is okay web page: one page on a website Sort examples into public or private communication.

## While Activities

- Choose the safest response for simple online situations. Practice naming the audience and permission need for three sample scenarios. Review the scenario quiz expectations before the next class.

## Post-Activities

- Record one responsible communication rule. Share one scenario that made you think carefully.
