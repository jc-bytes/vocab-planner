## Class Objective

Sort online actions into safe, unsafe, or unsure categories and write one responsible internet rule for sixth graders.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What makes the internet useful? Review May Week 1 practice vocabulary (part 1). safe: not likely to cause harm kind: friendly and caring respectful: kind and careful with others reliable: can be trusted Sort online actions into safe, unsafe, or unsure.

## While Activities

- Choose the safest response for three Internet Day situations. Discuss why respectful messages matter online. Record one responsible internet rule.

## Post-Activities

- Write one internet rule for sixth graders. Share your rule with a partner.
