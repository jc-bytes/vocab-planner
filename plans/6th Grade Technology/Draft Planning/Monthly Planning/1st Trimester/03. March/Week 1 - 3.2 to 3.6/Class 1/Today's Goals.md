## Vocabulary 
- **Communication:** Sharing information or ideas.
- **Internet:** A global network of connected computers.
- **Public:** Communication visible to everyone.
- **Private:** Communication shared with specific people.
- **One to Many:** Communication from one person to many. 
- **One to One:** Communication between two people.
