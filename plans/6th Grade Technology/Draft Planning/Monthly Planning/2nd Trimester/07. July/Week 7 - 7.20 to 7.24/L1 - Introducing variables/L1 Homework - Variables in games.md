# Homework: Real-world variables

In programming, a variable is a value that can be set and changed throughout the running of a program. In the real world, where have you encountered variables? (This could be at home, at school, or anywhere else.)

Think about the examples that you looked at in this lesson. Record your ideas below. Be creative. You can use words, pictures, or a combination of the two — just make sure that you identify the variables!
