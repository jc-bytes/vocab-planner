# Lesson 5: How we communicate

Enjoyed teaching these lessons? Found a mistake? Share feedback at the-cc.io/feedback.

## Introduction

Learners deepen their understanding of the term ‘communication’. They explore different methods of communication, before they consider internet-based communication in more detail. Finally, learners evaluate which methods of communication suit particular purposes.

## Learning objectives

To recognise how we communicate using technology

- I can explain the different ways in which people communicate

- I can identify that there are a variety of ways to communicate over the internet

- I can choose methods of communication to suit particular purposes

## Key vocabulary

Communication, internet

## Preparation

Subject knowledge:

- You will need an understanding of the term ‘communication’, as well as knowledge of different methods of communication.

- You will need to be familiar with different methods of internet communication, and the suitability of these in a variety of contexts.

You will need:

- L5 Slides

- A2 – Individual whiteboards

- A3 Handout – Choosing how to communicate (printed on A3 paper)

- L5 Homework – Communication at home (optional)

## Assessment opportunities

Activity 1: Assess whether learners can identify what communication is and the ways in which we can communicate.

Activity 2: Assess whether learners can recognise knowledge of internet communication methods.

Activity 3: Assess whether learners can justify their choices of communication methods.

## Outline plan

Please note that the slide deck labels the activities in the top right-hand corner to help you navigate the lesson.

*Timings are rough guides

| Introduction<br>(Slides 3–4)<br>5 mins | What is communication?<br>Show slide 3. Explain that in an earlier lesson in this unit, the learners considered how they can successfully communicate.<br>Display slide 4. Explain that just like humans use protocols — which can be based on language, culture, or tradition — computers also use protocols to communicate successfully. |
| --- | --- |
| Activity 1<br>(Slides 5–7)<br>10 mins | Communicating “Yes”<br>Display slide 5 and explain that there are many different ways of communicating with others. Organise learners into pairs and challenge them to think of as many ways as possible of communicating the word “Yes”.<br>After their feedback has been shared, build the slide. Emphasise that for a protocol to be effective, both people communicating need to understand it.<br>Display slide 6 and give learners three minutes to convey the three words and phrases on the slide in as many ways as possible.<br>Display slide 7. Ask learners to consider how many of the ways of conveying the previous words and phrases would work if they were not in the same room as the person that they were communicating with. Build the slide to show the image of connections between people, which represents the internet. |
| Activity 2 (Slide 8)<br>10 mins | Communicating using the internet<br>Share slide 8. In this activity, your learners will build a class list of ways in which people can communicate over the internet.<br>Give the learners two minutes to make a list on their individual whiteboards of the ways in which they can communicate over the internet. Once they have done this, ask learners to share their lists with the rest of their table (or group) and to agree on a table (or group) list. Nominate a spokesperson for each group to share their lists, and then agree on one class list. Display the class list on a large whiteboard or flip chart. |
| Activity 3<br>(Slide 9)<br>10 mins | Choosing how to communicate<br>Show slide 9. Give each table (or group) one scenario from the handout, printed on A3 paper. Tell the learners that they need to work together to create a mind map of the many different ways that their message could be communicated over the internet. Learners can use the class list created in Activity 2 to help them.<br>Note: Learners need their mind maps in Lesson 6 of this unit. |
| Plenary<br>(Slide 10)<br>10 mins | Showcase<br>Display slide 10. Invite one learner from each group to share one of the responses from their mind map with the class. Ensure each group has the opportunity to share a response. |
| Next time<br>(Slides 11–12)<br>5 mins | This lesson, next lesson<br>Review the ‘Assessment’ and ‘Summary’ slides. |
| Homework<br>(Optional) | Communication at home<br>Ask learners to complete the task below on their homework sheets:<br>Pick a time this week, such as an evening or part of the weekend, to note down when people in your home communicate with other people outside your home. This could be any communication, not just communication over the internet. Record your findings in the table. |
