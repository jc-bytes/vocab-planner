# 6° Technology - August 2026

## Monthly Focus

Students finish the mBot unit by practicing with mBot Knowledge Cards Part 1, completing an integrated robotics challenge, and reflecting on how they tested and improved their work.

## Weekly Time

- Class 1: 45 minutes
- Class 2: 90 minutes
- Weekly total: 3 class hours

## Main Resources

- Pen
- pencil
- technology notebook
- student's email and password

## Competences

- Use mBot Knowledge Cards to review movement, output, sensor, and debugging tasks.
- Combine movement with one output or sensor in a simple final robot challenge.
- Demonstrate and explain a robot program using clear testing and improvement evidence.

## Learning Objectives

- Read and apply selected mBot Knowledge Cards to complete guided practice stations.
- Plan, build, and test a realistic mBot challenge with a goal and success rule.
- Explain the robot action, one card, block, or part used, and one improvement made after testing.

## Learning Outcomes

- Complete an mBot card practice check with recorded robot action and explanation.
- Submit a final challenge plan and station notes showing testing and debugging.
- Demonstrate a final mBot challenge and complete a reflection about goal, action, and improvement.

## Evaluation

- Complete mBot review activities.
- Demonstrate the final mBot challenge.
- Complete a vocabulary/parts check.
- Complete Summative Activity #5: Use one assigned mBot Knowledge Card Part 1, complete the card practice task, record the robot action, and explain what the card helps the robot do.

## Teacher Reality Notes

- Main purpose: finish robotics with one controlled integrated mBot challenge.
- Grade evidence: Summative Activity #5 is the card practice check; grade correct card use, recorded robot action, and explanation of what the card helps the robot do. Final challenge evidence is a demonstration checklist plus reflection: goal, movement, output or sensor, test result, one improvement, and simple explanation.
- Flow guardrail: teams should work in pairs when possible. If groups of three are necessary, assign driver, tester, and recorder so the grading evidence shows each student's contribution.
- Materials to prepare: charged mBots, mBot Knowledge Cards Part 1, station cards, final challenge planning sheet, demonstration checklist/rubric, reflection form, floor tape/markers, projector, computers/software.
- Make-up option: card practice can be made up at one robot station; final demonstration needs a short scheduled make-up slot.
- Risk: demonstrations can overflow. Limit each team to one minute of robot demo and one minute of explanation.
