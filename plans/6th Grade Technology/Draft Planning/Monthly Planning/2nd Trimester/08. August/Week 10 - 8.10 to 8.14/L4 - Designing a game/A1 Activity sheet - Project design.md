### Activity 2: Falling star algorithm

| The falling star moves down by eight from a random x position at the top of the screen.<br>If the sprite falls onto the bowl, change the score by three. It falls again from a random x position at the top of the screen.<br>If the falling star touches the screen bottom, it falls again from a random x position at the top of the screen. |
