# Explorer task

# Introduction

The calculations you have added into your spreadsheet calculate the cost of travelling from school to each of the attractions.

This doesn’t include the cost it would take to get back to school. Can you use formulas to work out the total cost of petrol from school to each attraction and back again? You can add extra data headings if needed.
