# Weekly shopping

Use the table below to list some of the items that your family buy in your weekly shopping. Can you construct some formulas to work out how much was spent on each item?

If you would prefer to complete your work in a spreadsheet application, please save or print your work to allow your teacher to mark it.

If you do not have a weekly shopping receipt to help you with this task, you could use an online shopping website to find out some example prices, or ask an adult to help you.
