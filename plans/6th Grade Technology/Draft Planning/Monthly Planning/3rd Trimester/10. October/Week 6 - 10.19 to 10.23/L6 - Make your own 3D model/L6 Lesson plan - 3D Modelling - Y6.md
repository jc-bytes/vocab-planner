# Lesson 6: Make your own 3D model

Enjoyed teaching these lessons? Found a mistake? Share feedback at the-cc.io/feedback.

## Introduction

Learners will create a computer 3D model based on their design. They will then evaluate their model and that of another learner, before modifying their own model to improve it.

## Learning objectives

To create my own digital 3D model

- I can construct a 3D model based on a design

- I can explain how my 3D model could be improved

- I can modify my 3D model to improve it

## Key vocabulary

Construct, evaluate, modify

## Preparation

Subject knowledge:

You will need to be familiar with using 3D objects as placeholders in Tinkercad. Tinkercad’s Start Learning 3D tutorials provide a good starting point: https://www.tinkercad.com/learn/designs.

You will need:

- Slides

- A2 Handout – Evaluation

- Designs from the previous lesson

- Desktop or laptop computers with internet connection to access Tinkercad. If using laptop computers, an external mouse is preferable, but not essential.

## Assessment opportunities

Activity 1: Assess learners’ ability to translate a paper-based design into a computer 3D model.

Activity 2: Assess learners’ ability to suggest improvements to their own and a partner’s model.

Activity 3: Assess learners’ ability to make improvements to their computer 3D model.
