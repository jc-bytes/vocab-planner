# Lesson 2: Modifying 3D objects

Enjoyed teaching these lessons? Found a mistake? Share feedback at the-cc.io/feedback.

## Introduction

Learners will manipulate 3D objects digitally. They will resize objects in one, two, and three dimensions. They will also lift and lower 3D objects relative to the workplane and combine two 3D objects to make a new shape. Finally, learners will recolour 3D objects.

## Learning objectives

To identify that digital 3D objects can be modified

- I can resize an object in three dimensions

- I can lift/lower 3D objects

- I can recolour a 3D object

## Key vocabulary

Handles, resize, lift, lower, recolour

## Preparation

Subject knowledge:

You will need to be familiar with resizing, lifting/lowering, and altering the colour of 3D objects in Tinkercad. Tinkercad’s Start Learning 3D tutorials provide a good starting point: https://www.tinkercad.com/learn/designs.

You will need:

- Slides

- A1 Handout – Handle with care!

- Desktop or laptop computers with internet connection to access Tinkercad. If using laptop computers, an external mouse is preferable, but not essential.

## Assessment opportunities

Activity 1: Assess learners’ ability to use handles to resize objects, identifying which handle is the most appropriate to use for combinations of width, depth, and height.

Activity 2: Assess learners’ ability to lift and lower, resize, and move objects to position them accurately. Also look for learners to change the viewing perspective to make positioning easier.

Activity 3: Assess learners’ ability to recolour objects.

## Outline plan

Please note that the slide deck labels the activities in the top right-hand corner to help you navigate the lesson.

*Timings are rough guides

| Introduction<br>(Slides 1–3)<br>5 mins | Working in three dimensions<br>Introduce the lesson and the learning objectives.<br>Remind learners that in the previous lesson they worked with 3D shapes in Tinkercad, moving them in two dimensions on the workplane.<br>Build the slide to reveal that in this lesson, they will work in three dimensions by lifting and lowering objects, and modifying their size, including their height. |
| --- | --- |
| Activity 1<br>(Slides 4–6)<br>15 mins | Handle with care!<br>Show slide 4. Explain to learners that when a shape is selected, a number of ‘handles’ appear. In the handout, learners are directed to try each of the different types of handles.<br>Once they have completed the handout, build slide 5 to show the function of each of the handles. When clicked and dragged:<br>The white corner handles on the base of the shape will resize the objects in two dimensions, changing its width and length.<br>The black handles on each side of the base of the shape will resize the shape in one dimension, either width or length.<br>The white handle on top of the shape will resize the height of the shape.<br>When shift is pressed, all the handles will resize the shape evenly in all three dimensions.<br>Show slide 6 and explain that clicking on a handle will show the dimensions of the object along the dimensions which the handle can change the shape. For example, the handle selected on the slide is shown red and will enable you to change the width and length of the cuboid.<br>Note: Size and dimensions will be covered in more detail in Lesson 4 of this unit. |
| Activity 2 (Slides 7–8)<br>15 mins | Lifting and lowering<br>Show slide 7. Explain that the black cone shaped handle on top of a shape enables you to lift or lower a shape relative to the workplane, but that it will not change the size of the shape.<br>Show slide 8 and challenge learners to manipulate a cuboid and a triangular prism so that they are the same size and the triangular prism is on top of the cuboid, creating a house-type shape.<br>Explain that they will need to move, resize, and lift the triangular prism so that it sits neatly on top of the cuboid.<br>By clicking on the top handle of a shape, learners will see the distance in mm of the shape from the workplane, with a value of 0 meaning that it is sitting on the workplane. This will tell learners how much they will need to lift the triangular prism. |
| Activity 3<br>(Slide 9)<br>5 mins | Changing colours<br>Show learners that when a shape is selected, the colour option appears above and to the right of the workplane. Give learners the opportunity to recolour the objects on their workplane or add further objects of different colours. |
| Plenary<br>(Slides 10–11)<br>5 mins | 3D printing<br>Explain to learners that models generated on a computer can be printed using a 3D printer. Explain, in simple terms, that a 3D printer works by heating plastic filament and then feeding it through a nozzle to create thin layers of plastic which are built by the printer. The video shows a 3D printer creating a cuboid.<br>Explain that one of the constraints of 3D printing is that a single object can only be printed in one colour.<br>Show the image on slide 11 of a 3D printed cuboid and highlight the small horizontal lines on each side which show the layers of the print.<br>Note: On the video, both the nozzle and the bed of the printer are moving — the camera is being held still. |
