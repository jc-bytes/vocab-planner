# Parts of 3D objects

# Introduction

Find some 3D objects at home or in school such as:

- A chest of drawers

- A pencil holder

- A shoe rack

- A TV unit

- A table

Examine the objects and look for the 3D shapes that make up the object. For example, a table might be made up of four cylinders (the legs) and a cuboid (the top). Only move an object if it is safe to do so. If you are not sure, ask a responsible adult.

Name and draw the 3D object, including labelling the various 3D shapes that are contained within it.
