# Moving objects

# Try moving the objects on your workplane and then answer the questions in the table below.

| Q: When you move objects, can you move them in three dimensions? |
| --- |
| A: By clicking on the shapes and dragging them, or by selecting the shapes and using the arrow keys, learners will only be able to move shapes in two dimensions on the workplace. Another tool is needed to lift and lower objects, which learners will be introduced to in the next lesson. |
| Q: How easy is it to position objects where you want them to be? |
| A: It is relatively easy to move objects across the workplace, however depending on the perspective the objects are viewed from, it can be difficult to place them accurately. |
| Q: What can you do to help you to position objects? |
| A: Changing the viewing angle can help you to position objects more accurately. For fine movements it can also help to zoom in on the object. |
| Q: What happens when you put more than one object in the same place? |
| A: The objects will overlap each other. Sometimes, if one object is smaller than the other, the larger object can completely obscure the smaller object. |
