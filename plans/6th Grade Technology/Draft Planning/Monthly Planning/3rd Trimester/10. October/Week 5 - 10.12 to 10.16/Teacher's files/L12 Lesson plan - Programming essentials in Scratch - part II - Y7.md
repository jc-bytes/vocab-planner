# Lesson 12: Translate this! (Part 2)

Enjoyed teaching these lessons? Found a mistake? Share feedback at the-cc.io/feedback.

## Introduction

Learners are given a scenario to create a translation quiz for a Modern Foreign Languages teacher. The learners will decompose the problem and start to build a Scratch program to meet the requirements. This is a pair programming project to take place over two lessons; pairs will develop their programs to differing levels. A rubric is used for peer- or self- assessment to check learners’ progress. Extension activities allow learners to explore more challenging aspects of the solution. In Lesson 12, learners are given a multiple choice quiz as a formal final assessment.

## Learning objectives

- To decompose a larger problem into smaller subproblems

- To apply appropriate constructs to solve a problem

## Key vocabulary

Decompose

## Assessment opportunities

Lessons 11 and 12 involve a pair programming activity. A rubric has been provided in order for learners to peer- or self- assess their work.

A multiple choice quiz has been provided to individually assess the progress of each learner. This will be completed in Lesson 12.
