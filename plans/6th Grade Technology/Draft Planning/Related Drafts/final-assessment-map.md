# Final Assessment Map - 6th Grade Technology 2026

This map is for approval before creating worksheets, rubrics, forms, or templates. It keeps each trimester to exactly five counted summatives and separates additional demo/reflection evidence from extra gradebook items. The second and third trimesters now use controlled STEM projects to replace existing summative slots, not add new grades.

## 1A. Quiz And Presentation Balance Check

| Trimester | Quiz Or Quiz-Style Check | Timing Check | Presentation, Demonstration, Or Product Share | Balance Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Week 4: Online Communication Scenario Quiz | Acceptable. Students practice online communication, public/private sharing, audience, permission, and safe responses before completing the quiz-style scenario task. | Week 6: Website Product Share; Week 10: Internet Day/Recycling Awareness Product | Meets requirement. | 2 | Week 3: mBot Movement Debugging Check | Strong. Students practice movement, sequence, forward/backward/turn/stop, and first mBot movement before the debugging check. | Week 12: mBot STEM Demonstration and Reflection | Meets requirement. The mBot STEM project naturally counts because students explain and demonstrate a robot challenge. |
| 3 | Week 3: Formula Practice With mBot Data | Strong. Students practice formulas after creating the mBot STEM data table. | Week 8: Project Presentation and Reflection; Week 12: Final micro:bit Demonstration and Reflection | Meets requirement. Week 8 provides an earlier presentation before the final micro:bit project/demo period. |

Notes:

- No extra counted summative grades were added for this balance rule.
- The Trimester 3 formula work is practice evidence, not an extra summative.
- The Trimester 1 website product share is presentation/product-sharing evidence, not an added summative.
- Exam/project demonstrations can count as trimester presentations when students explain the product, design choice, test result, or improvement.

- Projector/screen.

## 5. Make-Up Plan For Likely Interruptions

| Risk | Likely Activities Affected | Make-Up Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Account/login problems | Google Sites, Scratch, Google Sheets, Tinkercad, MakeCode | Use printed planning/checklist task during class; schedule device completion during make-up station |
| Robot batteries or missing parts | mBot movement, sensor practice, final challenge, second STEM bridge challenge | Use paper route/debugging/flowchart evidence first; use teacher-provided mBot data cards for spreadsheet tasks; complete robot demo later at supervised robot station |
| Internet outage | Online forms, research, Google tools, MakeCode | Use printed worksheets and offline planning; upload/submit later |
| Shortened class period | Packet simulation, robotics challenges, presentations/demos | Stop at planning or partial test evidence; collect exit ticket; finish demo in next available station |
| Student absence on summative day | Any counted summative | Provide printed or Google Classroom version; for hardware tasks, schedule supervised station make-up |
| End-of-year interruptions | December micro:bit final demo | Collect project file/screenshot before presentation day; allow one-minute individual or small-group make-up demo |
