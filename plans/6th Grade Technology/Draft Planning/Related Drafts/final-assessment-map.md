## 1A. Quiz And Presentation Balance Check

| Trimester | Quiz Or Quiz-Style Check | Timing Check | Presentation, Demonstration, Or Product Share | Balance Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Week 4: Online Communication Scenario Quiz | Acceptable. Students practice online communication, public/private sharing, audience, permission, and safe responses before completing the quiz-style scenario task. | Week 6: Website Product Share; Week 10: Internet Day/Recycling Awareness Product | Meets requirement. | 2 | Week 3: mBot Movement Debugging Check | Strong. Students practice movement, sequence, forward/backward/turn/stop, and first mBot movement before the debugging check. | Week 10: Final mBot Demonstration and Reflection | Meets requirement. The mBot final demonstration naturally counts because students explain and demonstrate a robot challenge. |
| 3 | Week 3: Spreadsheet Formula Check | Strong. Students practice formulas in Week 2 before the short quiz-style formula check in Week 3. | Week 7: Project Presentation and Reflection; Week 12: Final micro:bit Demonstration and Reflection | Meets requirement. Week 7 provides an earlier presentation before the final micro:bit project/demo period. |

Notes:

- No extra counted summative grades were added for this balance rule.
- The Trimester 3 formula check is quiz-style practice evidence, not Summative #6.
- The Trimester 1 website product share is presentation/product-sharing evidence, not an added summative.
- Exam/project demonstrations can count as trimester presentations when students explain the product, design choice, test result, or improvement.

## 4. Hardware/Resource Checklist

### Accounts And Software

- Google Classroom submission space.
- Google Sites access.
- Scratch accounts or offline Scratch access.
- Google Sheets or spreadsheet software access.
- Tinkercad or approved 3D modelling tool accounts.
- MakeCode access for micro:bit.
- Safe image-search links.
- Safe research links for Internet Day and recycling/e-waste.

### Classroom Technology

- Projector/screen.
- Teacher computer.
- Student computers or shared devices.
- Reliable internet access.
- Printed fallback copies for all counted non-hardware summatives.

### Robotics And Physical Computing

- Charged mBots.
- mBot Knowledge Cards Part 1.
- USB cables or confirmed Bluetooth setup.
- Batteries and chargers.
- Spare batteries if available.
- Numbered robot kits.
- Floor tape, route markers, or obstacle markers.
- Sensor test area.
- micro:bits or confirmed simulator-only plan.
- micro:bit USB cables.
- micro:bit batteries if physical devices are used.

## 5. Make-Up Plan For Likely Interruptions

| Risk | Likely Activities Affected | Make-Up Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Account/login problems | Google Sites, Scratch, Google Sheets, Tinkercad, MakeCode | Use printed planning/checklist task during class; schedule device completion during make-up station |
| Robot batteries or missing parts | mBot movement, sensor practice, final challenge | Use paper route/debugging/flowchart evidence first; complete robot demo later at supervised robot station |
| Internet outage | Online forms, research, Google tools, MakeCode | Use printed worksheets and offline planning; upload/submit later |
| Shortened class period | Packet simulation, robotics challenges, presentations/demos | Stop at planning or partial test evidence; collect exit ticket; finish demo in next available station |
| Student absence on summative day | Any counted summative | Provide printed or Google Classroom version; for hardware tasks, schedule supervised station make-up |
| End-of-year interruptions | December micro:bit final demo | Collect project file/screenshot before presentation day; allow one-minute individual or small-group make-up demo |
