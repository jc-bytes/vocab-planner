# 6° Technology - September 2026

## Monthly Focus

Read printed school-data narratives, turn sentences into spreadsheet rows, and complete the first two formal grades.

## Weekly Time

- 6A: Monday 45 minutes; Tuesday 90 minutes
- 6B: Tuesday 45 minutes; Wednesday 90 minutes
- 6C: Wednesday 45 minutes; Friday 90 minutes
- Weekly total when both blocks meet: 3 class hours

## Calendar and evidence rules

- Trimester dates: September 14 to December 11, 2026.
- Students use their own or assigned charged computer and school login when required. Phones are prohibited.
- Accepted evidence: saved files, working share links, printed records, teacher observation, and live demonstrations.
- A documented school system, account, network, or hardware failure does not reduce readiness points when the equal fallback is completed.
- Closures: Nov 3, 4, 5, 10, and 28; Nov 28 is Saturday and has no scheduled Technology class. Dec 8 is closed. Oct 27 and Dec 1 are calendar-caution days, so no required new summative is placed there.

## Competences

- Extract structured data from printed narrative evidence and represent it accurately.
- Design and test digital products from measured or programmed inputs.
- Save, explain, and submit checkable evidence responsibly.

## Learning Objectives

- Follow the exact group-date schedule and complete the assigned formative or formal task.
- Use the named module sections, provided source, starter, and fallback.
- Explain how source information, measurements, or program inputs produce the submitted result.

## Learning Outcomes

- Produce the files and records named in each class block.
- Complete exactly five formal grades across the trimester.
- Use printed or live fallback evidence when required.

## Evaluation

- Summative 1: Technology Vocabulary Table. Summative 2: Narrative-to-Spreadsheet Data Table.
- All other work is formative. Readiness and responsibility are 4 of 40 points on each summative.

## Teacher Reality Notes

- Closures: Nov 3, 4, 5, 10, and 28; Nov 28 is Saturday and has no scheduled Technology class. Dec 8 is closed. Oct 27 and Dec 1 are calendar-caution days, so no required new summative is placed there.
- Open each pilot module route before class and keep the printed fallback ready.
- Formal tasks use fixed sources or teacher-assigned objects. Students do not browse for assessment content.
