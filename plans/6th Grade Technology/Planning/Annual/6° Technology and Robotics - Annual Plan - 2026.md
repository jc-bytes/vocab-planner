## Teacher Reality and Assessment Notes

- First trimester includes Internet Day and recycling/e-waste as a community technology application in May. Keep this as a small awareness and responsibility unit rather than a large design project.
- Second trimester robotics uses mBot and mBot Knowledge Cards Part 1. Do not replace this with Python, text-based programming, or a Worm Robot project for 6th grade.
- Hardware-dependent robotics grades should include make-up opportunities through a supervised robot station, emulator/card task, or paper planning/debugging task when appropriate.
- Third trimester spreadsheet, 3D modelling, and micro:bit products should be small controlled products. Assess visible evidence: worksheet/form, saved file or screenshot, demonstration checklist, and short explanation/reflection.
- Final project rubrics/checklists should name the exact evidence: goal, required features, test result, improvement, and explanation.
