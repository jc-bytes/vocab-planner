# 6th Grade Technology - Simple Classroom Progress

This file explains the year in simple classroom sentences. It is meant for quick teacher review, parent-friendly explanation, or student-facing overview.

## 1st Trimester

### March

- Week 1, Class 1: First, students work on Internet addresses. They compare a home address with a website address.
- Week 1, Class 2: Then, students work on Domain names and IP addresses. They complete Summative Activity #1: Create a vocabulary table with definition, illustration, and example sentence for these words: internet, website, browser, address, domain name, DNS, IP address, packet, message, network.
- Week 2, Class 1: Then, students work on Data packets. They split a short sentence into packet pieces.
- Week 2, Class 2: Then, students work on Packet simulation. They create a short packet message in a group.
- Week 3, Class 1: Then, students work on Working together online. They sort online tools by purpose: communicate, share, create, or present.
- Week 3, Class 2: Then, students work on Shared digital work. They create a short shared slide or document with their group.
- Week 4, Class 1: Then, students work on Responsible communication. They practice naming the audience and permission need for sample online scenarios.
- Week 4, Class 2: Then, students work on Website features and planning. They complete Summative Activity #2: Answer 8 online communication scenarios by choosing public or private, naming the audience, and deciding if permission is needed.

### April

- Week 1, Class 1: Then, students work on Copyright and images. They choose the best image source for a class project.
- Week 1, Class 2: Then, students work on Finding useful images. They complete Summative Activity #3: Select one image for the assigned web page topic, record its source, decide if it is safe to use, explain why it fits the topic, and write the credit line.
- Week 2, Class 1: Then, students work on Web page structure. They choose a title and first section for their web page.
- Week 2, Class 2: Then, students work on Build and preview. They create or open their Google Sites page.
- Week 3, Class 1: Then, students work on Links and navigation. They identify links and menus on a sample website.
- Week 3, Class 2: Then, students work on Add links and evaluate. They add one internal or external link to their web page.
- Week 4, Class 1: Then, students work on Scratch variables. They open or view a simple Scratch score example.
- Week 4, Class 2: Then, students work on Scratch score challenge. They complete Summative Activity #4: Create a Scratch score task with one variable and two clickable sprites that change the score by different amounts, then explain what each sprite does to the score.

### May

- Week 1, Class 1: Then, students work on Internet Day: responsible choices. They choose the safest response for three Internet Day situations.
- Week 1, Class 2: Then, students work on Internet Day campaign. They create a short poster or slide with one internet rule and one example.
- Week 2, Class 1: Then, students work on Recycling and e-waste. They practice sorting sample technology items and discuss responsible choices.
- Week 2, Class 2: Then, students work on Technology and the environment. They complete Summative Activity #5: Sort 10 technology items into reuse, repair, recycle, or e-waste, then explain the best responsible choice for 2 of the items.
- Week 3, Class 1: Then, students work on Plan awareness product. They sketch their awareness product layout.
- Week 3, Class 2: Then, students work on Create and present. They create a poster, slide, infographic, bookmark, or short campaign message.

## 2nd Trimester

### June

- Week 1, Class 1: Then, students work on Robotics routines and safety. They create class rules for using mBot responsibly.
- Week 1, Class 2: Then, students work on Meet the mBot. They complete Summative Activity #1: Create a vocabulary table with definition, illustration, and example sentence for these words: robot, mBot, program, instruction, safety, motor, wheel, sensor, LED, buzzer.
- Week 2, Class 1: Then, students work on Instructions and movement. They complete an unplugged movement activity with step-by-step commands.
- Week 2, Class 2: Then, students work on First mBot movements. They prepare their mBot for programming.
- Week 3, Class 1: Then, students work on Debugging movement. They practice finding mistakes in sample mBot movement sequences.
- Week 3, Class 2: Then, students work on Path challenge 1. They complete Summative Activity #2: Review 3 simple mBot movement sequences, identify the mistake in each one, write the corrected command or value, and explain one correction.
- Week 4, Class 1: Then, students work on Robot outputs. They choose one robot state, such as start, turn, stop, or finish.
- Week 4, Class 2: Then, students work on Movement plus signals. They add an LED or buzzer signal to a movement program.

### July

- Week 1, Class 1: Then, students work on Movement review. They practice drawing a short mBot route map with movement steps and labeled turns.
- Week 1, Class 2: Then, students work on Path challenge 2. They complete Summative Activity #3: Draw an mBot route map with at least 4 movement steps, write the command sequence, and label each turn or stop point.
- Week 2, Class 1: Then, students work on Loops as repeated actions. They act out a repeated command pattern.
- Week 2, Class 2: Then, students work on Repeat patterns. They program a repeated movement pattern, such as a square or zigzag.
- Week 3, Class 1: Then, students work on Sensors around us. They identify the available mBot sensor on their robot or card.
- Week 3, Class 2: Then, students work on mBot sensor practice. They observe how the sensor reacts to an object or line.
- Week 4, Class 1: Then, students work on Simple conditions. They practice building an if/then flowchart with a sample sensor condition.
- Week 4, Class 2: Then, students work on ultrasonic condition practice. They create a practice flowchart, match it to sample if/else code, test one response, and correct one problem.
- Week 5, Class 1: Then, students prepare for Summative Activity #4. They review the teacher-assigned ultrasonic task, required flowchart features, assigned project, English explanation, and equipment procedures.
- Week 5, Class 2: Then, students complete Summative Activity #4: the teacher-assigned mBot Ultrasonic Sensor Flowchart and Programming Task.

### August

- Week 1, Class 1: Then, students review the assigned Rescue Robot line-following logic.
- Week 1, Class 2: Then, students test and correct the line-following section of the assigned project.
- Week 2, Class 1: Then, students finish the assigned Rescue Robot logic, test the full route, correct one problem, and rehearse their presentation.
- Week 2, Class 2: Then, students complete Summative Activity #5 by presenting and demonstrating the teacher-assigned mBot Rescue Robot.
- Week 3, Class 1: Then, students complete a formative Rescue Robot reflection and peer-feedback activity.
- Week 3, Class 2: Then, students complete formative robotics review stations and prepare the numbered kits for the next unit.

## 3rd Trimester

### September

- Week 1, 45 minutes (6A Mon Sep 14; 6B Tue Sep 15; 6C Wed Sep 16): Data stories: facts become rows. Students complete five printed sentence-to-row examples, including the ID in every row. Evidence/closure: Compare one row with the source sentence.
- Week 1, 90 minutes (6A Tue Sep 15: Summative 1; 6B Wed Sep 16: Summative 1; 6C Fri Sep 18: Summative 1): Technology Vocabulary Table. Students complete Summative 1 with definitions, connected examples or visual descriptions, and technology sentences. Evidence/closure: Run the checklist, save with the required filename, and submit.
- Week 2, 45 minutes (6A Mon Sep 21; 6B Tue Sep 22; 6C Wed Sep 23): Sentence-to-row extraction practice. Students complete five new sentence-to-row items and correct one misplaced value. Evidence/closure: Explain how the sentence became one row.
- Week 2, 90 minutes (6A Tue Sep 22; 6B Wed Sep 23; 6C Fri Sep 25): Paragraph-to-spreadsheet guided practice. Students enter the records into the starter, check spelling and numeric values, then format the header and widths. Evidence/closure: Write one evidence sentence and compare it with the paragraph.
- Week 3, 45 minutes (6A Mon Sep 28; 6B Tue Sep 29; 6C Wed Sep 30): Narrative extraction rehearsal. Students extract four practice records, then use the source to correct errors without guessing. Evidence/closure: Complete the readiness checklist and filename rehearsal.
- Week 3, 90 minutes (6A Tue Sep 29: Summative 2; 6B Wed Sep 30: Summative 2; 6C Fri Oct 2: Summative 2): Narrative-to-Spreadsheet Data Table. Students complete Summative 2: extract all records, preserve exact data, format the table, and write one supported sentence. Evidence/closure: Run the checklist, save with the required filename, and submit.

### October

- Week 1, 45 minutes (6A Mon Oct 5; 6B Tue Oct 6; 6C Wed Oct 7): STEAM project organization. Students record project, role, first task, evidence location, and checkpoint. Evidence/closure: Save the first individual log entry for Summative 3 evidence.
- Week 1, 90 minutes (6A Tue Oct 6; 6B Wed Oct 7; 6C Fri Oct 9): STEAM project development. Students work on the assigned project; save the current state and record the individual action and result. Evidence/closure: Complete the second individual log entry and store materials.
- Week 2, 45 minutes (6A Mon Oct 12; 6B Tue Oct 13; 6C Wed Oct 14): STEAM process checkpoint preparation. Students complete missing log details and plan one clear test or progress check. Evidence/closure: Teacher checks readiness without scoring early.
- Week 2, 90 minutes (6A Tue Oct 13: Summative 3; 6B Wed Oct 14: Summative 3; 6C Fri Oct 16: Summative 3): STEAM Individual Work Process. Students complete three dated contributions, one test/improvement record, and the individual evidence handoff. Evidence/closure: Submit `G6_[Class]_[LastName]_S3_STEAM_Process`.
- Week 3, 45 minutes (6A Mon Oct 19; 6B Tue Oct 20; 6C Wed Oct 21): STEAM Expo rehearsal. Students rehearse the problem, goal, how it works, own contribution, result, and improvement. Evidence/closure: Record one correction before the October 22 expo.
- Week 3, 90 minutes (6A Tue Oct 20: final preparation; 6B Wed Oct 21: final preparation; all groups Thu Oct 22: Summative 4 at STEAM Expo; 6C Fri Oct 23: reflection closure): STEAM Expo Explanation and Closure. Students teacher observes individual roles on Oct 22; students explain evidence and complete the reflection/cleanup record by Oct 23. Evidence/closure: Submit `G6_[Class]_[LastName]_S4_STEAM_Expo` or the equal make-up.
- Week 4, 45 minutes (6A Mon Oct 26: Summative 5; 6B Tue Oct 27: at-risk preview only; 6C Wed Oct 28: Summative 5): Narrative Data Table and Column Chart. Students 6A and 6C complete Summative 5. 6B completes preview only because Oct 27 is at risk; 6B is assessed Oct 28. Evidence/closure: Submit the spreadsheet/response sheet or printed fallback.
- Week 4, 90 minutes (6A Tue Oct 27: at-risk support only; 6B Wed Oct 28: Summative 5 in first 45 min, then practice; 6C Fri Oct 30: correction and interpretation): Narrative chart assessment and interpretation. Students 6B completes Summative 5 in the first 45 minutes. 6A uses the block only if held, for support or make-up. 6C completes interpretation and correction practice. Evidence/closure: Save/export practice; collect formal evidence only from 6B or approved make-up.

### November

- Week 1, 45 minutes (6A Mon Nov 2: measure real object; 6B Tue Nov 3: no class; 6C Wed Nov 4: no class): Measure a real object with rulers and calipers. Students 6A records object name, tool, length, width, height, and units, then sketches three views. 6B and 6C have no class. Evidence/closure: Teacher initials 6A measurement record.
- Week 1, 90 minutes (6A Tue Nov 3: no class; 6B Wed Nov 4: no class; 6C Fri Nov 6: measure real object): Measure and plan the real-object model. Students 6C records three dimensions with units, sketches three views, selects basic shapes, and begins the model. 6A and 6B have no class. Evidence/closure: Teacher initials 6C record and checks the saved plan.
- Week 2, 45 minutes (6A Mon Nov 9: model plan; 6B Tue Nov 10: no class; 6C Wed Nov 11: refine model): Build from measurements. Students 6A completes the model plan. 6C refines shapes and prepares the test. 6B has no class. Evidence/closure: Save plan/model and record one likely test.
- Week 2, 90 minutes (6A Tue Nov 10: no class; 6B Wed Nov 11: first November class, measure and begin model; 6C Fri Nov 13: formative model checkpoint): Measurement and model development. Students 6B measures the assigned real object and begins the model. 6C completes the measurement record, plan, saved model, test, change, and reflection as practice. 6A has no class. Evidence/closure: Collect formative 6C evidence and the 6B checkpoint.
- Week 3, 45 minutes (6A Mon Nov 16: final model preparation; 6B Tue Nov 17: final model preparation; 6C Wed Nov 18: micro:bit counter rehearsal): Model readiness and counter introduction. Students 6A and 6B finish model preparation and test planning. 6C traces input, process, output, and five counter values. Evidence/closure: Save the checkpoint and complete the readiness check.
- Week 3, 90 minutes (6A Tue Nov 17: formative model demonstration; 6B Wed Nov 18: formative model demonstration; 6C Fri Nov 20: formative counter checkpoint): 3D model or micro:bit counter application. Students 6A and 6B demonstrate the measured model. 6C demonstrates the fixed button-A counter and five tests. Evidence/closure: Save the practice evidence and record feedback.
- Week 4, 45 minutes (6A Mon Nov 23: counter rehearsal; 6B Tue Nov 24: counter rehearsal; 6C Wed Nov 25: sensor-condition extension): Counter readiness and sensor extension. Students 6A and 6B build/rehearse the fixed counter. 6C begins formative light-condition practice. Evidence/closure: Save the file and record predictions/actual results.
- Week 4, 90 minutes (6A Tue Nov 24: formative counter checkpoint; 6B Wed Nov 25: formative counter checkpoint; 6C Fri Nov 27: formative night-light lab; Nov 28 Saturday closure has no scheduled class): micro:bit Button Counter and Test Record. Students 6A and 6B demonstrate the fixed counter and five tests. 6C builds and tests the fixed night-light response. Evidence/closure: Save formative lab evidence and record feedback.

Students 6A gives the live demonstration and completes reflection. 6C rehearses and improves one sentence. 6B has no class. Evidence/closure: Collect 6A reflection; save 6C notes.

Students 6B and 6C show below/above results, explain input-condition-output, and complete reflection. 6A has no class. Evidence/closure: Collect saved file/share link, reflection, and practice export.
