# Grade 6 T3 final assessment map

Exactly five 40-point Summatives: three regular Technology Summatives and two individual STEAM Summatives. The STEAM grades are Summatives 3 and 4, not Appreciation grades.

| # | Assessment | Official date(s) | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Technology Vocabulary Table | 6A Sep 15; 6B Sep 16; 6C Sep 18 | 10-word Technology vocabulary table |
| 2 | Narrative-to-Spreadsheet Data Table | 6A Sep 29; 6B Sep 30; 6C Oct 2 | Eight records extracted from a fixed printed narrative |
| 3 | STEAM Individual Work Process | 6A Oct 13; 6B Oct 14; 6C Oct 16 | Individual project role, three dated contributions, test/improvement, evidence handoff |
| 4 | STEAM Expo Explanation and Closure | 6A, 6B, 6C Oct 22; closure by Oct 23 | Assigned expo role, project explanation, own evidence, reflection/closure |
| 5 | Narrative Data Table and Column Chart | 6A Oct 26; 6B Oct 28; 6C Oct 28 | Four narrative records, column chart, exact labels, answers and numerical conclusion |

STEAM project subject does not change the Technology criteria. 3D modeling and micro:bit work in November are formative. Readiness is capped at 4/40 with equal no-penalty fallback for documented school-controlled failure.
