# Teacher-only scoring notes: Summative 3

Score only the student's individual Technology evidence connected to the assigned Arts, Mathematics, or Science project. Do not score the project topic, overall group quality, or another student's work.

Expected evidence: assigned project/problem/goal/role/first task; three dated exact contribution entries; one expected result, actual result, and next change with reason; and a file, part, calculation, explanation, shared-log location, or teacher observation that verifies the contribution.

The group product alone is not individual evidence. Use the shared make-up path for equivalent printed, written, saved, observed, or live evidence.
