# Grade 6 teacher expected responses and check notes

Teacher-only. Do not display during an independent graded task.

## Data module

- A complete record includes every cell in one row, including the ID. Do not accept `Science, 45` as a complete record when the ID is part of the row.
- Cleaning requires the source note: consistent `Math`, A03 value `45`, A04 value `30`, and only one exact A04 Music row in the module example.
- Formula example B2:B5: `=SUM(B2:B5)` gives 160 and `=AVERAGE(B2:B5)` gives 40 when values are 35, 50, 45, and 30.
- A column chart fits category comparison. It needs the assigned title, category axis, value axis, and complete data range.

## 3D modeling

- The teacher assigns one real classroom object. The student names the ruler or calipers and records length, width, height, and units before modeling.
- The plan shows top, front, and side views, three dimensions, and the basic shapes. The saved model/share link must open.
- A useful test names fit or stability, expected result, actual result, one change, and retest.

## microbit

- Button A is input; code changes the variable; LED display is output.
- Counter starts at 0. After five A presses, expected display is 5.
- Night light: if light is below the threshold, show the night icon; otherwise clear the display.
- Students test below and above the threshold, separate expected from actual, and change one likely cause before retesting.

## Formal checks

- Summative 1: use the provided 10-word template; check each of the 10 definitions, examples/visual descriptions, and technology sentences separately. See `Summative 1 Expected Output.md`.
- Summative 2: use only the provided printed eight-record narrative and starter; check the exact records, four header/data-placement checks, four readability checks, and three sentence checks. See `Summative 2 Expected Output.md`.
- Summative 3: score the student's individual role plan, three dated contributions, test/improvement record, and checkable evidence handoff from the one assigned Arts, Mathematics, or Science project. See `Summative 3 Expected Output.md`.
- Summative 4: score the assigned expo or make-up role; the five school explanation points (created, problem, how it works, learning, better-world connection); own contribution/test/improvement evidence; and individual reflection/closure. See `Summative 4 Expected Output.md`.
- Summative 5: use only the fixed four-record narrative and response/grid; check extraction, complete range, Column chart, exact labels, three answers, and the numerical conclusion. See `Summative 5 Expected Output.md`.

The STEAM project topic and responsible subject do not change the Technology criteria. Never infer an individual's grade from the group product alone.

For every summative, a forgotten or uncharged personal device, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated responsibility issue may reduce only the 4-point readiness criterion. A teacher-confirmed school network, platform, teacher-provided account, connection, hardware, or assigned-material failure outside the student's control activates the announced printed, live, or written fallback and does not reduce those points.
