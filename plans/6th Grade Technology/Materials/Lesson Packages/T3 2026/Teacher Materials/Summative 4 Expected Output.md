# Teacher-only scoring notes: Summative 4

Score only the student's assigned expo or approved make-up role and individual explanation/reflection. Do not score the project topic or infer participation from the group display.

Expected school-expo explanation: what was created, problem, how it works, what was learned, and how it can help make the world better. Individual Technology evidence also includes own contribution, one test or progress result, and one improvement. Expected closure: completed reflection plus cleanup or materials-return responsibility.

An absent student completes the same explanation live or in writing and the same reflection. A documented school-controlled failure uses the equal fallback without deduction.
