## Rubric alignment

Each active DOCX and Classroom workbook has five criteria and a 40-point maximum: four 9-point product/evidence criteria plus one 4-point process or evidence-handoff criterion. Every band uses complete non-overlapping counts. The same teacher-only JSON specification generated both formats.

Each summative reserves exactly 4 of 40 points for punctuality, responsibility, and readiness. A forgotten or uncharged personal device, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated responsibility issue may reduce only those 4 points. Academic criteria score the work itself. A documented school network, platform, teacher-provided account, connection, hardware, or assigned-material failure outside the student's control does not reduce readiness points when the student follows the approved printed, live, or written fallback.
