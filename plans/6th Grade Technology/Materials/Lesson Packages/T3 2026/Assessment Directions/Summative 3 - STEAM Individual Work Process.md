- 6A: Tuesday, October 13, 2026

## Exact product

Complete one individual Technology evidence packet connected to the STEAM project already assigned to your group. 6A continues the Arts project, 6B the Mathematics project, and 6C the Science project. Every student earns this Technology grade even when another subject owns the project. Do not create a separate Technology project.

- `../../../../../Shared Materials/T3 2026 STEAM Kit/Assigned Project Brief and Group Plan.md`
- `../../../../../Shared Materials/T3 2026 STEAM Kit/Student Individual Work Log.md`

## Steps

1. Record the assigned project, problem, goal, your role, and first task.
2. Complete three dated individual log entries. Each entry names an exact action, evidence location, result, and next step.
3. Record one real test or progress check: expected result, actual result, evidence location, and the next change with a reason.
4. Show the teacher the current file, calculation, model, physical part, explanation, or shared-log location that verifies your contribution.
5. Submit the individual packet. The group product alone is not individual evidence.

## Submission checklist

- [ ] Assigned project, problem, goal, role, and first task are complete.
- [ ] Three dated individual entries are complete and match the group work.
- [ ] One expected result, actual result, and next change with reason are recorded.
- [ ] My contribution has a checkable evidence location.
- [ ] I did not create a second project or submit phone/photo/screenshot evidence.

## Make-up and fallback

Use `../../../../../Shared Materials/T3 2026 STEAM Kit/STEAM Make-Up Path.md`. Continue the same assigned project and provide the same individual evidence in print, writing, saved work, teacher observation, or a live check.

## Readiness and responsibility: 4 of 40 points

The four announced checks are: bring your own or assigned charged computer and school login when needed; begin on time; use files, tools, devices, and materials responsibly; and submit or return evidence by the deadline. A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated responsibility reminders may lower only these 4 readiness points; the academic criteria score the work itself. A documented school system, teacher-provided account, network, or hardware failure does not reduce these points when the equal fallback is completed.
