# Summative 4: STEAM Expo Explanation and Closure

## Official Grade 6 date

- 6A, 6B, and 6C: Thursday, October 22, 2026, during the school STEAM Expo
- Reflection/evidence closure: by Friday, October 23, 2026

## Exact product

Complete your assigned expo role and one individual Technology explanation/reflection packet for the same Arts, Mathematics, or Science project already assigned to your group. The project topic is not graded by Technology. Do not create a separate Technology project.

## Provided files

- `../../../../../Shared Materials/T3 2026 STEAM Kit/Expo Explanation and Reflection.md`
- `../../../../../Shared Materials/T3 2026 STEAM Kit/Student Individual Work Log.md`
- `../../../../../Shared Materials/T3 2026 STEAM Kit/Teacher Individual Observation Sheet.md`

## Steps

1. Complete the assigned speaking, demonstration, question-answering, or materials role.
2. Explain what was created, the problem, how it works, what you learned, and how it can help make the world better.
3. Explain your own contribution, one test or progress result, and one improvement.
4. Complete the five-part individual reflection and cleanup/materials-return responsibility.
5. Submit the packet after the teacher observes the role or the approved make-up explanation.

## Make-up and fallback

If absent from the expo, give the same explanation live to the teacher or in writing, then complete the same reflection. Use `../../../../../Shared Materials/T3 2026 STEAM Kit/STEAM Make-Up Path.md`. A device failure never requires a second project.

## Readiness and responsibility: 4 of 40 points

The four announced checks are: bring your own or assigned charged computer and school login when needed; begin the assigned role on time; use files, tools, devices, and materials responsibly; and submit or return evidence by the deadline. A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated responsibility reminders may lower only these 4 readiness points; the academic criteria score the work itself. A documented school system, teacher-provided account, network, or hardware failure does not reduce these points when the equal fallback is completed.
