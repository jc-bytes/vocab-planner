# Summative 4: Real-Object Measurement and 3D Model

**Official dates:** 6A Tuesday November 17; 6B Wednesday November 18; 6C Friday November 13  
**Product:** one measurement record, measured-object design plan, saved 3D model, test/change record, and reflection  
**Time:** 90-minute formal completion block after the group-specific measurement classes  
**Filename:** `G6_S4_Measured_Model_Lastname_Firstname`

## Provided materials and allowed resources

- Teacher-assigned real classroom object, ruler, and caliper
- [Measurement and model record](<../Assessment Materials/Summative 4 - Measurement and Model Record.md>)
- Prior module practice: `3D-VIEWS-LEARN-01`, `3D-VIEWS-PRACTICE-01`, `TRANSFORM-LEARN-01`, `TRANSFORM-PRACTICE-01`, `MODEL-PLAN-01`, and `TINKERCAD-HANDOFF-01`
- Tinkercad or the printed three-view fallback. No web search, phone, photo, or screenshot evidence.

## Exact task

Use the object assigned by the teacher. Record its name, tool used, length, width, and height with units. Draw front, side, and top views; label at least three dimensions and the basic shapes you will use. Build a recognisable 3D model based on those measurements. Run one size, fit, proportion, or stability test; record expected and actual results; make one supported change; and write one reflection sentence.

The task grades the relationship between the assigned real object, measurement record, plan, and model. It does not require 3D printing.

## Steps and checklist

1. Use the teacher-approved measurement record from the first available November class.
2. Check the three dimensions, units, views, and selected shapes.
3. Build the model and inspect it from front, side, and top.
4. Run one test, make one supported change, and record the result.
5. Save with the exact filename and submit the model plus record.

- [ ] Object, tool, three dimensions, and units are recorded.
- [ ] Three-view plan and shapes use the measurements.
- [ ] Saved model is recognisable and based on the plan.
- [ ] Test, actual result, one change, and reflection are complete.

## Submission and fallback

Submit the saved Tinkercad model/share link and completed record. If Tinkercad or hardware is unavailable, complete the same measured three-view design at scale on paper, add the test/change/reflection, and explain it live. The teacher scores the equal evidence without deducting for the documented failure.

## Readiness and responsibility: 4 of 40 points

The four announced checks are: bring your own or assigned charged computer and school login when the task needs them; begin on time; use files, measuring tools, devices, and materials responsibly; and submit or return the required evidence by the deadline. A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated responsibility reminders may lower only these 4 readiness points; the academic criteria score the work itself. A documented school system, teacher-provided account, network, or hardware failure does not reduce these points when you complete the equal printed, live, or saved-file fallback.
