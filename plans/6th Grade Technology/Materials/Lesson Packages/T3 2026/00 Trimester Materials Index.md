# Grade 6 Technology, Trimester 3 materials index

**Dates:** September 14 to December 11, 2026  
**Groups:** 6A, 6B, 6C  
**Assessment count:** Exactly five 40-point Summatives: three regular Technology Summatives and two individual STEAM Summatives. The STEAM grades are Summatives 3 and 4, not Appreciation grades.

Students continue the one Arts, Mathematics, or Science project assigned to the group during STEAM. Every student earns both Technology STEAM grades from individual Technology evidence; no student starts a second project. Phones, photos, and screenshots are not evidence.

Readiness is 4 of 40 points. Personal device/login readiness, lateness, and repeated reminders can lower only those 4 points. Academic criteria score the work. A documented school-controlled failure uses the equal fallback without deduction.
