## Class Objective

Extract eight records from a printed paragraph and enter them into a spreadsheet.

## Pre-Activities

- Number the eight sentences and identify the three fixed headers.

## While Activities

- Enter the records into the starter, check spelling and numeric values, then format the header and widths.

## Post-Activities

- Write one evidence sentence and compare it with the paragraph.
