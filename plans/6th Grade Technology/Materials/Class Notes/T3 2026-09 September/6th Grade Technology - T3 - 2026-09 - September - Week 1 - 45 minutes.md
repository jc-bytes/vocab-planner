## Class Objective

Identify a person or item, category, and value in a printed sentence.

## Pre-Activities

- Read one sentence together and mark the ID, category, and value.

## While Activities

- Complete five printed sentence-to-row examples, including the ID in every row.

## Post-Activities

- Compare one row with the source sentence.
