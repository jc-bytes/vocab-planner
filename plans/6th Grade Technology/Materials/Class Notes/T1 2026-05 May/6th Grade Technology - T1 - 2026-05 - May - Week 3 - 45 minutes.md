## Class Objective

Plan an awareness product with a clear audience, message, title, key words, and action step.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What message should people remember? Choose your topic: Internet Day or recycling/e-waste. Plan your audience, message, and three key words from May Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 1). audience: people who see or hear work message: information sent to someone title: name of a page or project fact: true information tip: helpful advice

## While Activities

- Sketch your awareness product layout. Choose one title and one action step. Check that your message is clear for sixth graders.

## Post-Activities

- Share your plan with a partner. Improve one part of the plan after feedback.
