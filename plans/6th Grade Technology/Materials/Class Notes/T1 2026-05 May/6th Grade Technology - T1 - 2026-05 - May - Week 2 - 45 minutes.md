## Class Objective

Sort technology items into reuse, repair, recycle, or e-waste and explain why some items should not go in regular trash.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: What happens to old devices? Review May Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 1). recycle: turn waste into usable material reuse: use again repair: fix so it works again e-waste: old electronic waste device: technology tool Sort items into reuse, repair, recycle, or trash.

## While Activities

- Identify examples of technology waste at home or school. Practice sorting sample technology items and discuss why some items should not go in regular trash. Review the sorting check expectations before the next class.

## Post-Activities

- Record one responsible technology habit. Share one item that should not go in regular trash.
