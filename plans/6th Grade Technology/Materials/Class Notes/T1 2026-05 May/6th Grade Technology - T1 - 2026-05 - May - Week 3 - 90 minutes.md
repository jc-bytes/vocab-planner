## Class Objective

Create and present a short awareness product with a title, three facts or tips, one image or icon, and one action step.

## Pre-Activities

- Review May Week 3 practice vocabulary with a quick check (part 2). action: something someone does poster: visual message slide: presentation page infographic: visual facts or tips present: show and explain Open your plan and prepare your materials.

## While Activities

- Create a poster, slide, infographic, bookmark, or short campaign message. Include a title, three facts or tips, one image or icon, and one action step. Check your work with the product checklist. Practice a one-minute presentation.

## Post-Activities

- Present your product in one minute. Submit your final product and reflection.
