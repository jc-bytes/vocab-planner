## Class Objective

Complete an e-waste sorting check and collect three facts or tips about reducing technology waste.

## Pre-Activities

- Complete a word soup or matching activity using May Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 2). battery: part that stores power trash: waste thrown away sorting: putting into groups waste: things thrown away environment: the natural world around us Review one example of responsible technology use.

## While Activities

- Complete Summative Activity #5: Sort 10 technology items into reuse, repair, recycle, or e-waste, then explain the best responsible choice for 2 of the items. Submit the completed sorting check in class or on Google Classroom. Find three facts or tips about reducing technology waste. Organize your facts into a simple class list. Choose one fact that would help students at school. Explain why that fact matters.

## Post-Activities

- Write one habit you can change to reduce waste. Submit your three facts or tips.
