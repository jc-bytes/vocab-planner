## Class Objective

Add and test a web page link, evaluate the page with a checklist, and improve it after peer feedback.

## Pre-Activities

- Review April Week 3 practice vocabulary with Blooket, Gimkit, or matching cards (part 2). button: thing you click or press page: one screen or sheet of information test: try and check feedback: helpful comments Complete one example of a good link label.

## While Activities

- Add one internal or external link to your web page. Test the link to make sure it works. Evaluate your page with a short checklist. Give a partner one positive comment and one suggestion.

## Post-Activities

- Improve one part of your website after feedback. Submit or show the updated page.
