## Class Objective

Select a copyright-friendly image, record its source, make a safe-use decision, and write a credit line.

## Pre-Activities

- Review April Week 1 practice vocabulary with a word soup or Blooket (part 2). safe: not likely to cause harm permission: saying it is okay image: picture Use the simple definitions from the practice vocabulary list. Complete one sentence using fair use correctly.

## While Activities

- Find two or three copyright-friendly images for your web page topic. Complete Summative Activity #3: Select one image for the assigned web page topic, record its source, decide if it is safe to use, explain why it fits the topic, and write the credit line. Submit the completed image-selection form in class or on Google Classroom.

## Post-Activities

- Share one reason your selected image fits the topic. Write one reminder about giving credit.
