## Class Objective

Explain why navigation matters, map how pages connect, and plan one useful link for a web page.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: How do people move around a website? Review April Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 1). link: clickable connection menu: list of choices home page: main page of a website navigation: moving around a site Draw a simple map showing how two pages connect.

## While Activities

- Identify links and menus on a sample website. Decide where one link should go on your page. Record why the link is useful for your audience.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining why clear navigation matters. Circle the link or menu in your web page plan.
