## Class Objective

Program a repeated movement pattern, such as a square or zigzag.

## Pre-Activities

- Complete a vocabulary review activity using July Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 2). square: shape with four equal sides zigzag: path that turns left and right shorter: using less space or time Review the mBot card or block connected to repeated actions.

## While Activities

- Program a repeated movement pattern, such as a square or zigzag. Test the pattern and adjust one value. Add an LED or buzzer signal at the end if time allows. Compare the long version and loop version of the program.

## Post-Activities

- Show the repeated movement pattern. Explain how the loop helped.
