## Class Objective

Run a basic mBot movement program, change one time or speed value, and record the result.

## Pre-Activities

- Complete a quick review puzzle using June Week 2 practice vocabulary (part 2). stop: end movement speed: how fast something moves time: how long something lasts
- Review the setup card or movement card from mBot Knowledge Cards Part 1.

## While Activities

- Prepare your mBot for programming. Run the basic movement program from the knowledge cards.
- Change one value, such as time or speed, and observe what changes.
- Test forward, stop, turn, and return movements. Write the results in the worksheet.

## Post-Activities

- Show one successful movement. Write one thing you changed to improve movement.
