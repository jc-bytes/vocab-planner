## Class Objective

Choose an LED or buzzer signal for a robot state and explain why the output makes sense.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: How can a robot communicate without speaking?
- Review June Week 4 practice vocabulary (part 1). output: what a device shows or does signal: sign that gives information LED: small light buzzer: part that makes sound
- Match robot actions with possible signals, such as a green light for moving, a red light or buzzer for an obstacle, and a flashing light or sound when the task is complete.

## While Activities

- Choose one robot state, such as start, turn, stop, or finish. Choose an LED or buzzer signal for that state. Explain why your signal makes sense.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining what an output does. Share your signal idea with a partner.
