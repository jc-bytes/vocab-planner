## Class Objective

Sort online tools by purpose and explain one benefit and one challenge of online collaboration.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: How can people work together when they are not in the same room? Review March Week 3 practice vocabulary (part 1). communicate: share information collaborate: work together share: let others use or see shared file: file more than one person can use Choose the best word for three short situations.

## While Activities

- Sort online tools by purpose: communicate, share, create, or present. Discuss which tool would help a group finish a project. Record one benefit and one challenge of online collaboration.

## Post-Activities

- Write one responsible rule for working in a shared file. Share your rule with a partner.
