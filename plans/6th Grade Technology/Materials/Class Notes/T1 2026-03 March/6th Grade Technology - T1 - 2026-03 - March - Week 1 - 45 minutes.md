## Class Objective

Identify a domain name and explain, with a simple example, how DNS helps a browser find a website.

## Pre-Activities

- Answer the warm-up question: How do we find a website? Copy one example of a domain name in your notebook.

## While Activities

- Compare a home address with a website address. Identify the part of a website address that is the domain name. Follow the example to connect a domain name with an IP address.

## Post-Activities

- Write one sentence explaining what DNS does. Share one website address you already know.
