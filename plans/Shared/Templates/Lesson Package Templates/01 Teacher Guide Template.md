## Plain-Language Check

- [ ] The student-facing title is short and literal, usually two to six words.
- [ ] Student directions use familiar words, direct verbs, and short sentences.
- [ ] Each numbered step contains one main action.
- [ ] Necessary technical terms are explained before students use them.
- [ ] A student can identify the first action, product, required parts, submission, and finish condition after one reading.
- [ ] Teacher directions are concise, operational, and free of unexplained abbreviations.
