**Student:** ____________________  **Grade/group:** ______  **Project:** ____________________

## Prepare your assigned expo role

Write short speaking notes. Do not read a long paragraph.

1. **Problem or need:** Our project addresses ____________________________________.
2. **Goal:** The project should _________________________________________________ .
3. **My contribution:** I completed or helped with _________________________________ .
4. **How it works:** When __________________________________, the project __________ .
5. **One test and result:** We tested ______________________ and found ______________ .
6. **One improvement:** We changed or would change __________________ because ______ .
7. **What I learned:** I learned _________________________________________________ .
8. **How it can help:** This can help make the world better by _____________________ .

**My expo role:** introduce / explain / demonstrate / answer questions / manage materials

## Readiness check

- [ ] I can state the problem and goal clearly.
- [ ] I can explain my own contribution.
- [ ] I can describe one real test or progress result.
- [ ] I know when to speak or demonstrate.
- [ ] The group knows the order of speakers.
- [ ] Files open and materials are labeled.
- [ ] We have a non-digital explanation ready if a device fails.
- [ ] I know my cleanup task.
