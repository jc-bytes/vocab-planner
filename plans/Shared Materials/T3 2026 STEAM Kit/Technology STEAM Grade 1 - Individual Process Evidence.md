# Technology STEAM Grade 1: Individual Process Evidence

This is one 40-point Technology grade for every student, regardless of whether the assigned project belongs to Mathematics, Arts, Science, or Technology. Grade 6 records it as Summative 3. Grades 7–9 record it as Daily Grade 4. It is not an Appreciation grade. Do not start a second project.

Submit or show:

1. the assigned project, problem, goal, your role, and first task;
2. three dated individual work-log entries that name exact contributions;
3. one test or progress check with expected result, actual result, and next change with reason;
4. the current file, calculation, model, physical part, explanation, or shared-log location that lets the teacher verify your contribution.

The teacher may use the individual observation sheet. A group product alone is not enough to prove an individual's work.

If absent or access fails, use the matching section of `STEAM Make-Up Path.md`. No phone, photo, or screenshot evidence is required.

Readiness is 4 of 40 points. A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated responsibility reminders may lower only those 4 points; academic criteria score the evidence itself. A documented school system, account, network, or hardware failure uses the equal fallback without deduction.
