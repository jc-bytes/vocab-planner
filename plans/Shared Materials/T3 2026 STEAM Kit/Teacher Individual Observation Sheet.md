# Teacher Individual STEAM Observation Sheet

Use one row per student. Record observable evidence for both Technology STEAM grades. Grade 6 records Individual Work Process as Summative 3 and Expo Explanation and Closure as Summative 4. Grades 7–9 record Expo Explanation and Closure as Daily Grade 3 and Individual Work Process as Daily Grade 4, never Appreciation grades. Never infer individual participation from the group product alone.

Use the record with the student's own log, test record, explanation, and reflection. The project topic or responsible subject does not change the Technology criteria.
