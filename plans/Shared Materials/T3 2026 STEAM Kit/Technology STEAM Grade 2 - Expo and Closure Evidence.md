# Technology STEAM Grade 2: Expo and Closure Evidence

This is one 40-point Technology grade for every student, regardless of the assigned project's subject. Grade 6 records it as Summative 4. Grades 7–9 record it as Daily Grade 3. It is not an Appreciation grade or the Exam. The school expo is Thursday, October 22, 2026. Complete the reflection and evidence closure by October 23.

Submit or show:

1. complete your assigned speaking, demonstration, question-answering, or materials role;
2. explain what was created, the problem, how it works, what you learned, and how it can help make the world better;
3. explain your own contribution, one test or progress result, and one improvement;
4. complete the individual reflection and cleanup/materials-return record.

If you miss the expo, give the same explanation live to the teacher or in writing and complete the reflection. Do not build a second project. No phone, photo, or screenshot evidence is required.

Readiness is 4 of 40 points. A forgotten or uncharged personal computer, unprepared login, lateness, or repeated responsibility reminders may lower only those 4 points; academic criteria score the evidence itself. A documented school system, account, network, or hardware failure uses the equal fallback without deduction.
