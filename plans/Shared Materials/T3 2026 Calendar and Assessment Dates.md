# T3 2026 calendar and assessment dates

The third trimester runs from September 14 through December 11, 2026. The attached AID activity calendar is a tentative pattern guide: activity dates may be replaced, but its review, project, exam, ceremony, reduced-day, and closure patterns are used to protect assessment timing. Current 2026 school notices, the 2026 STEAM email, and the actual class timetable take priority when they conflict with that guide.

Grade 9 is planned to finish one to two weeks before Grades 6–8. Its Technology exam is completed during the November 17–21 Grade 9 review-and-project window: 9A on November 17 and 9B on November 18. No required Grade 9 evidence is scheduled after November 21. November 23 onward is contingency only because the activity calendar places Grade 9 academic exams and affective closure in the following week.

## Tentative AID activity-pattern safeguards

- Student Day may replace regular teaching with reduced periods and afternoon activities; do not place a new formal assessment on the replacement date without confirmation.
- November patriotic events commonly remove November 3, 4, 5, 10, and 28 or their announced replacement dates.
- Grade 9 commonly has a final civic act, review/project week, academic-exam week, affective closure, and graduation before the rest of secondary finishes.
- Teacher Day commonly creates a half day or replacement activity around December 1.
- These are planning safeguards, not confirmed 2026 event dates. Confirm the school notice before posting a Classroom due date.

## Date-label format

`Grade/group - weekday, month day, year - duration`

Example: `6A - Monday, September 14, 2026 - 45 min`.
