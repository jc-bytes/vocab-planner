Threshold practice, five activities

Rule: if distance > 18 cm, send ALERT.

  1. Predict 12 cm.
  2. Predict 18 cm.
  3. Predict 25 cm.
  4. Write values below, at, and above the boundary.
  5. The alert happens too late. Describe one controlled change and retest.
