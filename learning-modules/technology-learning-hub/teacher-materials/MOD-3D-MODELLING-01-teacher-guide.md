# MOD-3D-MODELLING-01 Teacher Guide

Version 0.1.0 · Pilot · Grade 6 · Formative only

## Intended use

Use the module before and during the fixed three-shape desk-organizer task. Teach the Learn page with students following on their computers, assign as many of the five-item practice sets as time allows, and then send students to Tinkercad for the actual build.

## Suggested sequence

1. `3D-LEARN-01` and `3D-VIEWS-PRACTICE-01`: width, height, depth, and useful views.
2. `TRANSFORM-LEARN-01` and `TRANSFORM-PRACTICE-01`: move, resize, rotate, and duplicate.
3. `COMBINE-LEARN-01` and `COMBINE-PRACTICE-01`: connect and group shapes.
4. `MODEL-PLAN-01`: record measurements and a test before building.
5. `TINKERCAD-HANDOFF-01`: build in Tinkercad and record changes and test results.
6. `HISTORY-01`: export the local practice report.

The required product is one base, one pencil holder, and one tray. Holes, precise alignment tools, and extra decoration are extensions only after the required model works.

## Evidence and fallback

The module saves reconstructable practice attempts and student records on the current browser. Students should export before changing computers. If the site or Tinkercad is unavailable, use `printable-fallback.html`; students can still plan the model and explain tool choices.

## Classroom constraints

Students use school computers or their own computers only when allowed. Phones and phone-camera evidence are not part of the lesson. The teacher verifies the Tinkercad file directly.
