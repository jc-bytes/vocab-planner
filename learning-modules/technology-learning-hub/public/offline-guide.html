Scientific report writing guide

Use this guide when the learning module or Google Docs is unavailable. Draft on paper or in a local document, then transfer the group's work to the shared Google Doc when access returns.

Keep the report connected. The question guides the method. The method produces results. The results support the analysis and conclusion.

Plan the investigation

Research question

□ Names what changes or is compared, what is measured, what is studied, and a useful limit.

Problem statement

□ Explains the concrete problem, evidence that it exists, its consequences, and the missing knowledge.

Objectives

□ Includes one general objective and two or three specific objectives beginning with To plus an action verb.

Hypothesis and variables

□ Predicts a measurable relationship and names independent, dependent, and controlled variables when applicable.

Build the investigation

Theoretical framework

□ Connects reliable sources, key concepts, earlier findings, and the group's scientific decisions. Every borrowed idea has a citation.

Methodology

□ Identifies the sample or participants, materials and quantities, ordered procedure, measurements, repetitions, controls, data recording, analysis, timeline, safety, consent, and ethics.

Explain the findings

Results

□ Reports complete evidence with units, labels, table or figure titles, and no explanation of causes.

Analysis and discussion

□ Explains patterns, compares them with the hypothesis and sources, and avoids claims the method cannot support.

Conclusions

□ Answers the research question directly and uses only results already presented.

Limitations and recommendations

□ Names a real limitation, its possible effect, and a specific improvement or next test.

Finish the report

□ Title is concise and specific.

□ Abstract is no more than 250 words and covers problem, objective, method, main results, and conclusion.

□ Three to five keywords follow the abstract.

□ Table of contents matches headings and page numbers.

□ Every citation has a matching APA-style reference, and every listed reference is cited.

□ Appendices are numbered, titled, mentioned in the report, and contain no private participant information.

Required format

Letter-size pages • Arial 12 • 1.5 line spacing • numbered pages • upper margin 3 cm • lower margin 2 cm • left margin 3.5 cm • right margin 2 cm • clear impersonal scientific writing
